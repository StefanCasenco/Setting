# NEW SETTING SCRIPT: leads who grabbed a resource

Built on Andres' setting template (Intent, Logical certainty, Transition), with the booking and lock-in sections from your framework kept because they already work. The intro is rewritten for people who downloaded something and may not remember you, the company, or why anyone is calling.

Placeholders: `$NAME`, `$COMPANY`, `$RESOURCE` (what they grabbed, e.g. "the trading guide"), `$CLOSER`.

Nothing here is a script to read. Pick one line per slot, say it your way, keep the outcome.

---

## 0. Why "just to see if I can help you get more out of it" blows up on cold leads

Andres' opener works for his audience because they bought or downloaded something and already know who he is. The sentence has three problems for yours:

1. **"It" has no owner.** After "you grabbed the PDF, is that right?" the PDF is the subject. Two seconds later "get more out of it" could mean the PDF, the program they never heard of, or trading. They guess. You hear the guess as a weird answer, and now you're confused too.
2. **"Is that right?" is a dead end.** Yes or no, and the yes tells them nothing about why you're calling. They're still waiting for the catch.
3. **The question is too big, too early.** "What was the main goal of getting into trading?" asked cold, with no reason to answer, gets "idk, make money."

### The fix: five rules for the intro

1. **One subject for the whole intro: them and trading.** The resource gets mentioned once, as the reason you called, then you drop it.
2. **Never say "it."** Say "trading." Say "the guide" once. No "our program," "the coaching," or "what we do" until they know what that is.
3. **Make the confirm tiny, or skip it.** `$NAME?` already confirms the person. Confirming the download is only worth it if it leads somewhere.
4. **Give them a reason to talk before you ask them to talk.** The reason is honest: you want to know why they were looking, so you can tell them whether you can help.
5. **Make the bridge a door, not a pit.** A question with two obvious answers is easier to say yes to than an open "what's your goal."

## 1. INTRO (seven versions)

Every version ends on the same destination: **what was the real reason you got interested in trading?** The difference is how you get a cold person to the point where that feels like a normal question.

### Version 1: "The PDF isn't the point"
Direct, a bit disarming. Good default.

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY.
> You grabbed $RESOURCE a little while ago, that was you, right?

**YES →**
> Cool. Honestly I'm not calling about the guide itself. When somebody goes looking for something on trading there's usually a reason behind it, and that's the part I'm interested in.
> So tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

*Why it works:* you tell them straight what the call is not about, and the "it" problem disappears because the next thing you name is trading.

---

### Version 2: "Two doors"
Best when you don't know if they're a beginner or already trading. The answer also decides which goal question you use (section 2).

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY.
> You grabbed $RESOURCE earlier, so I wanted to reach out personally. Quick one before anything else. People who grab that are usually one of two types: either they're curious about trading and just starting to look, or they've been at it a while and something's not clicking. Which one sounds more like you?

**"Just starting / curious" →**
> Okay cool. So what got you curious about trading in the first place?

**"Been at it a while" →**
> Got it. So what are you actually trying to get out of trading, what's the goal?

*Why it works:* easy to answer, nobody feels interrogated, and you get the new-versus-existing split for free.

---

### Version 3: "Did it help?"
Softest. Good for guarded or quiet people.

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY.
> You grabbed $RESOURCE a bit ago. Did you get a chance to look through it?

**YES →**
> Nice. What stood out to you?
> *(one answer, then move on)* Okay. And what was it about trading that had you go looking in the first place?

**NOT YET →**
> All good, most people don't get to it straight away. What made you grab it though?
> *(if they answer with something surface like "wanted to learn trading")* Okay, and what had you interested in trading in the first place?

*Why it works:* they hear their own reason in the first answer. *Watch out:* "what stood out" can pull them into reviewing the PDF for three minutes. One answer, then bridge.

---

### Version 4: "Who we are in one sentence"
Use when they sound suspicious or say "who is this?"

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY. So you know who's calling: we help people who are trying to get serious about trading, and when someone grabs $RESOURCE, one of us gives them a quick call. Not to push anything, just to understand where you're at and see if there's a way we can help. Cool if I ask a couple of questions?

**YES →**
> Nice. So tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

*Why it works:* names the company's job in plain words, states the call's purpose, and asks permission. Cold people relax when they know what the call is.

---

### Version 5: "Still on your mind?"
Gives them an honest way out up front. The ones who stay are real.

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY.
> You grabbed $RESOURCE a little while ago so I'm following up. I'll keep it quick. Is trading still something you're thinking about, or was that more of a passing curiosity?

**Still thinking about it →**
> Okay good. What's behind that, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Passing / not really →**
> No worries man, appreciate you being honest. Out of curiosity, what made you click on it then?
> *(if there's nothing real there)* All good, I won't take your time. Take care.

---

### Version 6: "Two minutes"
For people who answer sounding busy or irritated.

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY. I know, random call. You grabbed $RESOURCE earlier. Do you have two minutes right now or should I catch you later?

**NOW →**
> Cool, I'll keep it simple. I just want to hear what's behind you looking into trading. If I can help, I'll tell you. If not, I'll point you somewhere useful.
> So what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**LATER →**
> No stress. What's better for you, later today or tomorrow?
> *(lock a time, confirm the number, done)*

---

### Version 7: "Straight shot"
Shortest. Use only when they're warm, friendly, and already talking.

> Heeey, $NAME? Ștefan from $COMPANY. You grabbed $RESOURCE, so I wanted to reach out personally instead of just sending an email.
> Tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

*Watch out:* no confirm and no frame. If they hesitate, switch to a curveball line below.

---

### Mix-and-match bridge lines
Any of these can replace the middle of a version. They all move the subject from the PDF to the person.

- "I'm more curious about you than the guide."
- "There's usually a reason behind a download, and that's the part I care about."
- "Some people grab it out of curiosity, some are fed up with something. Which is closer to you?"
- "What's the story with trading for you?"
- "What made you go 'yeah, I should look into trading'?"
- "If I'm going to be any use to you, I need to know what you're actually after."

### Words to avoid in the intro
"Get more out of it" · "our program" · "the coaching" · "what we do" · "did you like it?" · "Is that right?" as the last thing you say before a long silence.

---

### Curveballs (cold leads will throw these in the first 20 seconds)

**"Who is this?" / "How did you get my number?"**
> Fair. You filled in your details to get $RESOURCE, and I'm the person at $COMPANY who follows up. I just wanted to ask you a couple of quick questions about it, about trading, and see if I can help. Cool?

**"I don't remember downloading anything."**
> No stress, a lot of people grab things and forget. Was trading something you were looking into, or more of a random click?
> *(if random)* Fair enough. Is trading something you'd ever want to get into, or not really?

**"I only wanted the PDF." / "Not interested."**
> All good, no pressure. Out of curiosity, what were you hoping to get from the guide?
> *(one try. If still no, say "No worries man, take care" and end the call.)*

**"Is this a sales call?"**
> I called because you grabbed $RESOURCE. If there's something that fits, I'll tell you straight. But first I need to understand what you're after. What had you interested in trading?
> *(Never say it's free and never deny there's a cost.)*

**"Bad time."**
> Totally. When's better, later today or tomorrow?

---

### Optional frame (after the intro, before the intent question)
Your original two-step line. Use after Version 1, 2 or 3 if the person seems to want to know where this is going.

> Just so you know how this works, our process is two steps. This call is the first: I just want to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and see if we can actually help you. If by the end I feel like that's the case, I'll walk you through the next step. Cool?

## 2. INTENT: the overarching goal question

Pick by who they are. If you used Version 2, you already know.

**If they're new to trading (a new thing):**
- "What had you interested in trading in the first place?"
- "What was the thing that made you think 'maybe I should look into trading'?"
- "What were you hoping trading could do for you?"

**If they already trade (improving something existing):**
- "What do you want to be different about your trading?"
- "What's the main thing you're trying to get out of trading?"
- "What's one or two things you feel could be better, and what would that get you?"

**If you don't know:**
- "What were you hoping trading would get you?"

### Reading the answer
You want a **tangible goal**: money, time, freedom, out of the job, a specific thing.

**Tangible →** repeat it back lightly and move on.

**Vague ("make more money") →**
> Sure, everyone wants more money. At the end of the day money's just a tool, nobody wants to stare at digits on a screen. Everyone's got a different reason behind it. What's the real reason for you? Is it more about getting out of something, or getting to something?

**Tangible, but you want one more layer →**
> Well, what makes you feel like you don't have $TANGIBLE right now?
> What keeps you busy, what keeps your pockets empty?

*(Optional, from your original script)* If the job is part of the story:
> What do you do work-wise? How do you feel about it? What makes you want out?

**Confirm:**
> Okay so basically your main goal is to $REAL_GOAL, correct?

**Done when:** you have their real goal in their words, not "make more money."

## 3. LOGICAL CERTAINTY

Outcome: understand what they're doing now, plant a little doubt in it, find the catalyst, make the pain real.

### Strategy: what are they doing about it?
- "What are you doing right now to get to $GOAL?"
- "What's the current strategy with trading? Are you trading, learning, still on the sidelines?"
- "What have you actually tried so far?"

### Duration: how long?
- "How long have you been doing it that way?"
- "Is that recent or have you been at it a while?"

### Justification: why that route?
- "What made you go that route?"
- "Where'd that come from, YouTube, a friend, a course?"

### Catalyst: why now? *(the most important question)*
Front-load with validation, then ask what changed. They can't answer without naming the pain.

- "I mean you've put real time into that. What changed recently, in the past few weeks or months, that had you looking for more help rather than just carrying on?"
- "It sounds like it's going okay. Out of curiosity, what shifted that made you open to looking at something else?"
- "That's very common in the people we speak to. What changed for you?"

**Cold-lead bonus, tie it to the download:**
- "What was going on around the time you grabbed $RESOURCE? Something happen, or was it just a feeling?"
- "You were looking for something. What made today or this week the time?"

### Probe every answer once or twice
- "What do you mean by that?"
- "How do you mean?"
- "Can you give me an example?"
- "Can you describe that so I get it?"

### Problem duration
- "How long's that been going on?"
- "Is that recent or has it been a while?"
- "How often does that happen?"

### Impact: what's it costing them?
**Plug and play:**
- "This might seem obvious, but just so I understand: how does that affect your day to day?"
- "Do you feel like that's had an impact on your ability to get to $GOAL, and your whole dream life?"

**Predicted (only when you're sure it would be true for almost anyone):**
- Long time, no results: "Has that taken a bit of a toll on your confidence?"
- Stuck in a job: "Has being stuck there started to cost you time or energy at home?"
- Opened with: "I've been in those shoes, so I appreciate you being straight with me."

**Done when:** you can say their pain in their words, with one feeling and one cost. Then stop digging.

---

## 4. TRANSITION: the booked call

### Financial qualification
- "What's the goal here, what are we looking to make from trading each month, a year from now?"
- "And how far are we from that right now, in terms of current monthly income?"
- "And with how expenses are nowadays, how much are you actually able to save from $INCOME every month?"
- "Is $SAVING usually on a tight month, or is that your usual?"
- "And in total, how much have you been able to save?"

**"Why do you need to ask that?"**
> Yeah man, I'm pretty sure you know that in trading it takes money to make money, right? So talk to me, how much have you saved?

### Pitch to the next call
> Alright $NAME, it sounds like this could be a fit. The next step would be connecting you with one of our CSMs, $CLOSER. He can get more in-depth and help you build a plan to get you to $GOAL. Sounds good?

### Schedule and lock
- "Great, let me check the calendar. What would work best, tomorrow or $2DAYS_FROM_NOW?"
- "Morning, afternoon or evening?"
- "$TIME_1 or $TIME_2, which works best?"
- "Okay, $TIME and $DATE."
- "What's your best email for the call details?"
- "Is this number good for a reminder text?"
- "I've sent the invite. Take a moment to check your email and accept it."

### Commitment to show up
- "You're locked in. Just to double check, are you 100% sure $TIME and $DATE works and nothing's coming up that could clash?"
- "If life gets in the way, just message me and we'll reschedule. For the call, find a quiet space, be at your computer, and $CLOSER will break it all down and you two figure out if it's a fit. Cool?"
- "Amazing, stay blessed and take care."

1. Pick one intro version. Try 1 or 2 first.
2. Pick one goal question from section 2 to match it.
3. Pick one catalyst question and practice it out loud until it sounds like you.
4. Everything else is a pick-one from its list.
