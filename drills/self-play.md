# Self-play: be the prospect against yourself

Why: you learn the pull of each question by answering it. (Coach advice: play the prospect against yourself, or an AI.)

## 10-minute drill
1. Pick one persona from `prospect-simulator.md` (cards 1-10).
2. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Say both sides **out loud**.
3. As the prospect: answer in 3-12 words. Don't hand over the emotional truth until the setter has echoed or asked "what do you mean?" twice.
4. As the setter: one question per turn, two short sentences max. Use their word.
5. Stop at the exit test: their pain in their words, one feeling, one cost.
6. Note: layers reached (L1-L5), echoes used, questions that assumed something.

## Variations
- **Same persona, three runs.** Aim for one more layer each time.
- **Swap sides** halfway: prospect for 3 min, setter for 3 min.
- **Hard mode.** The prospect only says "yeah" or "I dunno" until the setter guesses out loud.
- **Read first.** Read one call from `../conversations/`, then play the same persona without looking.

## Check yourself
- Did you recap the deep goal, not the tactical one?
- Did you ask any "why aren't you / why haven't you"?
- Did you keep digging after the exit test?
