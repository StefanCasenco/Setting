> **Dating calls are personal and often a bit embarrassing.** Keep it light where you can, serious where they are. Never judge, never joke about their looks or past. Don't give dating advice on the call. That's the coach's job.

## What to notice

- **"Bit embarrassing, this." "Not at all, mate. Loads of guys apply."** Normalise straight away. Embarrassed prospects need to hear they're not weird.
- **"I talked about my ex-wife for an hour."** He made fun of himself. "That's really normal after twelve years. She's been your whole life." Kind, and true.
- **"How are you doing with the divorce itself, if you don't mind me asking?"** One gentle question. He shared and the setter didn't dig.
- **"The quiet weeks."** Repeating his phrase found the deeper pain (Sunday nights, the drive home).
- **"Yeah. That's a hard drive."** Nothing more. Let it breathe.
- **"No talking about Sarah on Tuesday."** Using his mate's advice as a closing joke. It shows you listened, and it ends warm.
