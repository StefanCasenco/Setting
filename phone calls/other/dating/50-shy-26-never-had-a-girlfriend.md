# 50 · Dating coaching · Old lead, 3 months · 26, never had a girlfriend, very shy

**Prospect:** Aaron, 26, software tester, works from home, US · **Lead:** downloaded a free "confidence" guide in August · **Length:** ~10 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He's very shy and almost hangs up at the start. The setter goes slow, uses easy questions, and never pushes.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Aaron?

**[A]:** Uh. Yes.

**[S]:** Hey Aaron, it's Stefan from Rob's team. Back in August you downloaded our free confidence guide. I'm just checking in to see if it was helpful. Does that ring a bell?

**[A]:** Oh. Um. Yeah. *(long pause)* I'm not really… I don't really do phone calls.

**[S]:** *(gently)* That's totally okay. Honestly, a lot of people don't. I can keep it really short, like five minutes, and if it's too much, just say and I'll let you go. No problem at all. Okay?

**[A]:** *(pause)* …Okay.

**[S]:** Did you get to read the guide?

**[A]:** Yeah. All of it. Twice.

**[S]:** Twice! That's more than most. Was any of it useful?

**[A]:** The part about eye contact. I tried it at the grocery store. *(small laugh)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Nice. How did it go?

**[A]:** The cashier smiled at me. That was it. But it was… something.

**[S]:** That's a real thing, Aaron. A small thing, but real.

**[A]:** *(pause)* Yeah.

**[S]:** Can I ask, what made you download it in the first place?

**[A]:** *(long pause)* I'm 26 and I've never had a girlfriend. *(fast)* Sorry, that's weird to say.

**[S]:** Not weird at all. Honestly, a lot more guys are in that spot than you'd think. They just don't say it. Thanks for telling me.

**[A]:** *(pause)* Okay.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, for you?

**[A]:** Lonely, I guess. I work from home. I game in the evenings. My friends are online, mostly. And I see people my age getting married. My sister's getting married next year. And everyone's going to ask, "Aaron, are you bringing anyone?"

**[S]:** The "are you bringing anyone" question.

**[A]:** Yeah. I already know my aunt's going to ask.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* There's always an aunt.

**[A]:** *(small laugh)* Yeah.

**[S]:** And if things changed, what would you want? Even just a small version?

**[A]:** I think, just to be able to talk to a girl without freezing. Like, I freeze. My mind goes blank. And then I go home and think of what I should have said.

**[S]:** Freezing, then thinking of it later. That happens to a lot of people.

**[A]:** Really?

**[S]:** Really. So it's not that you have nothing to say. It's that it locks up in the moment. Fair?

**[A]:** *(pause)* Yeah. That's exactly it.

**[S]:** Okay. Can I ask a couple of quick questions about money, so I know if it makes sense? Then I'll let you go. Promise.

**[A]:** Okay.

**[S]:** What do you make each month, roughly, from testing?

**[A]:** About $4,800.

**[S]:** And are you able to save?

**[A]:** Yeah. I don't spend much. *(small laugh)* Like $2,000 a month.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[A]:** About $40,000.

**[S]:** Okay. And if there was a coach who could help with the freezing, at your pace, would you be open to investing in yourself for that?

**[A]:** *(pause)* Maybe. I'd be nervous about the call though.

**[S]:** That's okay. So here's the thing. The coach is Josh, he's actually really chill, and he was shy too. The call can be on video or just audio, your choice. You don't have to perform. He'll just talk to you, like this. And if it's too much, you can end it. Does that sound okay?

**[A]:** Audio? I can do audio.

**[S]:** Audio it is. I'll put that in the notes. When's good?

**[A]:** Evenings. After 6.

**[S]:** Josh's got Thursday at 7 or Friday at 7. Which one?

**[A]:** Thursday.

**[S]:** Thursday at 7. Best email?

**[A]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. And Aaron, you did great on this call. Honestly. That was a phone call. *(laughs)*

**[A]:** *(small laugh)* Yeah. I guess it was.

## What to notice

- **"I don't really do phone calls."** Give control: "I can keep it really short… if it's too much, just say and I'll let you go." Very shy prospects stay when they know they can leave.
- **Start with something he did.** "Did you get to read the guide?" "Twice!" It praises effort and makes the first answer easy.
- **"The cashier smiled at me." "That's a real thing, Aaron."** Celebrate small wins with shy people. They don't notice their own.
- **"Sorry, that's weird to say." "Not weird at all."** The biggest moment of the call. Normalise it and thank him.
- **"There's always an aunt."** A small, safe joke that made him laugh for the first time.
- **Adapt the closer call to him** (audio only). Removing his biggest fear is what gets him to show up.
- **"You did great on this call. That was a phone call."** End by pointing out what he just did.
