# 38 · Gaining muscle · Old lead, 2 months · "I've joined a gym already, I'm fine"

**Prospect:** Sam, 28, delivery driver, UK · **Lead:** downloaded a free 4-week plan in September · **Length:** ~9 min · **Ends:** booked

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Sam?

**[SA]:** Yeah?

**[S]:** Hey Sam, it's Stefan from Dan's team. In September you grabbed our free 4-week training plan. I'm just checking in. Did you give it a go?

**[SA]:** Oh, yeah, I did the first week. *(laughs)* Then I joined a gym. I'm alright now, to be honest.

**[S]:** Nice, good for you, man. Which gym?

**[SA]:** PureGym. Near my flat.

**[S]:** How's it going, two months in?

**[SA]:** Yeah, good. I go like… three times a week. Well, I did. Last couple of weeks it's been twice. Work's been mad.

**[S]:** Work's mad how? What do you do?

**[SA]:** Delivery driver. Amazon. And it's Black Friday season, so it's like 11-hour days.

**[S]:** 11-hour days and still getting to the gym twice. That's not bad, honestly.

**[SA]:** *(laughs)* I suppose.

**[S]:** And when you grabbed the plan in September, what was the goal?

**[SA]:** Honestly, just to look better. I've let myself go a bit. I used to play rugby, and I was in decent shape. Then I stopped, and I just… you know. Takeaways. Sitting in a van all day.

**[S]:** Sitting in a van, eating on the go. Hard not to.

**[SA]:** It's impossible. McDonald's at lunch every day, basically.

**[S]:** And since joining the gym, have you seen a difference?

**[SA]:** *(pause)* Not really, no. Honestly. I'm the same weight. A bit stronger maybe.

**[S]:** Okay. And what's the thing that bugs you most, when you think about it?

**[SA]:** I don't know. I used to be the fit one. On the rugby team, I was the fit one. Now my old teammates post pics and I'm like… *(laughs)* I don't post pics.

**[S]:** "I used to be the fit one."

**[SA]:** Yeah. It's a bit of an identity thing, I suppose.

**[S]:** Makes total sense. So it's not just about looking better. It's getting back to feeling like yourself. Fair?

**[SA]:** Yeah. Fair.

**[S]:** And Sam, I'm not here to tell you to quit PureGym, it's good you're going. Can I ask, what do you think is stopping the results, with the gym and everything?

**[SA]:** The food, probably. And I don't really know what I'm doing in there. I just do a bit of everything.

**[S]:** "A bit of everything." *(laughs)* So you've got the gym, which is great. What's missing is a plan that fits around 11-hour van days, and food that isn't McDonald's.

**[SA]:** *(laughs)* Yeah. Basically.

**[S]:** Okay. Quick couple of questions about investing in that, just so I know what makes sense.

**[SA]:** Go on.

**[S]:** What are you spending now? Gym, food, all that?

**[SA]:** Gym's £25. Food… honestly, probably £150 a month on McDonald's alone. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's a lot of McFlurries. And what's coming in, work-wise?

**[SA]:** About £2,200.

**[S]:** And savings?

**[SA]:** About £2,000.

**[S]:** And if there was a coach who built a plan around your actual van days, and checked in every week, would you be open to investing in yourself for that?

**[SA]:** Yeah. If it's not some crazy diet.

**[S]:** Fair. The call's with Jack, one of our coaches, and he works with guys on shifts all the time. He'll look at your actual day, the van, the gym, and tell you honestly what would work. You keep PureGym. Sound good?

**[SA]:** Yeah, go on.

**[S]:** When's a quieter day?

**[SA]:** Sunday.

**[S]:** Jack's got Sunday at 11 or 3. Which one?

**[SA]:** 11.

**[S]:** Sunday at 11. Best email?

**[SA]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. And Sam, maybe skip one McDonald's before Sunday. *(laughs)*

**[SA]:** *(laughs)* No promises.

## What to notice

- **"I've joined a gym, I'm alright now."** "Nice, good for you, man." Then curiosity: "How's it going, two months in?" He told you himself it's not working.
- **"11-hour days and still getting to the gym twice. That's not bad, honestly."** A compliment before the hard question keeps him open.
- **"I used to be the fit one."** An identity pain. Repeat it once.
- **"I'm not here to tell you to quit PureGym."** Respect what they already chose. Then: "What do you think is stopping the results?" He names the gap himself.
- **"That's a lot of McFlurries."** Light humour in the money section, using his own detail.
