> **Fitness calls are different from bizopp.** The goal isn't money, it's how they look and feel. The money questions are about what they can invest in themselves monthly, not about "capital". Never promise a result ("you'll gain 10kg"). Never give medical advice.

## What to notice

- **"What made you start, two years ago?"** The first gym moment is usually the pain. It was the wedding photo and the "coat hanger" line.
- **"I laughed. But I didn't."** Don't laugh along at the pain moment. "Yeah. I know what you mean."
- **"Sounds stupid."** "Doesn't sound stupid at all." Men in fitness calls often downplay how they feel. Take it seriously.
- **"Revenge wedding. I love it."** Matching his humour on the "why now". It keeps it light after the heavier bit.
- **The money section in fitness:** what they spend now, income, savings, then "investing in yourself". Simpler than bizopp, same principle.
- **"Not wasted, you've built the habit, that's the hard bit."** Turn his frustration into progress. He leaves feeling capable.
