## What to notice

- **"Why did it hit you?"** On a webinar call, ask why a stat or moment stood out. It goes straight to the person.
- **"Scared to lift stuff. How does that show up day to day?"** It turns a physical problem into a life one (his son).
- **"He goes to his mom." Pause.** Repeat it and stop. The pause did more than any question.
- **"Treadmill, safe zone."** A gentle joke that shows you understand *why* he's stuck: not lazy, scared.
- **The health disclaimer is said simply, before the money.** "Our coaches aren't doctors… they'd want you cleared." It's safe and honest, and it builds trust.
- **"Already paying a chiropractor to fix it after."** He made the value argument himself. The setter just agreed: "Exactly that."
