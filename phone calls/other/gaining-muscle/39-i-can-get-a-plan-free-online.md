## What to notice

- **"Why pay when I can get it free on YouTube?" in the first ten seconds.** Agree: "You can. There's tons of good free stuff." Then the frame back: "You filled out the application anyway. What had you doing that?"
- **"How long have you been doing the free stuff? What's happened?"** Let his own results answer the objection.
- **"Has someone said something?"** A guess that's often right in fitness. It got "do you even go anymore?"
- **"There's always a girl. I respect it."** Match his energy. Don't make it awkward.
- **One clear line on the value of a coach, after the pain, not before.** "It's not the plan, it's someone watching and adjusting." Short and simple.
