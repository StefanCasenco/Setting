# 44 · Losing weight · Webinar attendee · "My husband says it's a waste of money"

**Prospect:** Linda, 50, school dinner lady, US · **Lead:** attended webinar · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked, husband on the call
**What happens:** The objection comes from someone not on the phone. The setter doesn't attack the husband and makes him part of it.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Linda?

**[L]:** Yes, hi honey.

**[S]:** Hi Linda, it's Stefan from Claire's team. You were on the webinar last night. Does that ring a bell?

**[L]:** Oh, yes. I watched the whole thing. I liked her, the coach lady.

**[S]:** Ah, good! So quick ten minutes, just to see where you're at. Sound okay?

**[L]:** Sure. But I'll tell you now, honey, my husband says these things are a waste of money. So I don't know.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's okay, honestly. A lot of husbands say that. And he might be right, some of them are. Let's just chat, and if it's not right for you, I'll say. Okay?

**[L]:** Okay. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** So what had you watching, the whole thing?

**[L]:** My knees. Honestly. I work in a school cafeteria, I'm on my feet all morning, and my knees are terrible. The doctor said losing some weight would help them a lot. I'm about sixty pounds over what she wants.

**[S]:** Okay. And how are the knees day to day?

**[L]:** Bad. Stairs are hard. I've stopped going to church on Sundays because the parking lot's on a hill and I can't do the walk.

**[S]:** You've stopped going to church because of the hill?

**[L]:** Yes. *(pause)* That's been hard. I've been going to that church thirty years. My friends are there.

**[S]:** Thirty years, and now you can't get up the hill. That's a big thing to lose.

**[L]:** It is. My friends call. But it's not the same.

**[S]:** No. It's not.

**[S]:** So it's not really about the weight for you. It's your knees, and getting back to church, back to your friends. Is that fair?

**[L]:** That's it, honey. That's exactly it.

**[S]:** And have you tried anything before?

**[L]:** Oh, sure. Weight Watchers in the 90s. *(laughs)* Some walking. But with the knees, walking hurts.

**[S]:** Yeah. That makes it hard. Can I ask about your husband? What does he think, about the knees?

**[L]:** Oh, he worries. He drives me to the doctor. He just thinks these programs are scams. His brother paid for one of those infomercial things, and it was a scam.

**[S]:** That makes sense, then. If his brother got scammed, of course he's careful. He's looking out for you.

**[L]:** He is. He's a good man.

**[S]:** He sounds like it. So here's what I'd suggest. Instead of you trying to convince him, what if he was on the next call? He can ask the coach whatever he wants. If he still thinks it's a waste of money, then that's your answer. Would he do that?

**[L]:** *(laughs)* Oh, he'd love that. He'd have a whole list.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Good. Let him bring the list. Okay, couple of quick money ones, just so I know what's realistic.

**[L]:** Okay.

**[S]:** What's coming in for the house, roughly?

**[L]:** I make about $1,800. He's retired, he's got his pension, about $2,500.

**[S]:** And are you able to put anything aside?

**[L]:** A little. We're careful. About $300.

**[S]:** And savings?

**[L]:** We've got some put away. About $15,000. For emergencies.

**[S]:** Of course. And if this was something that helped your knees, and you both felt good about it, would you be open to investing in it?

**[L]:** If he says yes. *(laughs)* Yes.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Fair. The next step is a call with Barbara, she's one of our coaches. She works with a lot of women with knee problems, so it's all low-impact. She'll talk about what's realistic, and your husband can ask her every single question. Sound good?

**[L]:** That sounds good, honey.

**[S]:** When are you both home?

**[L]:** Afternoons, after 2. I finish at the school at 1:30.

**[S]:** Barbara's got Wednesday at 3 or Thursday at 2:30. Which one?

**[L]:** Wednesday.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 3. Best email?

**[L]:** Oh, I don't use email much. Can you text it?

**[S]:** Of course, I'll text it to this number. And tell your husband to bring the list. *(laughs)*

**[L]:** *(laughs)* I will. Thank you, honey.

## What to notice

- **"My husband says it's a waste of money" in the first minute.** "A lot of husbands say that. And he might be right, some of them are." Agreeing removes the fight.
- **"You've stopped going to church because of the hill?"** Repeat the surprising detail as a question. It opened the real pain (friends, thirty years).
- **"If his brother got scammed, of course he's careful. He's looking out for you."** Never make the absent spouse the bad guy. Make him the protector.
- **"Instead of you trying to convince him, what if he was on the next call?"** It takes the pressure off her and gives the closer the real decision maker.
- **"Let him bring the list."** It turns his scepticism into something useful.
- **Adjust for older prospects** (texting instead of email). Small things like this stop no-shows.
