# 42 · Losing weight · Old lead, 5 months · "I've tried everything, nothing works for me"

**Prospect:** Tanya, 36, hairdresser, US · **Lead:** downloaded a free meal plan in June · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** She's tired and a bit hopeless. The setter doesn't argue with "nothing works" and finds the one thing she hasn't tried.

---

**[T]:** Hi, who's this?

**[S]:** Hey Tanya, it's Stefan from Claire's team. Back in June you grabbed our free meal plan. I'm checking in on people to see how it went. Does that ring a bell?

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Oh, yeah. I did it for like five days.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Five days is five more than most people. What happened on day six?

**[T]:** *(laughs)* My kid's birthday. Cake. And then I was like, eh, I already messed it up.

**[S]:** Ha, the "already messed it up" thing. Everybody does that.

**[T]:** Yeah. Honestly, I've tried everything. Keto, Weight Watchers, Noom, intermittent fasting, the shakes, I even did the shots for a bit. Nothing works for me.

**[S]:** That's a lot of things. How long have you been trying?

**[T]:** Since my second kid. So like, seven years.

**[S]:** Seven years of trying. That's exhausting.

**[S]:** Yeah. I can hear you're tired of it. And honestly, I'm not going to tell you this time is magic. Can I ask you something, though?

**[T]:** Sure.

**[S]:** All those things, keto, Weight Watchers, the shakes, did any of them work at all, even for a bit?

**[T]:** Oh, yeah. They all work for a bit. I lost 20 pounds on keto. Then it came back.

**[S]:** So it's not that nothing works. It's that nothing *lasts*.

**[T]:** *(pause)* …Huh. Yeah. That's true actually.

**[S]:** Okay. And what's the main reason you want it to last this time? Like, what's the thing?

**[S]:** Yeah. Kids say it straight.

**[T]:** They do. And also I'm on my feet all day at the salon, and my knees and my feet are killing me. I'm 36.

**[S]:** On your feet all day, and it hurts. That's hard.

**[S]:** So it's not about another plan. It's having help for when life happens, so it actually lasts. For your son, and for your knees. Fair?

**[T]:** Yeah. That's fair.

**[S]:** Couple of quick ones about investing in this, so I know what makes sense.

**[T]:** Okay, but I've spent a lot on this stuff already. Just saying. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **"Five days is five more than most people."** Turn the failure into a small win on an old lead.
- **"Nothing works" was never argued with.** "I'm not going to tell you this time is magic." Then a question: "Did any of them work at all, even for a bit?"
- **"So it's not that nothing works. It's that nothing *lasts*."** The reframe came from her own answer. She saw it herself.
- **"Has anyone ever helped you with the 'after'?"** It finds the gap in all seven years without criticising any of the programs.
- **"I've spent a lot on this stuff already."** "That's exactly why I'm asking." The same move as always: their worry is your reason.
- **"No need to start a diet before Monday. Just show up as you are."** It takes away the pressure to "prepare", which often turns into a no-show.
