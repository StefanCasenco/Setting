## What to notice

- **Match high energy.** "Multitasking!" and laughing along. A flat setter kills an excited prospect.
- **"Oh no. What happened?"** When the story turns. Simple, warm, and it gets the full dress-shop moment.
- **"That's not what you want to hear in a wedding dress shop."** Siding with her against the shop assistant. A shared bit of outrage builds rapport fast.
- **"The ones I put on the wall."** Repeat the image. It's for her kids, which is deeper than the dress.
- **"I'm a bit worried about this bit."** She flagged it, so thank her for it and give the reason. Then a gentle question: "would that come from the wedding pot, or separate?" She framed it herself.
- **"She'll go through the cost straight, so you can decide."** A worried prospect needs to know the price won't be a trap.
