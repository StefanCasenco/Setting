> **Credit repair rules (US).** Never promise a score, a number of points, or that anything will be "deleted" or "removed". Never say "we guarantee". Never tell them to stop paying anything. ## What to notice

- **"Good for you for walking."** Praise the decision he's embarrassed about. It shows respect.
- **"'Oh, you're one of those.'"** That was the shame moment. "That's a bad feeling." No more needed.
- **"So there's a real clock on this."** The broken car is the "why now". Name the deadline out loud.
- **"Most people I talk to have a story behind the number."** The key credit repair lubricant. It turns shame into a story.
- **"You pay something and it doesn't move."** Agree with his frustration. Don't explain how credit scoring works. That's for the specialist.
- **"I can't promise you any numbers, nobody honest can."** Said plainly in the pitch. It's legally safe and it builds trust.
