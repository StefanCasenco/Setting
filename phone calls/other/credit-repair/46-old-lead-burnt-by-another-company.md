## What to notice

- **"I already got scammed by one of you people."** Don't defend: "I'd be angry too." Then: "You should be." Validating anger brings it down.
- **"I'm not going to stand here and defend them."** Never defend the industry. You lose trust instantly.
- **"I'm not going to ask you to trust me on this call."** For a burnt prospect, asking for trust raises the guard. Not asking lowers it.
- **"Break-ins, in your building?"** Repeat the scary detail. That was the real pain (safety, not a score).
- **"If you don't like the answers, you walk."** Give her full control. Burnt prospects book when they feel in control.
- **"Write your questions down."** It turns suspicion into prep for the closer.
