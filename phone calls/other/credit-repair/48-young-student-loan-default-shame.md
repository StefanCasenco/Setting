## What to notice

- **"No judgement at all, I promise," at the very start.** With a quiet, ashamed prospect, say it before anything else.
- **"She got in and you didn't."** A simple repeat of the painful bit, with no added drama.
- **"Nobody knows?"** Small surprise, then: "That's a lot to carry on your own." Name the hidden weight.
- **"That's not you being careless. That's you being 20 and dealing with a lot."** It reframes the shame with the facts she gave you. It's not flattery.
- **"Is that bad?" "Not bad at all. It's your business."** Respect her privacy, and plan the booking around it ("a time you can talk privately?").
- **Student loans: don't give advice.** "There can be specific programs, she'll explain." Point to the specialist. Don't guess.
