## What to notice

- **Let them vent fully when it's not their fault.** "$23,000. In your name. That you didn't do." Repeat the unfairness slowly. He needs to feel heard before anything else.
- **"Like I'm the criminal." "That's so unfair."** Two words of agreement are enough. Don't add advice.
- **"Don't apologise, honestly. I'd be furious too."** When an angry prospect apologises for being angry, give them permission.
- **"It's not that you're not trying… you're doing it alone."** That reframe respects his effort (IT guy, police reports) and names the real problem.
- **"I can't promise outcomes. But you won't be doing it alone."** Honest about limits, clear about what help is.
