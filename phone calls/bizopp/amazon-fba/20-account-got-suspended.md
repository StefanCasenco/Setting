## What to notice

- **Use what you already know.** "You stayed all the way through the Q&A… the one about suspended accounts, right?" Starting from his own question is the best context there is.
- **"A bot deciding on eight grand of your money. Yeah, I'd throw my phone too."** Join his anger. Don't calm him down.
- **"So after that, why come back? Most people would never touch Amazon again."** A compliment and a pain question together. It opened the "cha-ching in the attic" moment.
- **"March and October, like clockwork."** The setter repeated it and later used it to ask "why now". He laughed: "Right on time."
- **"I can't promise you Amazon won't do anything, nobody can."** Never promise what you can't control. Then give what you can: someone who's been through it.
- **Bring evidence to the closer** (suspension emails). Past failure is easier to sell when the closer can see it.
