## What to notice

- **"Whose idea was the webinar?"** Light and playful. It shows you who's driving and who's careful in the first ten seconds.
- **Ask the quiet partner directly.** "Tom, what did you think, being dragged?" He felt included instead of ambushed.
- **"Somebody in the house needs to be careful."** It respects his role straight away, so he stays on side.
- **"Most families I speak to are doing exactly that."** The Center Parcs embarrassment, normalised. Jess calmed down.
- **When they started arguing: "Can I jump in for a sec?"** Then "you're both right," with a specific reason for each. Never pick a side. Then: "That's not something you have to solve tonight." That's the closer's job.
- **Two goals, two people.** "700, Tom's off call. 2,000, Jess goes part-time." Each partner hears their own goal.
