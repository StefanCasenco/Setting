# 35 · SMMA · No-show twice · The setter names the pattern gently

**Prospect:** Jake, 23, works at a sports shop, does TikTok for his mate's gym for free, UK · **Lead:** booked twice with the closer, no-showed both times · **Length:** ~7 min · **Ends:** rebooked, with a real commitment

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Jake?

**[J]:** Oh. Hey. Yeah.

**[S]:** Hey Jake, it's Stefan, from Sam's team. I think you know why I'm calling. *(laughs)*

**[J]:** *(laughs awkwardly)* Yeah. Sorry, man. I know. Twice.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Twice. Honestly, I'm not angry. I'm just curious. Can I ask you something straight, and you be honest with me?

**[J]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** Do you actually want to do this? Because if you don't, that's completely fine. I'll take you off and you won't hear from me again. No hard feelings at all.

**[J]:** *(pause)* No, I do. I do want to.

**[S]:** Okay. So what happened? Both times?

**[J]:** First time I genuinely forgot. I was at work and I didn't check my phone. Second time… *(pause)* honestly, I got nervous. I thought he was going to ask me loads of questions I didn't know the answer to. And then it'd be about money and I don't have loads.

**[S]:** Thank you for being honest, man. That's actually really normal. A lot of people get nervous before that call.

**[J]:** Yeah?

**[S]:** Yeah. And honestly, the call isn't a test. There's no wrong answers. He's just going to look at what you're doing with your mate's gym TikTok, and see if it makes sense to help. And if the money doesn't work, he'll say, and that's fine.

**[J]:** Okay. That helps.

**[S]:** And remind me, when we spoke, you said the main thing was?

**[J]:** I'm at the sports shop, and I hate it. And I'm good at TikTok, my mate's gym went from nothing to like 9k followers. And I want to do that as a job, instead of selling trainers.

**[S]:** Yeah. And is that still true?

**[J]:** Even more now. My manager put me on Saturdays every week. Every week.

**[S]:** Okay. So, Jake, I'll rebook you, but I want to do it properly. When is a time you will 100% be at home, not at work, phone in your hand?

**[J]:** Sunday. Sunday afternoon.

**[S]:** Okay. Sunday at 3 or Sunday at 5?

**[J]:** 3.

**[S]:** Sunday at 3. I'm going to send the invite. And I want you to do three things for me. Accept it now, while we're on the phone. Set an alarm on your phone for 2:45. And if you get nervous on Sunday, text me before, not after. Deal?

**[J]:** *(laughs)* Deal.

**[S]:** Okay, sent. Accept it?

**[J]:** Done. And I'm setting the alarm now. Okay. 2:45.

**[S]:** Perfect. And Jake, it's a conversation, not an exam. Bring your mate's gym stats if you want, and just be yourself. Speak Sunday.

**[J]:** Cheers, Stefan. Sorry again.

**[S]:** Don't be sorry, just be there. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **"I think you know why I'm calling."** Lightly, with a laugh. It names the elephant without blame.
- **"Do you actually want to do this? If you don't, that's completely fine."** After two no-shows you need a real answer. Giving the exit makes the yes real.
- **"So what happened? Both times?"** He told you the real objection: nerves about questions and money.
- **"That's actually really normal."** Then reframe the call: "It isn't a test. There's no wrong answers." Most repeat no-shows are fear, not laziness.
- **Three small actions, on the phone:** accept now, set an alarm, text before you cancel. Small steps beat promises.
