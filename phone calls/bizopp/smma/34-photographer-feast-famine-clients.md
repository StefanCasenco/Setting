# 34 · SMMA (social media marketing agency) · Webinar attendee · Wedding photographer, feast and famine

**Prospect:** Ellie, 29, wedding photographer, self-employed, UK · **Lead:** attended the webinar, asked about "getting retainer clients" · **Length:** ~12 min · **Ends:** booked

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Ellie?

**[E]:** Hi! Yes.

**[S]:** Hey Ellie, it's Stefan from Sam's team. You were on Sam's webinar last night about starting a social media agency. You asked about retainer clients, I think?

**[E]:** Yes! That was me.

**[S]:** Perfect. So quick ten minutes, see where you're at and if we can help. Sound fair?

**[E]:** Yes.

**[S]:** So, retainers. That question sounded like it came from somewhere. *(laughs)*

**[E]:** *(laughs)* It did. I'm a wedding photographer. And wedding season is April to September. I work every weekend, I edit all week, I'm making good money. And then October hits and… nothing. Like, I've had one booking since September.

**[S]:** One since September. How's that been?

**[E]:** Scary. Every year I tell myself I'll save enough in summer. And every year I don't, quite. And then January comes and I'm checking my account every morning.

**[S]:** Every morning in January.

**[E]:** Yeah. And I also, honestly, I'm getting a bit tired of weddings. *(laughs)* Is that bad?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Not bad at all. Tired how?

**[E]:** Like, twelve-hour days on my feet with two cameras, then 60 hours of editing per wedding. And I'm 29, and all my friends are getting married, and I can't go to their weddings because I'm shooting someone else's.

**[S]:** Wait, you miss your friends' weddings to shoot strangers' weddings?

**[E]:** *(laughs)* Yes! I missed my best friend's hen do this year. I was in Cornwall shooting a couple I'd met twice.

**[S]:** Oof. How did that feel?

**[E]:** *(pause)* Awful. She was nice about it. But I saw the photos on Instagram and I just felt… left out of my own life.

**[S]:** "Left out of my own life."

**[E]:** Yeah. That's how it feels most summers.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's a big thing to say.

**[S]:** So it's not just about steady money. It's having something that pays all year, so you're not missing your own life every summer. Fair?

**[E]:** Yes. That's it exactly.

**[S]:** And why social media management, out of everything?

**[E]:** Because I already do my own Instagram really well. I've got 14k followers, and most of my bookings come from it. And other small businesses keep asking me how I do it. My hairdresser literally asked me last week.

**[S]:** So you've already got the skill and people asking. Have you done it for anyone?

**[E]:** I did my friend's café, for free. Their followers went from 800 to 3,000 in four months.

**[S]:** That's a real case study, Ellie. For free. *(laughs)*

**[E]:** *(laughs)* I know. I'm bad at charging.

**[S]:** Okay, quick money ones, just so I know where you're at.

**[E]:** Sure.

**[S]:** What would the agency need to make for you to only shoot weddings you actually want?

**[E]:** If I had £2,500 a month, all year round, from retainers, I'd only do ten weddings a year instead of thirty.

**[S]:** And averaged across the year, what do you take home a month?

**[E]:** About £2,800. But it's like £5,000 in summer and £500 in winter.

**[S]:** And savings, right now?

**[E]:** About £6,000. That's meant to get me to April. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** So that's your runway. Okay. Credit?

**[E]:** I've got a business card.

**[S]:** And would you make the decision on your own?

**[E]:** I'd talk to my boyfriend. But it's my business, so mostly me.

**[S]:** And if there was a way to turn the café results into paying retainers, would you be open to investing time, energy and resources into it?

**[E]:** Yes. Especially now, in the quiet months.

**[S]:** Okay. Next step is a call with Rachel, one of our coaches. She'll look at the café case study, how to price it, and how to get retainer clients before next wedding season. Sound good?

**[E]:** Yes!

**[S]:** I'm guessing you're free-ish at the moment? *(laughs)*

**[E]:** *(laughs)* Very free. Unfortunately.

**[S]:** Rachel's got tomorrow at 11 or Thursday at 2. Which one?

**[E]:** Tomorrow.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 11. Best email?

**[E]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Bring the café's before and after numbers. And Ellie, go to the next hen do. *(laughs)*

**[E]:** *(laughs)* I will!

## What to notice

- **"That question sounded like it came from somewhere."** Turn their webinar question into the opener. It's easy and personal.
- **"Is that bad?" "Not bad at all."** Give quick permission when people feel guilty about their own feelings.
- **"Wait, you miss your friends' weddings to shoot strangers' weddings?"** Saying the irony out loud, with surprise. She laughs, then tells the real cost.
- **"Left out of my own life."** That was the pain. One repeat, then "That's a big thing to say."
- **"That's a real case study, Ellie. For free."** Point out the proof she already has, with a little tease about charging.
