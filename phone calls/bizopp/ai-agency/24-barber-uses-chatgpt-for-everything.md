# 24 · AI agency · New lead · Barber who uses ChatGPT for everything

**Prospect:** Kwame, 30, owns a one-chair barbershop, UK · **Lead:** application 1 hour ago · **Length:** ~12 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He's at work. He has to stop for a client halfway through, and the setter holds the thread.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Kwame?

**[K]:** Yo! *(clippers buzzing)* Hold on, let me turn these off. Yeah?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Hey Kwame, it's Stefan from Zain's team. You put an application in about an hour ago, about starting an AI agency. Does that ring a bell?

**[K]:** Yeah, yeah, that was me. I've got like fifteen minutes before my next one.

**[S]:** Perfect, that's all I need. So just a quick chat to see where you're at, and if we can help. If we can, great, if not, no stress. Sound fair?

**[K]:** Yeah, go.

**[S]:** So, a barber looking at an AI agency. *(laughs)* I love it. What's the story?

**[K]:** *(laughs)* Right? So, I've got my own shop, one chair, it's just me. And like a year ago I started using ChatGPT for everything. My Instagram captions, replying to DMs, I made a booking bot thing with ManyChat. And my bookings went up like 40%. And then other barbers in my area started asking me, "yo, how did you do that?" And I set it up for two of them. They paid me like £150 each.

**[S]:** Hold on, so you've already sold it. Twice.

**[K]:** *(laughs)* I suppose. But like, for cheap. I didn't know what to charge.

**[S]:** And what made you think about doing it properly?

**[K]:** Because I'm standing here ten hours a day, and my back is killing me, and I'm thinking, I made £300 in like two evenings on a laptop. And it takes me like three hours of cutting to make £300.

**[S]:** Two evenings vs three hours of cutting. And you're doing ten-hour days?

**[K]:** Ten, eleven. Six days. Saturdays are mad.

**[S]:** And how's that on you, standing ten hours?

**[K]:** Bro, my lower back. I'm 30 and I've got a physio. And I'm like, I can't be doing this at 45. The older barbers I know, they're all broken. My uncle's a barber, he can't lift his arm properly.

**[S]:** Your uncle can't lift his arm.

**[K]:** Rotator cuff. From thirty years of this. *(gestures)* And he's still cutting because he's got no choice.

**[S]:** "No choice."

**[K]:** Yeah. That's what scares me. Not the back. The no choice.

**[S]:** Yeah. That makes sense.

*(door chime)*

**[K]:** Oh, hold on, that's my 2 o'clock, he's early. Give me one sec. *(muffled)* Yeah, bro, sit down, two minutes. *(back)* Sorry.

**[S]:** No stress. I'll be quick. So basically, it's not about AI being cool. It's having a choice before your body makes it for you, like your uncle. Fair?

**[K]:** Yeah. That's it.

**[S]:** Quick money ones, then I'll let you get back to him. If the agency worked, what would you want it bringing in, a year from now?

**[K]:** If it made £3k a month, I'd cut three days a week instead of six.

**[S]:** And the shop, what are you taking home a month?

**[K]:** About £3,200 after rent and everything.

**[S]:** And saving each month?

**[K]:** About £500. I'm good with money.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[K]:** About £8k.

**[S]:** And if there was a way to turn the £150 setups into proper clients, would you be open to investing time, energy and resources into that?

**[K]:** Hundred percent.

**[S]:** Okay. Next step is a call with Zain's coach, Leon. He'll look at what you've already built, the ManyChat thing, and help you work out what you should actually be charging, and how to get more than two barbers. Sound good?

**[K]:** Yeah, man.

**[S]:** When's the shop closed?

**[K]:** Mondays.

**[S]:** Leon's got Monday at 11 or 3. Which one?

**[K]:** 11.

**[S]:** Monday at 11. I'll text you the link now since you've got a client. Just click accept when he's gone.

**[K]:** Perfect, bro, appreciate that.

**[S]:** And Kwame, charge more than £150. *(laughs)*

**[K]:** *(laughs)* I know, I know. Cheers.

## What to notice

- **"A barber looking at an AI agency. I love it. What's the story?"** A warm, curious opener that fits the person. It's better than a standard intent question when the combination is surprising.
- **"Hold on, so you've already sold it. Twice."** Point out what he's already done. People undervalue their small wins.
- **"No choice."** He said it, the setter repeated it, and he gave the real fear: "Not the back. The no choice."
- **When the client walked in, the setter got shorter, not slower.** "I'll be quick." A one-line summary, then the money questions in short form. Respect his time and he'll respect yours.
- **"I'll text you the link now, just click accept when he's gone."** Adapting the booking to his situation, instead of making him check email in the middle of his shop.
