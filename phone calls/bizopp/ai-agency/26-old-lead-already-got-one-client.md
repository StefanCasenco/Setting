# 26 · AI agency · Old lead, 4 months · "I've already started, I've got one client"

**Prospect:** Danielle, 26, used to work in a call centre, now part-time with one AI client, US · **Lead:** applied in July, didn't book · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Danielle?

**[D]:** Hey, who's this?

**[S]:** Hey Danielle, it's Stefan from Zain's team. You applied back in July about starting an AI agency. I'm checking in on people who never got a call. Does that ring a bell?

**[D]:** Oh! Yeah. Actually, I started! I've got a client.

**[S]:** No way, that's awesome! Congrats. How'd you land them?

**[D]:** *(laughs)* My aunt has a dental office. I built her a chatbot that answers questions and books appointments. She pays me $500 a month.

**[S]:** $500 a month, recurring. That's real. How's it going?

**[D]:** Good! She loves it. She says it saves her receptionist like two hours a day.

**[S]:** That's a great case study. So what's next for you?

**[D]:** *(pause)* That's the thing. I don't know. I've been trying to get a second client for like two months. Nothing.

**[S]:** Two months. What have you tried?

**[D]:** I emailed like fifty dentists. Two replied. One said "not interested", one asked for a price and then ghosted.

**[S]:** Ghosted after the price. What did you quote them?

**[D]:** $1,500 setup, $500 a month. Same as my aunt, basically.

**[S]:** Okay. And did you show them your aunt's results?

**[D]:** *(pause)* …No. I didn't think of that.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's okay, that's literally what the next step is for. And Danielle, you said you used to work in a call centre?

**[D]:** Yeah, I quit in September. I'm doing part-time at Target now so I have time for this.

**[S]:** You quit to do this? That's brave.

**[D]:** Or stupid. *(laughs)* My mom thinks it's stupid.

**[S]:** What does she say?

**[D]:** "You had a stable job, Danielle." She worked at the same hospital for 25 years. She doesn't get it.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, her saying that?

**[D]:** *(pause)* It makes me want to prove her wrong. But also, like, what if she's right? I'm making less now than before. I'm at Target folding shirts.

**[S]:** Folding shirts, after you quit to build your own thing.

**[D]:** Yeah. It's humbling. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Yeah. So basically, you've proven it works with your aunt. What you need now is client number two, three, four, so you're not folding shirts and your mom's not right. Fair?

**[D]:** *(laughs)* Yeah. Exactly.

**[S]:** Okay, quick money ones, just so I know where you're at.

**[D]:** Okay, but I don't have a lot. Just so you know.

**[S]:** That's okay, I'd rather know than guess. What would the agency need to make for you to quit Target?

**[D]:** Like $3,000 a month.

**[S]:** And right now, Target plus your aunt?

**[D]:** About $1,900.

**[S]:** And are you able to save any of that?

**[D]:** Not much. Like $100. I live with my boyfriend, we split rent.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[D]:** About $4,000. From the call centre.

**[S]:** And credit?

**[D]:** I have a card. I'm scared of using it.

**[S]:** Fair. And if there was a way to get clients two, three and four, would you be open to investing time, energy and resources into that?

**[D]:** Yes. That's literally all I want.

**[S]:** Okay. Next step is a call with Leon, one of our coaches. He'll look at your aunt's chatbot, the fifty emails, and what you should be doing differently to get the next client. And he'll tell you honestly if it makes sense with where you're at money-wise. Sound good?

**[D]:** Yeah!

**[S]:** When are you not at Target?

**[D]:** Wednesdays and Sunday mornings.

**[S]:** Leon's got Wednesday at 10 or 1. Which one?

**[D]:** 10.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 10. Best email?

**[D]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Bring your aunt's numbers, how many hours it saves, anything like that.

**[D]:** Okay! Thank you!

## What to notice

- **An old lead who already started is a great lead.** "No way, that's awesome! Congrats." Celebrate first. Then "what's next for you?" finds the gap.
- **"What did you quote them?" and "Did you show them your aunt's results?"** Small, specific questions about what she tried show the problem without a lecture.
- **"You quit to do this? That's brave."** Her mum's voice is the pain. "Or stupid" told you it was there.
- **"I don't have a lot, just so you know."** "That's okay, I'd rather know than guess." The same calm reply as always. Don't promise anything.
- **"Bring your aunt's numbers."** Prep the closer with real proof. That makes the call easier for both of them.
