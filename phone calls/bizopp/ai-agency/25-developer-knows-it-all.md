# 25 · AI agency · Webinar attendee · Software developer: "I could build this myself"

**Prospect:** Arjun, 31, backend developer at a fintech, UK · **Lead:** watched the webinar, asked a technical question in chat · **Length:** ~12 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He's sceptical that he needs help, because he can code. The setter agrees with him on the tech and finds the gap that isn't technical.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Arjun?

**[A]:** Yeah, hi.

**[S]:** Hey Arjun, it's Stefan from Zain's team. You were on the webinar on Wednesday. You asked about the LangChain stack in the chat, I think?

**[A]:** *(laughs)* Yeah. That was me.

**[S]:** I'll be honest, I had to Google half of that question. *(laughs)* So it's a quick ten minutes, to see where you're at and if we can help. Sound fair?

**[A]:** Sure. Though, honestly, I'm not sure I need a course. I could build everything they showed in a weekend.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I believe you. You're probably more technical than most of the people on that webinar. So what made you sign up, if you could build it yourself?

**[A]:** *(pause)* …That's a fair question. I suppose I wanted to see how they get clients.

**[S]:** Ah. So the building isn't the problem.

**[A]:** No. I've built like four things. A lead scorer, a support bot, a document thing for a law firm my friend works at. They're good. Nobody's paying for them.

**[S]:** Four things built, nobody paying. Why do you think that is?

**[A]:** I don't know how to sell. I'm a developer. I hate calls. *(laughs)* No offence.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* None taken, I get it. And what had you building things on the side in the first place?

**[A]:** Honestly, I'm bored at work, and I'm underpaid. I'm on 62k. Contractors on my team, doing the same work, are on 600 a day. That's like 140k.

**[S]:** Same work, more than double.

**[A]:** Yeah. And I watch them, and they're not better than me. They just took the risk.

**[S]:** And how does that sit with you, sitting next to them?

**[A]:** It's annoying. *(pause)* More than annoying. I feel like I'm wasting my best years making someone else money. I'm 31, I wanted to have my own thing by 30.

**[S]:** You wanted it by 30.

**[A]:** Yeah. I had a plan. *(laughs)* I missed it.

**[S]:** Yeah. That kind of deadline sticks with you.

**[S]:** So it's not about learning to build AI stuff. It's not wanting to be the guy who could have done it, while everyone else took the risk. Fair?

**[A]:** *(pause)* …Yeah. That's annoyingly accurate.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* And what's stopped you, other than hating calls?

**[A]:** Pricing. Like, what do I even charge? And finding clients. I don't have a network outside tech.

**[S]:** Makes sense. Can I ask a couple of quick money ones, just so I know where you're at?

**[A]:** Sure.

**[S]:** A year from now, what would the agency need to make for you to go independent?

**[A]:** If I had three clients on £2k retainers, I'd leave.

**[S]:** And current take-home?

**[A]:** About £3,700 a month.

**[S]:** And able to save each month?

**[A]:** About a grand.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[A]:** About 30k. Plus some shares.

**[S]:** And honestly, Arjun, since you could build it all yourself, would you be open to investing time, energy and resources into learning the part you don't know, the selling, the pricing?

**[A]:** If it's actually about sales, not "here's how to use ChatGPT", then yes.

**[S]:** Fair. That's exactly what I'd want to check. So next step is a call with Leon, one of our coaches. I'll tell him straight, you don't need the tech side. He'll look at your four builds, help you with pricing, and show you how people with no network get clients. If it's just ChatGPT basics for you, he'll tell you. Sound good?

**[A]:** Yeah, that's fair.

**[S]:** When are you free?

**[A]:** Lunchtimes, I work from home Tuesdays and Fridays.

**[S]:** Leon's got Tuesday at 12:30 or Friday at 1. Which one?

**[A]:** Tuesday.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 12:30. Best email, not the work one?

**[A]:** *(laughs)* Definitely not. *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Have the four builds ready to demo if you can.

## What to notice

- **"I could build this in a weekend."** Don't argue: "I believe you, you're probably more technical than most." Then the key question: "So what made you sign up, if you could build it yourself?" He has to name the real reason.
- **"I had to Google half of that question."** Honest self-humour with a technical prospect. Don't fake knowledge.
- **"Same work, more than double."** A short summary of his comparison. Then "how does that sit with you, sitting next to them?" makes it personal.
- **"I wanted to have my own thing by 30."** A missed self-deadline is a strong pain for this kind of person. The setter repeated it once and didn't push.
- **Brief the closer to skip what he knows.** "I'll tell him straight, you don't need the tech side." That handles his objection before the next call.
