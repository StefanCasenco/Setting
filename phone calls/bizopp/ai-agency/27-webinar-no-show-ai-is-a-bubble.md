# 27 · AI agency · Webinar no-show · "Isn't AI just a bubble?"

**Prospect:** Graham, 47, project manager at a construction firm, UK · **Lead:** registered for the webinar, didn't attend · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He pushes back on the whole idea. The setter doesn't debate. He asks why he signed up anyway.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Graham?

**[G]:** Speaking.

**[S]:** Hi Graham, it's Stefan from Zain's team. You registered for Zain's webinar on AI agencies, Tuesday night. Does that ring a bell?

**[G]:** Ah. Yes. Didn't make it, I'm afraid. Football.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Football wins, fair enough. Who do you support?

**[G]:** Villa. Lost, as well, so it was a bad night all round. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Ah, unlucky. So, quick ten minutes, just to see where you're at and if it's even worth it for you. Sound fair?

**[G]:** Go on. Although I'll be honest, I'm a bit sceptical. Isn't this AI thing just a bubble? Like the dot-com stuff. Everyone's selling courses on it.

**[S]:** Honestly, Graham, could be. There's definitely a lot of hype, and a lot of people selling courses who shouldn't be. I'm not going to argue with you on that. But you signed up anyway, so something caught your eye. What was it?

**[G]:** *(pause)* …Fair point. My son showed me something. He made a thing that reads tender documents. In construction, we get these 200-page tenders, and somebody has to read them all. It took him an afternoon. It used to take my team a week.

**[S]:** A week to an afternoon.

**[G]:** Yeah. And I thought, well, if that's real, there's money in that. Every construction firm in the country has that problem.

**[S]:** That's a real problem you know from the inside. And what had you thinking about doing it yourself, rather than just telling your boss?

**[G]:** *(laughs)* Because my boss would take the credit and I'd get a pat on the head. Twenty-two years in construction, I've had a lot of pats on the head.

**[S]:** Twenty-two years of pats on the head. *(laughs)* How do you feel about the job these days?

**[G]:** Tired. The site stuff, the stress, contractors not turning up, me getting it in the neck from the client. And I'm 47. I look at the directors, they're my age, and they got there because they had the right school, the right connections. I didn't.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, at 47?

**[G]:** Like the door's closing. Honestly. If I don't do something in the next few years, I'll be doing this till I'm 67.

**[S]:** Till 67.

**[G]:** Twenty more years of contractors not turning up. *(laughs)* No thanks.

**[S]:** So basically, it's not about AI being the next big thing. It's about using what you know from 22 years, before the door closes. Fair?

**[G]:** *(pause)* Yes. Put like that, yes.

**[S]:** And you've got your son, who can build it?

**[G]:** He's 20, he's at uni. He can build it, but neither of us knows how to sell it or price it.

**[S]:** Okay. Money side, quick, just so I know where you're at.

**[G]:** Go on.

**[S]:** If this worked, what would it need to make for you to leave the firm?

**[G]:** Honestly, I'd need to match my salary. About 4,500 a month after tax.

**[S]:** And what are you on now?

**[G]:** That. 4,500.

**[S]:** And are you able to save much?

**[G]:** About 800. Kids are mostly grown.

**[S]:** And savings in total, outside pension?

**[G]:** About 25k.

**[S]:** And would you decide with your wife?

**[G]:** Yes, definitely.

**[S]:** And if there was a way that turned the tender thing into something real, would you be open to investing time, energy and resources into it?

**[G]:** If it's real, yes. I'm not throwing money at hype.

**[S]:** Totally fair. So, next step, a call with Leon, one of our coaches. He'll look at the tender idea specifically and tell you honestly if it's a real business or just hype. Bring your son if you want, since he built it. Your wife too. If Leon thinks it's not worth it, he'll say. Sound fair?

**[G]:** That's fair.

**[S]:** When suits? Not a Villa night. *(laughs)*

**[G]:** *(laughs)* Thursday evening.

**[S]:** Leon's got Thursday at 7 or 8. Which one?

**[G]:** 7.

**[S]:** Thursday at 7. Best email?

**[G]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it for me? And if your son can show the tender thing on the call, even better.

**[G]:** I'll ask him. Cheers, Stefan. Better than the webinar, probably. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **"Isn't AI just a bubble?"** Don't debate the market. "Could be, there's a lot of hype, I'm not going to argue with you." Then the frame back: "But you signed up anyway, so something caught your eye."
- **Small talk with a purpose.** Football is a natural way in, and the lost match became a shared joke at the end. It helped book a non-Villa night.
- **"A week to an afternoon."** Repeat the result he saw. That's his real reason, not the hype.
- **"Twenty-two years of pats on the head."** His joke, repeated, and it turned into the pain (the door closing).
- **"If Leon thinks it's not worth it, he'll say."** For a sceptic, permission to hear "no" is what makes him book.
