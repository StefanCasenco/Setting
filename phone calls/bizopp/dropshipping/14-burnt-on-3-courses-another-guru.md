## What to notice

- **"I'm over gurus" in the first ten seconds.** "That's fair, and I appreciate you saying it straight." Then the frame: "I'm not going to pitch you anything on this call." Agreeing with his past disappointment lowers the wall.
- **"Walk me through it"** lets him vent the three courses. A burnt prospect needs to tell that story before he can hear anything new.
- **"A Discord where nobody answered, that's like 80% of them."** Agreeing with his complaint about the industry puts you on his side.
- **"Why keep going after three? Most people quit after one."** That's a great lubricant. It's a compliment and a pain question in one, and it led straight to the freezer and the baby.
- **The money section was framed with his own history.** "After spending two grand already, I don't want to put you in something that doesn't make sense." He said, "I appreciate that."
- **"If she's not convinced, you shouldn't do it."** Taking the girlfriend's side builds more trust than trying to get around her.
