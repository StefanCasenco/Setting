## What to notice

- **Don't answer the yapper's questions, and don't ignore them either.** "They're all good ones. Can I make a suggestion?" Then a trade: you answer mine, the coach answers yours, properly, with real numbers.
- **Give a reason that helps *him*.** "You're a busy guy and I don't want to waste your hour." Busy business owners respect that.
- **When he rambles, pull out one phrase and ask if it's the main thing.** "'Everything is you.' Is that the main thing?" He stopped and said yes.
- **"How is that on you? Not the business. You."** The second sentence matters. Business owners answer as the business unless you ask about them.
- **He tried to take the frame back mid-call ("let me ask you, the products…").** A laugh plus "that's on the list, I promise" got it back. No "please let me finish."
- **"Please do, he likes a list."** Turning his question habit into prep for the closer instead of a problem.
