## What to notice

- **No pain, no urgency, no money set aside means no booking.** He's happy, it's been a week, and his savings are "Dubai money". Booking him would hurt the closer's show and close rates.
- **The test question:** "If it took like a year of working on it at night, would you still want it?" It's fair and honest, and it shows whether they want the result or the work.
- **Don't sell the fantasy.** "That's what the Lambo videos make it look like. It's not like that." Setters who promise easy money create refunds.
- **"Is that more Dubai money?"** A playful way to ask if the savings are really available. He laughed and told the truth.
- **Leave the door open.** "If the phone shop starts doing your head in…" A respectful no today is a real lead in six months.
