# 15 · Dropshipping · Webinar attendee · 21-year-old uni student, quiet, one-word answers

**Prospect:** Aisha, 21, final-year biology student, works at Boots weekends, UK · **Lead:** attended the webinar yesterday · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** She gives short answers. The setter uses guesses and options to get her talking, never pressure.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Aisha?

**[A]:** Hi.

**[S]:** Hey Aisha, it's Stefan from Nate's team. You were on Nate's webinar yesterday, about dropshipping. Does that ring a bell?

**[A]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** Cool. So it's just a quick chat, ten minutes, to see where you're at. If we can help, great, if not, no stress. That okay?

**[A]:** Yeah, okay.

**[S]:** Did anything stand out from the webinar?

**[A]:** Um. The part about not needing a lot of money to start.

**[S]:** Okay. Is money a bit tight at the moment?

**[A]:** Kind of. I'm a student.

**[S]:** Ha, student life. What are you studying?

**[A]:** Biology.

**[S]:** Biology. What year?

**[A]:** Final year.

**[S]:** Oh, final year, so you're nearly done. Is the plan to do something with biology after, or are you not sure yet?

**[A]:** *(pause)* My parents want me to do pharmacy. Like a master's.

**[S]:** Your parents want you to. What do you want?

**[A]:** *(long pause)* …I don't really want to. *(laughs nervously)*

**[S]:** *(gently)* That's okay. Lots of people don't know yet at 21. What makes you not want to?

**[A]:** I don't know. Another four years. And then working in a pharmacy, like, every day, the same thing. My cousin does it and she's always stressed.

**[S]:** So you're seeing what it looks like already, through your cousin.

**[A]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** And if you didn't do pharmacy, what would you want instead? Even if it sounds silly.

**[A]:** *(pause)* I like making things. Like, I do these… I make jewellery. Beaded stuff. People at uni buy them sometimes.

**[S]:** Oh, really? People buy them?

**[A]:** Yeah, like, I sold maybe thirty? On Instagram.

**[S]:** Thirty! Aisha, that's actually a business. *(laughs)*

**[A]:** *(laughs)* It's just a small thing.

**[S]:** Small things are how everything starts. So when you saw the webinar, was it about that? Doing something that's yours, instead of four more years of something you don't want?

**[A]:** *(pause)* Yeah. I think so. But I haven't told my parents.

**[S]:** That's okay. Do you think they'd be against it?

**[A]:** They'd be… worried. They didn't go to uni. They really want me to have a "proper job".

**[S]:** That makes sense. They want you safe.

**[A]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, honestly? Wanting something different from what they want?

**[A]:** Bad. Like I'm being ungrateful. They worked really hard.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's a heavy thing to carry at 21.

**[A]:** *(quiet)* Yeah.

**[S]:** Okay. Can I ask a couple of quick questions about money? Just so I know what's realistic for you. No pressure.

**[A]:** Okay.

**[S]:** You work as well as studying, right?

**[A]:** Weekends at Boots.

**[S]:** And what do you make there a month, roughly?

**[A]:** Like 600.

**[S]:** And living at home, or in student halls?

**[A]:** At home.

**[S]:** So are you able to save some of that?

**[A]:** Yeah, like 400 a month.

**[S]:** That's actually really good. And in total, have you saved up anything?

**[A]:** About 4,000. From Boots, since first year.

**[S]:** 4,000 from weekend shifts. That's discipline, Aisha.

**[A]:** *(laughs)* Thanks.

**[S]:** And if there was a way to turn the jewellery or a store into something real, would you be open to investing in yourself for it, time, energy and resources?

**[A]:** Maybe. I'd be scared to use the savings without telling my parents.

**[S]:** That's honest, and that's a real thing to think about. Here's what I'd suggest. A call with Leah, she's one of our coaches. She'll look at what you've already done with the jewellery, and tell you honestly if it makes sense, and what it would actually take. And you can ask her how to even talk to your parents about it. She's had that conversation herself. No pressure to decide anything on the call. Would that help?

**[A]:** Yeah. That would help actually.

**[S]:** When's good for you, around uni?

**[A]:** Tuesday afternoons I'm free.

**[S]:** Leah's got Tuesday at 2 or 3:30. Which one?

**[A]:** 2.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 2. Best email?

**[A]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Can you check it and accept it?

**[A]:** Done.

**[S]:** Perfect. And Aisha, have a couple of the bracelets with you if you can. Leah will want to see them. *(laughs)*

**[A]:** *(laughs)* Okay. Thank you.

## What to notice

- **One-word answers need guesses and options, not more open questions.** "Is money a bit tight at the moment?" "Is the plan to do something with biology, or not sure yet?" She only had to say yes or pick one.
- **"Your parents want you to. What do you want?"** A short, gentle follow-up on the word that mattered ("want").
- **"Even if it sounds silly."** It gives permission. Quiet people hide things that sound silly, and the jewellery was the real story.
- **"Thirty! Aisha, that's actually a business."** Real excitement about something she was downplaying. After this she talked more.
- **The parents weren't treated as the enemy.** "They want you safe." The setter respected them. The closer can help with that conversation, and the setter offered it as part of the call.
- **"No pressure to decide anything on the call."** For a young, anxious prospect, this is what gets her to show up.
