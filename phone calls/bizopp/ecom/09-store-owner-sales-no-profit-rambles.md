# 09 · Ecom · New lead · Has a store with sales but no profit · A big rambler

**Prospect:** Marco, 38, owns a small pet accessories store on Shopify, also works in logistics, Canada · **Lead:** application this morning · **Length:** ~15 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He talks for two minutes at a time about suppliers, ads and Shopify apps. The setter lets him go, then brings him back with one short line each time.

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Marco?

**[M]:** Yes, hello!

**[S]:** Hey Marco, it's Stefan from Leo's team. You filled out an application this morning about getting some help with your store. Does that ring a bell?

**[M]:** Yes, yes, that was me! Thank you for calling so quick.

**[S]:** Of course. So it's a quick 10, 15 minutes. I want to understand where the store's at, where you want it to be, and if we can actually help. If we can, I'll walk you through the next step. If not, no stress. Sound good?

**[M]:** Perfect, perfect.

**[S]:** So tell me a bit about the store.

**[M]:** Okay, so, it's pet stuff. Collars, leashes, these harnesses with the little reflective strips, and we started with, like, dog bandanas, because my wife had the idea, she makes them, well she used to make them, now we get them from a supplier in China because she couldn't keep up, and the supplier is good, mostly, but sometimes the shipping is like three weeks and then people complain, and I had to change the Shopify theme because the old one was slow, and I installed maybe, I don't know, fourteen apps? Reviews, upsells, the bundles one, and then my Facebook ads guy, he's from Fiverr, he says the ROAS is 2.1 but I don't know, when I look at the bank account there's nothing there, so I'm thinking, where is the money going, you know?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Okay, so a lot going on. Let me make sure I've got the main thing. You've got sales, but when you look at the bank account, there's nothing there.

**[M]:** Exactly. Exactly that.

**[S]:** How much is the store doing a month, roughly, in sales?

**[M]:** Around 8,000. Some months 10.

**[S]:** That's real sales. And after the ads, the supplier, the apps, Fiverr guy, what's left?

**[M]:** Honestly? I don't know. My accountant says we made like $2,000 profit last year. For the whole year.

**[S]:** For the whole year. On maybe 100k in sales.

**[M]:** Yes. I know. *(laughs)* It's crazy.

**[S]:** And you said you also work in logistics?

**[M]:** Yes, full-time, dispatch for a trucking company. I do the store at night. And weekends. And lunch. *(laughs)* My wife says I'm married to the laptop.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Married to the laptop. What does a normal night look like?

**[M]:** Kids go to bed at 8:30, I open the laptop, I answer emails, customer is saying where is my package, I check the ads, I talk to the Fiverr guy, he's in another time zone so sometimes I'm up at midnight. Then I'm at work at 7.

**[S]:** Midnight, then work at 7. How long have you been doing that?

**[M]:** Two years. Since we started.

**[S]:** And honestly, how is it on you, two years of that?

**[M]:** *(pause)* I'm tired, Stefan. And the worst part is, I don't even know if it's working. If I knew it was building something, I'd do it. But I'm working all these hours for $2,000 a year? That's like, I don't know, fifty cents an hour. *(laughs)* I should just sleep.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Fifty cents an hour, ouch. And what does your wife think?

**[M]:** She is supportive. But last month she said, "Marco, either it works or we stop. You're never with us." And she's right.

**[S]:** "Either it works or we stop."

**[M]:** Yes. That's when I filled out the form, honestly.

**[S]:** Yeah. That makes sense.

**[S]:** So if I'm hearing you, the store isn't the problem. The problem is you're putting in two years of nights and you can't see if it's going anywhere, and now there's a deadline at home. Fair?

**[M]:** Yes. That's very good. That's exactly it.

**[S]:** And over those two years, have you had any help, other than the Fiverr guy? A course, a mentor?

**[M]:** I bought a course in the beginning, $500, about Facebook ads. But it was old, from 2020. Everything changed.

**[S]:** Yeah, ads change every six months. And if you had to point at the one thing that's eating the profit, what would you guess? Ads, product cost, the apps, or you don't know?

**[M]:** Honestly, I don't know. That's the problem. I think the ads. But maybe it's the shipping, because we do free shipping, and maybe that's… you know, and also the returns, because harnesses, people get the wrong size, and then—

**[S]:** *(gently)* So you don't know. That's the honest answer, right?

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Yes. I don't know.

**[S]:** That's okay. That's what we'd look at. Can I ask a couple of quick questions on the money side, just so I know where you're at personally?

**[M]:** Of course.

**[M]:** If it made $4,000 profit a month, I would quit dispatch. Then I can do the store in the day and be with my family at night.

**[S]:** $4,000 profit, quit dispatch, family at night. And what's coming in from dispatch now?

**[M]:** About $4,200 a month after tax.

**[M]:** We have about $15,000. And the store has some money in the account, maybe $3,000, for inventory.

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Yes. She will like that.

**[S]:** When are you both free? Not midnight. *(laughs)*

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Sunday afternoon is best.

**[S]:** Nina's got Sunday at 2 or 4. Which one?

**[M]:** 2. Kids nap.

**[S]:** Sunday at 2. Best email?

**[M]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Can you check and accept it? And if you can, have your Shopify and your Facebook ads manager open on the call. Nina will want to look at the numbers live.

**[M]:** Yes, I have it all. Fourteen apps. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* She'll have fun with those. Sunday at 2, nothing that'll come up?

**[M]:** No, no. We'll be there.

**[S]:** Perfect. Get some sleep tonight, Marco. *(laughs)*

**[M]:** *(laughs)* I will try. Thank you, Stefan.

## What to notice

- **Let the rambler finish the first one.** Then sum it up in one line: "So you've got sales, but when you look at the bank account, there's nothing there." He says "Exactly", and now you lead.
- **Bring him back with a short, kind line.** "So you don't know. That's the honest answer, right?" No "sorry to interrupt". Just name the point he's circling.
- **"Fifty cents an hour, ouch."** He made a joke. Joining his joke builds rapport faster than any question.
- **His wife's line was the pain.** "Either it works or we stop." The setter repeated it and didn't add anything.
- **"Ads, product cost, the apps, or you don't know?"** Giving "you don't know" as an option makes it okay to admit.
- **Prep for the closer** (have Shopify and ads manager open). Stores with real numbers close better when the numbers are on screen.
