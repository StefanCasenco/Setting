## What to notice

- **"How much is it?" #1, at the start.** "Straight to it, I respect that." Then the honest answer: it depends, that's what this call is for. Then an exit: "if it's clearly not a fit, I'll tell you in five minutes."
- **#2, in the middle.** "I can tell this is really bugging you." Naming her frustration defuses it better than repeating the script. Give a little ("it's an investment, not a £50 course") so it doesn't feel like hiding. Then a small time box: "Two more minutes."
- **#3, at the pitch.** Answered by a promise: the closer *will* tell you the price. She gets what she wants, at the right time.
- **"What are you checking for?"** About the bank app. A tiny detail question that opened the real feeling.
- **"No offence" about cold calling.** "None taken, I know exactly what you mean." You're on the phone with her, so own it with a laugh.
- **Don't fake a price range you don't know.** If your offer lets you give a range, do it at #2. If not, don't make one up.
