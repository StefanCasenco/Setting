## What to notice

- **When the spouse jumps in sceptical, welcome them.** "Great question, and I'm glad you're here." A sceptical spouse who feels heard becomes your best ally. One who feels dodged kills the deal.
- **Answer the scam question plainly, then don't over-sell.** "Nobody's recruiting anybody… but you shouldn't just take my word for it." Then, "I'd be suspicious too." Agreeing with the feeling defuses it.
- **"Can I ask Rosa a couple of questions? Then you can tell me if I've got it wrong."** That takes the frame back from Mike, kindly, and gives him a job.
- **Questions to both at key moments.** "Is that fair, both of you?" keeps Mike in it without letting him take over.
- **"Why do you need all this?" from the sceptic.** Answered with protecting *their* college fund. Mike: "That's more than my cousin's guys asked." Real reasons build trust.
- **"If you don't like what you hear, you walk."** The sceptic gets control, so he shows up.
