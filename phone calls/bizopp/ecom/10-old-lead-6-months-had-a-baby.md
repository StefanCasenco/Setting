## What to notice

- **Read the background noise.** "Sounds like you've got your hands full… is now a terrible time?" before anything else. Then "while she lets us". It turns the baby into a shared joke instead of a reason to hang up.
- **The cancellation was turned into a story.** "I'm guessing something pretty big happened since May?" She gets to tell good news, not explain why she flaked.
- **"Claire cried in the staffroom."** Someone else's pain is often how prospects show you their own fear. "And now that Isla's here, how are you feeling about going back?" brings it back to her.
- **She got emotional. The setter said less, not more.** "Don't apologise. That's a big thing to think about." Then moved on gently.
- **"Maternity pay is not a lot"** was a warning before the money questions. Defused with a laugh plus the reason.
- **"Babies don't check calendars."** It gives permission to reschedule, so she does that instead of no-showing.
