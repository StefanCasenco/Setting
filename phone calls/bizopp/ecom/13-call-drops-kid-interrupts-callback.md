## What to notice

- **Interruptions are normal. Don't fight them.** "Take your time." Then, when he's back and lost: give him his last line, word for word. "Patients shouting at you because the GP hasn't sent the prescription." It shows you were listening, and he jumps right back in.
- **Call drops: call back straight away, and restart from the exact sentence.** "We were at you waking up at 3am…" He laughed: "You remember." That moment builds more trust than any question.
- **"Is it bath-time madness?"** A context line at the open that shows you heard the chaos.
- **His wife's line was the pain.** "You're not here even when you're here." Repeated once, nothing added.
- **"That makes complete sense for a pharmacist."** Turning his caution into a strength, not a blocker.
- **Booking around the kids** ("after they're in bed"). Book family prospects for when they can actually talk.
