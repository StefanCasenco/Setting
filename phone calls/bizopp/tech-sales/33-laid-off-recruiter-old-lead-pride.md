# 33 · Tech sales · Old lead, 5 months · Laid-off tech recruiter, proud, a bit defensive

**Prospect:** Marcus, 35, was a tech recruiter for 7 years, laid off 4 months ago, US · **Lead:** downloaded "SDR resume template" in June · **Length:** ~11 min · **Ends:** booked

**[M]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** Hey Marcus, it's Stefan from Chris's team. Back in June you grabbed our SDR resume template. I'm checking in on people who never got a call. Does that ring a bell?

**[S]:** Totally get it. What were you doing before?

**[M]:** Tech recruiting. Seven years. I placed SDRs and AEs, actually. So I know the whole space. *(laughs)* I know exactly what hiring managers want.

**[S]:** Oh, so you're on the other side of this. *(laughs)* You probably know more about SDR hiring than I do.

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Probably.

**[S]:** So what had you grabbing an SDR resume template, if you know it all?

**[M]:** *(pause)* …I was thinking of switching to sales myself. Before the layoffs. I saw the AEs I was placing making double what I made.

**[M]:** In July. The whole recruiting team. Hiring freeze. Nobody needs recruiters when nobody's hiring. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Yeah. Four months. How's the search going?

**[M]:** It's fine. It's a tough market. I'm being picky.

**[M]:** *(pause)* …Okay, honestly? It's not going great. Recruiting jobs don't exist right now. And sales jobs, they see "recruiter" and they think I'm a resume guy, not a closer. Which is crazy, because recruiting is sales. I sold candidates on jobs every day.

**[M]:** It's maddening. I've placed people who were worse than me in jobs I can't get.

**[M]:** *(long pause)* My severance ran out last month. My wife's carrying us right now. She's a nurse. She's doing extra shifts.

**[S]:** And how does that feel?

**[M]:** Bad, man. I'm the one who's supposed to… *(stops)* Yeah. It's bad.

**[S]:** Yeah. I hear you.

**[S]:** So it's not that you don't know the space. It's that you're stuck on the wrong side of the resume, and every week it's on your wife. Fair?

**[M]:** Appreciate that.

## What to notice

- **"I'm fine, just between roles."** Pride. Don't push it. Ask about what he did before, and let him be the expert.
- **"You probably know more about SDR hiring than I do."** Giving status to a proud person lowers the guard. Then: "So what had you grabbing the template, if you know it all?"
- **"Picky how?"** The gentle follow-up on his cover word. He admitted the truth himself.
- **"Recruiting is sales. I agree with you completely."** Agreeing with his frustration puts you on his side.
- **"I'll be careful here, because I know it's tight."** Before the money questions with someone out of work. Saying it out loud is the defusing.
- **"He's the one who's supposed to…"** He didn't finish. The setter didn't make him. "Yeah. I hear you." Some pain doesn't need to be said in full.
