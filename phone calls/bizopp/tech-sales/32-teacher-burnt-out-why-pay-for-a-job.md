## What to notice

- **"Are you parked, or driving?"** Safety first, and it told you where she was emotionally (sitting in the car park after school).
- **"A B-minus."** Repeat the absurd detail with a laugh. She laughs, then tells you the real story.
- **"You don't have to be fine, honestly."** When she apologised for being upset. It's short and it lets her be real.
- **"Difficult parents are basically difficult customers."** Using her friend's line, she sees her skills fit.
- **"Why would I pay to get a job?"** Agreed first ("some people get in for free, like your friend"). Then her own 30 applications as the reason. Then the out: "if you don't need that, the coach will tell you."
