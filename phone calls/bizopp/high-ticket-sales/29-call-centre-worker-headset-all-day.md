## What to notice

- **Calling within minutes catches the emotion live.** "What had you applying, on your break?" got the fresh story (the screaming man) straight away.
- **"Called you thick. For twenty minutes. And you just had to sit there."** Repeat the hard detail back, slowly. She feels heard.
- **The reframe: her pain is her skill.** "You're good enough to stay calm with someone calling you thick. That's a skill." For call-centre prospects, this is the turning point.
- **"Timing your toilet breaks?"** Reacting honestly to a mad detail ("that's mad") builds rapport faster than any technique.
- **Short break, short money section.** "I know you've only got a few minutes." Respect the clock out loud.
