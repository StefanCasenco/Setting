**What happens:** The real problem isn't knowledge, it's follow-through. The setter names it kindly and doesn't pretend another program fixes it by magic.

## What to notice

- **Use their own words from the webinar chat.** "I saw your message, '3 programs lol'." He laughed, and the call started honest.
- **"Module 4?"** One small specific question showed the pattern faster than "what happened?".
- **"I'm going to be real with you, cool?"** Then the straight truth: "You don't have a knowledge problem. You have a finishing problem." Said with care, it builds trust. Said cold, it's an insult.
- **Don't promise the product fixes it.** "Another program on its own won't fix that." It's honest, and it sets the closer up to show what's different, if anything.
- **"This is your first test. Show up Sunday."** A playful line that turns showing up into proof for himself.
