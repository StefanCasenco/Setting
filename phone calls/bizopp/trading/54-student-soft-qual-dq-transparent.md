# 54 · Trading · New lead · Student, disqualified early with Scott's transparent line

**Prospect:** Max, 20, second-year engineering student, no job, UK · **Lead:** application from TikTok · **Length:** ~5 min · **Ends:** financial DQ, kindly
**What happens:** Soft qualification shows a student with no income within a minute. The setter uses Scott's transparent binary question and saves everyone's time.

---

**[S]:** Hey Max, how you doing, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You applied about getting help with trading. What got you into it in the first place?

**[M]:** Yo. Honestly, I've been watching ICT stuff, and I think I'm getting decent at reading charts.

**[S]:** Nice. Where are you based, and what are you doing at the moment, work or study?

**[M]:** Sheffield. I'm at uni, engineering, second year.

**[S]:** Engineering, good stuff. Are you working alongside it at all?

**[M]:** Nah. Just student loan, and my parents help a bit.

**[S]:** Okay. And are you trading with real money yet?

**[M]:** I've got like £200 in an account. Up about £40. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Up's up. Look, Max, I'll be transparent with you, so I don't waste your time. If you're looking for our coaching, the program's a mid four-figure investment. Is that something that'd be viable for you right now, or is it completely out of the question?

**[M]:** *(pause)* Ah. Nah. That's out of the question, mate. Like, I've got £200.

**[S]:** Totally fair, and honestly, thank you for being straight. That's the right answer for where you are. I'd much rather tell you now than have you sit through a 45-minute call.

**[M]:** Yeah, appreciate it. Most people would just try and sell me anyway.

**[S]:** Nah. Here's what I'd do. Keep the £200 account small and treat it like a lab. Write down every trade and why. Ray's got free stuff on YouTube on risk management. Watch that before anything else. And if you're still at it when you've got a summer job, or you graduate, give us a shout. Can I check in with you next summer?

**[M]:** Yeah, go on. That's cool.

**[S]:** Perfect. Good luck with the exams, man.

**[M]:** Cheers.

## What to notice

- **Soft qualification in the first minute.** "Where are you based, and what are you doing, work or study?" then "Are you working alongside it?" That's Scott's step 2. It tells you who to prioritise.
- **Scott's line, word for word:** "I'll be transparent with you… the program's a mid four-figure investment. Is that viable for you right now, or completely out of the question?" Here a binary question is right, because you want a fast yes or no.
- **A financial DQ, protecting the closer's calendar.** Scott: it respects your time, the closer's time *and* the prospect's time.
- **He leaves happy** ("most people would just try and sell me"). A good DQ is a future lead.
- **Don't do this to someone working a decent job just because they're young.** It's about income, not age.
