## What to notice

- **Not every call should book.** Booking a 19-year-old with $300 and no card into a high-ticket call wastes the closer's time and hurts the show rate. And it isn't right for him.
- **The money questions still ran fully.** You don't decide to disqualify on a feeling. You ask, and the answers decide.
- **"I'm gonna be straight with you, cool?"** It asks permission before giving bad news, so the "no" lands soft.
- **The reason was about him.** "You'd either stress yourself out or ask your parents." Not "you can't afford it".
- **He leaves with something.** Free videos, a homework habit, a check-in date. Leads you disqualify kindly come back, and they tell their friends.
