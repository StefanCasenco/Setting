## What to notice

- **"Just want more money" came back three times. The setter never asked "why?" once.** Instead: current income, then target number, then "what would three grand change?". Numbers are hard to be vague about.
- **"Give me a number, even a rough one."** When they say "loads", ask for a guess. Most people will give one.
- **"Move out from where?"** The first concrete thing was the way in. Then SIT: situation (dad's), impact (girlfriend asking "when"), timeline (two years).
- **One knowledge/guidance question, open-ended enough.** He picked "not knowing what to do", which is knowledge.
- **Temp check, then "what's holding it back from a 10?"** That got the fear ("scared I'll lose what I've got"), which led naturally into the money questions.
