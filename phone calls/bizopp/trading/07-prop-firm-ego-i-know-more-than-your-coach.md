# 07 · Trading · New lead · Blew 3 prop accounts, big ego · "I probably know more than your coach"

**Prospect:** Liam, 26, sells phones on commission, UK · **Lead:** application yesterday · **Length:** ~13 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He tests the setter with jargon and talks down. The setter doesn't compete, stays curious, and takes the frame back when it matters.

## What to notice

- **"What's your strategy?" in the first ten seconds is a test.** The setter didn't fake knowledge: "I'm not the trader, I'm the guy who makes sure you're speaking to the right person." That's honest, and it puts the frame back on the setter's job.
- **"I probably know more than your coach."** The setter agreed with a laugh ("could be, 200 hours is a lot") and asked the real question: "So what had you filling in an application?" He can't keep the ego up and answer that at the same time.
- **"Walk me through the last one."** A story question. He told the NFP story and found his own problem: "It's me."
- **"So it sounds like the problem isn't the theory."** One short sentence that flipped the whole call. No lecture.
- **"If this is a 5k course, I'm not paying."** The frame back: "I wouldn't want you to either… you don't need another strategy course." Agree with the part that's true, then repeat why you're asking.
- **Matching the closer to the prospect** ("he used to be prop"). An ego prospect needs to respect who's on the next call.
