# 05 · Trading · No-show reschedule · He missed the closer call and is embarrassed

**Prospect:** Ahmed, 35, IT support, UK · **Lead:** booked for yesterday 7pm with the closer, didn't show, didn't reply to texts · **Length:** ~7 min · **Ends:** rebooked

---

**[S]:** Heyy… Ahmed?

**[A]:** *(pause)* …Yeah.

**[S]:** Hey Ahmed, it's Stefan, from Ray's team. We spoke last week, about the trading.

**[A]:** Oh. Yeah. Look, mate, I'm really sorry about yesterday, I—

**[S]:** Ahmed, honestly, no stress at all. I'm not calling to tell you off. *(laughs)* I just wanted to make sure you're alright. You were really up for it when we spoke, so when you didn't show I thought, okay, something must've come up.

**[A]:** Yeah. *(sighs)* My daughter was sick. Temperature, the whole thing. We ended up at A&E till like midnight.

**[S]:** Oh man, I'm sorry. Is she okay now?

**[A]:** Yeah, she's fine, it was just a virus. But it was scary.

**[S]:** Yeah, I bet. That's way more important than any call, honestly. Glad she's okay.

**[A]:** Thanks. And I saw your texts, I just… I felt bad, so I didn't answer. Which is stupid.

**[S]:** Not stupid, man, everyone does that. *(laughs)* I do it with my dentist.

**[A]:** *(laughs)*

**[S]:** So look, when we spoke last week, you said the main thing was, you've been in IT support for nine years, you've been passed over for the promotion twice, and you want something that's yours, so you're not waiting on a manager to decide your worth. Is that still where you're at?

**[A]:** Yeah. Nothing's changed. If anything, last night made it worse.

**[S]:** Worse how?

**[A]:** Sitting in A&E till midnight, knowing I've got to be in at eight. Can't take the day. If I had my own thing, I could've just stayed home with her.

**[S]:** Yeah. That makes sense.

**[S]:** So do you still want to speak to Marcus? No pressure at all. If now's not the time, tell me and I'll take you off.

**[A]:** No, no. I want to. I just felt like I'd messed it up.

**[S]:** You didn't mess anything up. Marcus has kids too, he gets it. Honestly, he'll probably ask how she is before anything else. *(laughs)*

**[A]:** *(laughs)* Okay.

**[S]:** So, same time, evenings work better?

**[A]:** Evenings, yeah. But maybe not this week, she's still home.

**[S]:** Makes sense. He's got next Monday at 7 or Tuesday at 7:30. Which one?

**[A]:** Tuesday.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 7:30. Same email as before?

**[A]:** Same one.

**[S]:** Sent. Can you check it and hit accept? And this time, if anything comes up, even last minute, just send me one word. "Sick." "Busy." Anything. I'll move it, no questions.

**[A]:** Yeah. I will. Sorry again.

**[S]:** Stop apologising, man. *(laughs)* Go look after your little one. Speak soon.

**[A]:** Cheers, Stefan.

---

## What to notice

- **The first thing out of his mouth was an apology. The setter cut it off kindly.** "I'm not calling to tell you off." An embarrassed prospect will dodge you forever if they feel judged.
- **"I do it with my dentist."** A tiny self-joke that makes not answering texts feel normal.
- **Recap from last call, in his words.** "Nine years, passed over twice, something that's yours." He doesn't have to explain again, and he remembers why he booked.
- **"Worse how?"** He gave a new pain from last night (can't stay home with her). The no-show became a reason.
- **"If now's not the time, tell me and I'll take you off."** Giving an easy way out makes it easier to say yes.
- **"Just send me one word."** A super low bar for next time. People who feel bad don't write long messages.
