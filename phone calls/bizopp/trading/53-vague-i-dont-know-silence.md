# 53 · Trading · Free guide lead · Vague: "I don't know" to everything

**Prospect:** Jade, 25, works in a supermarket bakery, UK · **Lead:** downloaded a trading guide 4 days ago · **Length:** ~9 min · **Ends:** booked
**Vague pattern:** "I don't know" is her safe answer. The setter uses silence and "if you had to guess?" and doesn't fill the gap for her.

---

**[J]:** I don't know. I just saw it.

**[S]:** Fair. And is trading something you're actually looking to get into, or more just having a look?

**[J]:** I don't know. Maybe. Yeah.

**[J]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** *(pause, two seconds)*

**[J]:** Yeah. I've stopped getting invited, kind of.

**[J]:** *(pause)* …Normal hours. A normal life. Like, go to the pub on a Friday. Have a boyfriend. *(laughs)* I don't know.

**[S]:** That doesn't sound like "I don't know". That sounds pretty clear.

**[J]:** *(laughs)* I suppose.

**[S]:** Roughly. If you had to guess.

**[J]:** Same as now? Fifteen hundred. Then I could do it from home and sleep normal.

**[J]:** I don't kn— *(stops)* …My best mate had her birthday on Saturday and I left at 9 to sleep. She was upset. She said, "you're never here anymore."

**[S]:** "You're never here anymore."

**[J]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's hard to hear from your best mate.

**[J]:** It was.

**[J]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** When are you awake? *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **The two-second silence after "it's fine".** No new question. She filled it with the real answer. That's the most useful tool with "I don't know" people.
- **"If you had to guess?"** used three times. It's simple and it works. It gives her permission to say something without being sure.
- **"That doesn't sound like 'I don't know'. That sounds pretty clear."** It shows her she does know, kindly.
- **Never answer for her.** It would have been easy to say "so you want normal hours, right?" Scott: force them to say it.
- **She caught her own habit** ("I keep saying that"). Let her laugh at it. Don't point it out yourself.
