# 01 · Trading · New lead, 6 minutes after opting in · "Is this a scam?"

**Prospect:** Jamal, 33, Uber driver · **Lead:** application, called 6 minutes after · **Length:** ~14 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He rambles about crypto in 2021, asks if it's a scam, and goes cold for a second when money comes up.

## What to notice

- **"Is this a scam?" was answered with agreement, not defence.** "That's a smart question, there's a lot of fake stuff." Then the frame: nothing sold on this call, I'll tell you straight. Arguing that you're legit makes you sound less legit.
- **The ramble had the gold in it.** Out of a whole crypto story, the setter picked "I didn't sell, I just watched it go" and "didn't tell my girl for a month". That led to the car, which led to the pain.
- **"Here we go, this is where you ask for my card."** Tension. Defused with a laugh plus a real reason: "You've already been burnt once, I'm not trying to do that to you again." It uses his own story as the reason.
- **"I don't wanna go in debt for this."** The setter agreed: "I wouldn't want you to either." He didn't push. That's the closer's call.
- **Lubricant lines:** "out of all the ways to make money online", "drunk people tip" (echoing his joke), "you know how it is now, with rent, a four-year-old".
