## What to notice

- **"Don't be sorry, half the people who sign up don't make it."** Removes the guilt straight away. A guilty prospect tries to get off the phone.
- **"What happened Tuesday?"** is a lubricant question. It's easy, it's about her day, and her day turned out to be the whole pain (three jobs, cereal at 8:30).
- **"So that's three jobs, basically."** Saying her life back in one sentence. She hadn't seen it that way.
- **"If you had that time, what would you actually do with it?"** It finds the real goal (school) behind the surface goal (two hours a day).
- **"I'm not, like, rich, just so you know."** A small bit of tension before the money questions. "That's okay, neither am I" plus the reason defused it.
- **"I'm gonna be annoying about this one."** She's already no-showed once, so the setter says it with a laugh, no guilt, and makes sure she accepts the invite.
