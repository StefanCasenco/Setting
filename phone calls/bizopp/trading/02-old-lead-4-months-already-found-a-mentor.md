# 02 · Trading · Old lead, 4 months · "I already found a mentor"

**Prospect:** Ollie, 27, warehouse team leader, UK · **Lead:** downloaded a free guide in July, never booked · **Length:** ~12 min · **Ends:** booked
**What happens:** He shuts it down early ("sorted, mate"). The setter takes the frame back with curiosity instead of pushing.

## What to notice

- **The old-lead opener admits it's random.** "This is a bit of a random one… you were one of the people who never got a call." Honesty lowers the guard on a reactivation call.
- **"I'm sorted, I've got a mentor" was met with "nice, good for you".** No fight. Then curiosity: "how's it going?" He gave the real answer himself (down £400, can't trade alone).
- **"I'm not here to slag anyone off, if it's working, crack on."** That's taking the frame back without attacking his choice. Saying it makes him more honest, not less.
- **"Is it getting you closer to the house, or not really?"** This is the turning question. It ties the Discord to his own goal, so he judges it, not you.
- **"Most people I speak to have been in a signals group at some point."** It normalises the thing he's slightly embarrassed about.
- **The pitch left him the Discord.** "If it's not for you, you keep the Discord." No either/or, so less resistance.
