# 52 · Trading · Webinar attendee · Vague: "The job's boring" (the Scott reframe)

**Prospect:** Patrick, 31, bank clerk, Ireland · **Lead:** attended the webinar last night · **Length:** ~10 min · **Ends:** booked

---

**[S]:** Hey Patrick, how you doing, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You were on Ray's webinar last night. What stood out to you?

**[P]:** Ah, it was grand. Just the idea of doing something different, you know.

**[S]:** Different from what? What are you doing at the moment?

**[P]:** I work in a bank. Branch, front desk. It's boring.

**[S]:** Boring. How long have you been there?

**[P]:** Six years.

**[S]:** And when you say boring, what do you mean?

**[P]:** Same thing every day. It's repetitive.

**[S]:** Okay. And I'll be honest with you, Patrick, trading's repetitive too. Same charts every morning, same setups, waiting for the same thing over and over. A lot of days nothing happens at all. So knowing that, why trading? What do you actually want out of it?

**[P]:** *(pause)* …Huh. Fair point. I suppose… it's not the boring. It's that it's going nowhere.

**[S]:** Going nowhere. What do you mean?

**[P]:** Like, in the bank, there's no up. They've closed four branches in our area in two years. They're not promoting anyone. They're just waiting for us to leave or retire. I'm 31 and I'm on the same money as when I was 27.

**[S]:** Same money as four years ago. What are you on?

**[P]:** About €2,400 a month.

**[S]:** And if trading worked out, what would you need it to bring in for it to matter?

**[P]:** If it did a grand a month on top, I'd feel like I was moving.

**[S]:** And what would that extra grand change?

**[P]:** Honestly? I'd stop feeling like I'm wasting my thirties. My brother's in tech in Dublin, he's on triple what I am, and every Christmas I'm sat there at dinner and they ask, "still at the bank, Paddy?"

**[S]:** "Still at the bank, Paddy."

**[P]:** *(laughs)* Every year. Like clockwork.

**[S]:** And how does that land with you?

**[P]:** It's a joke, but it stings. I was meant to be the smart one, you know. In school.

**[S]:** Yeah. That one sits with you.

**[S]:** How long have you felt like it's going nowhere?

**[P]:** Since they closed the second branch. Two years.

**[S]:** And what's different now, compared to two years ago?

**[P]:** They announced another round of "restructuring" on Monday.

**[S]:** Ah. So it's not just going nowhere, it might actually go.

**[P]:** Yeah. That's it.

**[S]:** Have you tried trading at all yet?

**[P]:** A bit. I've an account with a few hundred in it. I'm down about €150. I read the charts, I get in, and then I panic and close too early, or I don't close and it goes the other way.

**[S]:** So you kind of know what to do, but not when, or how to stick to it.

**[P]:** Exactly. I'd say I need someone to tell me I'm doing it right.

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is it to you to have something going before the restructuring hits?

**[P]:** 8.

**[S]:** What makes it an 8?

**[P]:** Because if I'm let go, I don't want to be standing there with nothing. Again.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a 10?

**[P]:** Whether I'm actually any good at it.

**[S]:** Fair. Couple of quick money ones. With the cost of everything, how much would you say you're putting aside a month?

**[P]:** About €400.

**[S]:** Tight month or usual?

**[P]:** Usual.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[P]:** About €9,000.

**[S]:** Okay. Next step would be to set up a Zoom with one of our head coaches, Kev. He'll look at your trades, why you're closing too early, and break down how we'd guide you, so you've got something real going before the restructuring. How does that sound?

**[P]:** Yeah, sounds good, actually.

**[S]:** He's got Tuesday at 6 or Wednesday at 7. Which one?

**[P]:** Wednesday.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 7. Best email?

**[P]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Check it and accept for me now?

**[P]:** Done.

**[S]:** Locked in. Bring your trade history. Talk Wednesday, Paddy.

**[P]:** *(laughs)* Ah, don't you start.

---

## What to notice

- **This is Scott's reframe from the Syndicate call.** "Trading's repetitive too… so knowing that, why trading?" One line, and "boring" stops working. He had to find the real reason: "going nowhere".
- **"What do you mean?" on the new word, once.** Not "how so? how come? examples?".
- **"Still at the bank, Paddy."** Repeat their line. The pain is often a sentence someone else says to them.
- **Roadblock = guidance.** "You kind of know what to do, but not when, or how to stick to it." The setter said it back and he agreed.
- **Using his nickname at the end** gets a laugh, because the setter listened.
