## From Flavius ([F]), only where Atefur has nothing
- "What keeps you busy right now?" (a softer way to learn what someone does)
- "I'm super curious, how has that been on you?"
- "I'm not assuming anything, by the way" before a personal question
- The friend feel: talk like you're calling a friend and you want to help

- "Tell them what they want to hear" and the "perfect replica of our best students" pitch (#1). That means saying things you can't back.
- "Emotionally manipulated them enough" (#3). Not a method this folder uses.
- Flavius: hard-qualifying with a price ("high four figures"), "from 1 to 10 how committed", and handling limiting beliefs. They're closer moves, or Atefur said not to (#3, #4).
