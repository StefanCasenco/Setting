# 18 · "I know ecom. I've done a lot of research."

**Offer:** ecom · **Real length:** about 5 minutes · **Ends:** qualified

## The call

[SETTER]: Alright, so tell me man, what had you interested in ecom in the first place?

[PROSPECT]: Look, I'll be honest. I know ecom. I've done a lot of research. I understand the model: product research, Meta ads, the whole thing. I just haven't pulled the trigger, if that makes sense. I'm not a beginner.

[SETTER]: Makes sense. What's been stopping you from pulling the trigger?

[PROSPECT]: Honestly? Time. And a bit of, yeah. I work six days a week. Dubai. I'm a hotel supervisor. Long shifts, on my feet.

[SETTER]: Six days. What are the hours like?

[PROSPECT]: Usually ten to twelve. Sometimes more if there's an event. The pay is okay, nine thousand dirhams, but everybody in Dubai has a side thing.

[SETTER]: Everybody has a side thing?

[PROSPECT]: Yes. My colleague has a car rental thing. My cousin has a shop on Noon. My younger brother, he's twenty-eight, he just opened a coffee place. And I'm the older one. I'm thirty-four. Still in the hotel.

*≈ 1:30*

[SETTER]: How's that sitting with you?

[PROSPECT]: It's fine. It's not a competition. But my father asks. At the family dinner. He doesn't say it to hurt me. He says, "So, Omar, what are you building?" And everybody looks at their plate.

[SETTER]: What do you say?

[PROSPECT]: I say, "I'm working on something." And I change the subject. And I've been saying that for two years.

[SETTER]: Two years of "something."

[PROSPECT]: *(laughs)* Yes. I know how it sounds.

[SETTER]: It sounds like it's getting heavy.

[PROSPECT]: It is, a bit. I'm not ashamed of the hotel. I've done well there. But I'm the older brother. I'm supposed to lead. And my brother is leading. Sorry, I need to take this one second. *(muffled, Arabic)* Okay. Sorry. A colleague.

[SETTER]: Does your brother know you feel that way?

[PROSPECT]: No, no. He thinks I'm the successful one. He still calls me for advice. Which is funny, because I don't have any.

[SETTER]: *(laughs)* What does he ask you?

[PROSPECT]: How to deal with staff. And I give him advice and he does it and it works. So now he's the one with the business and I'm the one with the advice. *(laughs)*

*≈ 3:00*

[SETTER]: No problem. What have you tested, in the two years?

[PROSPECT]: I bought a course. And I tested a phone accessory. I put about two thousand dirhams into ads. A few clicks, no sales. I stopped.

[SETTER]: What did you take from that?

[PROSPECT]: That I don't know something. I don't know what. Maybe the product, maybe the ad. I don't have anyone who can look at it.

[SETTER]: And what's making you look for someone now?

[PROSPECT]: My brother's coffee place opened last month, and my father cut the ribbon. He was so proud. And I was proud too. But I went home and I thought, enough. I can't keep saying "I'm working on something."

[SETTER]: Yeah. That's a real moment.

*≈ 4:15*

[SETTER]: Let's talk numbers a bit. That fine?

[PROSPECT]: Of course.

[SETTER]: What would you want ecom to be making each month, a year from now?

[PROSPECT]: Twenty-five thousand dirhams. Then I could think about leaving the hotel.

[SETTER]: And what's coming in at the moment?

[PROSPECT]: Nine thousand, salary. Plus tips, maybe two thousand more.

[SETTER]: How much of that do you save?

[PROSPECT]: Four thousand. Four and a half. I live with my parents, so that helps.

[SETTER]: And in total, how much have you got saved?

[PROSPECT]: Why do you need that?

[SETTER]: I ask everyone. It tells me how much room you've got to work with. Just a ballpark.

[PROSPECT]: About forty thousand dirhams. It's my money. I've saved it for years.

[SETTER]: Is that all available, or is some of it committed?

[PROSPECT]: Yes. I'd consider twenty or so for this. The rest stays.

[SETTER]: Good. That helps, man.

## Where the framework landed
- **Intent** came with a status wall ("I'm not a beginner"). I agreed and asked a plain question. No argument.
- **The job and the hours** came from "what's been stopping you," so they fit.
- **The scene** was the family dinner, and it came from "how's that sitting with you?"
- **No recap.** "Two years of something" was the heart of it.
- **Timeline and why now** came as two plain questions, and the ribbon cutting answered the second.
- **The money part was the ladder,** and he'd decided what he'd put in before I asked.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"I know ecom. I'm not a beginner."** The wall again. I didn't argue or test him.
- **"Everybody in Dubai has a side thing."** A group pressure he can see from outside.
- **"So, Omar, what are you building?" And everybody looks at their plate.** The scene. It's what happens at the table.
- **"I've been saying that for two years."** The cost, in his own voice, with a laugh.
- **"I'm the older brother. I'm supposed to lead."** What stings. It isn't money.

## Why those questions and not the defaults
- **"What's been stopping you from pulling the trigger?"** is a respectful question to a man who just told me he knows his stuff.
- **"Everybody has a side thing?"** repeated his phrase, and he told me about the people.
- **"What do you say?"** is a scene, asked right after the dinner line.
- **I didn't test his knowledge.** Not one question about ads or margins. The advisor will find out what he knows.
