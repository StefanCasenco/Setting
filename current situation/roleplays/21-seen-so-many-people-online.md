# 21 · "I've seen so many people online make money from trading, I wanna do the same."

**Offer:** trading · **Real length:** about 6 minutes · **Ends:** qualified

## The call

[SETTER]: Alright, so what was the main intent behind you wanting to get into trading?

[PROSPECT]: Yeah man, I've seen so many people online make money from trading, and I wanna do the same. Like, it's everywhere. Every time I open Instagram it's some guy in a nice car doing it from his phone. And I'm like, why not me?

[SETTER]: Ha, yeah, it's everywhere. Who's the guy you watch the most?

[PROSPECT]: Uh, there's a few. There's this one, I don't even know his name, he's got a hat and a beach. He posts his profits every day. I watch him every morning.

[SETTER]: Every morning, huh. What's the first thing you think when you watch?

[PROSPECT]: That I'm getting ready for work and he's on a beach. *(laughs)* I'm serious. I'm brushing my teeth, watching a dude on a beach.

[SETTER]: Ha. What are you getting ready for?

[PROSPECT]: Work. I work at a phone store. Sales, on commission. I'm on my feet nine hours, I upsell insurance. It's fine.

[SETTER]: Is it fine?

[PROSPECT]: It's fine. I mean the pay's up and down. Some months I make thirty-eight hundred, some months twenty-two. And my manager's on me about the numbers. He puts a leaderboard on the wall. I'm third from the bottom this month.

[SETTER]: Third from the bottom. How's that?

[PROSPECT]: *(laughs)* Not great. I'm not bad at it. It's just the same thing every day. You hit a number and then they raise the number.

[SETTER]: They raise the number.

[PROSPECT]: Every quarter. And if you miss it, you're talking to HR. Hold on, my girlfriend's calling. I'll text her. Okay.

[SETTER]: You good?

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. Where was I.

[SETTER]: The number going up every quarter.

[PROSPECT]: Right. Yeah. So I'm always kind of waiting for it. Like, what's the number going to be next time.

[SETTER]: What's that like, living with that?

[PROSPECT]: It's stressful, honestly. In August I was short on rent. Like two hundred bucks. I had to ask my girlfriend. She paid it. She never said anything about it, but I know she thinks about it.

[SETTER]: You think she does?

[PROSPECT]: I would. And I hate that. I hate that I had to ask. I'm twenty-three, I've got a job, I should be okay.

[SETTER]: Yeah.

*≈ 3:00*

[PROSPECT]: Anyway. That's why the beach guy, you know? He's just. He doesn't have a number.

[SETTER]: So it's less the car, more not having a number over you.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. Yeah. That's actually it. I don't even care about the car.

[SETTER]: Have you tried any of it yourself?

[PROSPECT]: A bit. I downloaded one of those apps and put in a hundred bucks. I lost like sixty. I was clicking on whatever the guy on Instagram said.

[SETTER]: Mm.

[PROSPECT]: And then I thought, okay, this is not how he does it. He must know something.

[SETTER]: Can I make money like him? Is that what you're wondering?

[PROSPECT]: Honestly? Yeah.

[SETTER]: I can't promise you that, man. I don't know what he does or what he really makes. What I can do is find out where you're at, and if it makes sense, get you in front of someone who can go through it properly. Fair?

[SETTER]: Out loud.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. I didn't really say anything to anyone after that.

[PROSPECT]: Honestly, since August, not a lot. Maybe a hundred.

## Where the framework landed
- **Intent** gave me a social-proof answer, not a goal. The setter didn't challenge it. He asked who he's watching.
- **The beach guy was the way in.** "What's the first thing you think when you watch?" is the question that turned a picture into his actual day.
- **The job came out of the story,** not from "what do you do?"
- **Recap was skipped,** replaced by a short guess: "less the car, more not having a number over you."
- **Why now** came as a casual "what made you reach out this month?", with the Friday meeting as the answer.
- **The "can I make money like him?" question** got an honest answer: no promise.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"Why not me?"** The comparison behind the whole call.
- **"I'm brushing my teeth, watching a dude on a beach."** A scene about his morning, not about trading. It's the real subject.
- **"Third from the bottom."** The leaderboard, which is a daily humiliation he mentioned in passing.
- **"She never said anything about it, but I know she thinks about it."** The August rent, and the real cost.
- **"He doesn't have a number."** What the beach guy represents.
- **"I don't even care about the car."** The surface goal is not the goal.

## Why those questions and not the defaults
- **"Who's the guy you watch the most?"** instead of "what do you do for work?" He brought up a person, so I asked about the person.
- **"What's the first thing you think when you watch?"** moved from the guy to him in one step.
- **"What are you getting ready for?"** was the lightest possible way to get to the job, using his sentence.
- **"Can I make money like him?"** He didn't ask it out loud. I said it for him, and answered it plainly, because it was the thing underneath.
