# 03 · "More time with my kids, mostly."

**Offer:** trading · **Real length:** about 6½ minutes · **Ends:** qualified

## The call

[SETTER]: Okay, so what was the main intent behind you wanting to get into trading?

[PROSPECT]: More time with my kids, mostly. And the money also, of course, but mostly the time. I work a lot of hours.

[SETTER]: How many are we talking?

[PROSPECT]: I drive a taxi. In Lisbon. Twelve, fourteen hours a day, six days. Sometimes seven if I'm behind on the month.

[SETTER]: Wow. And how old are your kids?

[PROSPECT]: Nine and five. The small one, she is asleep when I leave and asleep when I come back. I see her on Sundays. On Sundays I am so tired I sleep on the couch while she plays. She just plays next to me.

[SETTER]: Mm.

[PROSPECT]: And my wife doesn't say anything. She is very patient. But it is not, you know. It is not the life I imagined.

*≈ 1:15*

[SETTER]: What did you imagine?

[PROSPECT]: When I got married? That I would, I don't know. Have dinner. Be there. My father drove a bus for thirty years and I saw him on Sundays also. I said I would not do it like this.

[SETTER]: And here you are.

[PROSPECT]: And here I am. *(laughs)* Yes.

[SETTER]: Is the money okay, at least?

[PROSPECT]: Okay. It's okay. Not great. In summer it is good, in winter it is slow. When the month is tight, I drive more. So I see them less.

[SETTER]: Mm. A circle.

[PROSPECT]: Exactly this. When money is tight I drive more, and when I drive more I do not see them, and when I do not see them I feel terrible. Then I drive more to feel like I am doing something.

[SETTER]: Do you talk to your wife about that last part?

[PROSPECT]: No, no. I am the man. I will not cry to my wife about being tired.

[SETTER]: Who do you talk to?

[PROSPECT]: The radio. *(laughs)* Nobody. It is fine.

[SETTER]: Does your friend, the one who trades, know how tired you are?

[PROSPECT]: No. He thinks I am a happy driver. I smile. People like me, I get good tips because I talk. But it is a job I do with a face.

[SETTER]: A face.

[PROSPECT]: Yes. Passenger gets in, I am "Boa tarde, where to?" Smiling. Then at the red light I am, I don't know. Switched off. Nobody sees this part.

*≈ 3:00*

[SETTER]: So where did trading come from?

[PROSPECT]: A guy I drive. He showed me on his phone. He says he makes in a week what I make in a month. I don't know if it's true. That was maybe a year ago.

[SETTER]: Have you tried anything yourself?

[PROSPECT]: I opened an account. I put in two hundred euros and I lost it in one day. I did not know what I was doing. Then I watched videos, but I only have time when I am waiting for a passenger. Five minutes here and there. I do not learn like that.

[SETTER]: Yeah. That's hard to learn from.

[PROSPECT]: Yes.

[SETTER]: What got you to book the call, though? A year is a while.

[PROSPECT]: Honestly? Last week my son, Tiago, he has a school project, "My Family." And my wife shows me. He wrote, "My dad works in the car." Just that. Not "my dad is a taxi driver." Just "works in the car." I read it three times in the street outside our house.

[SETTER]: *(pause)* That would stay with me.

[PROSPECT]: Yes. It does.

*≈ 4:30*

[SETTER]: Okay, man. Let me ask you about the money side, so I know where you're at. What are we looking to make from trading each month, a year from now?

[PROSPECT]: If I could make three thousand a month from trading, I would drive part-time. Three days. Maybe four.

[SETTER]: Okay, nice. And how far are we from that right now, in terms of your current monthly income?

[PROSPECT]: Around two thousand five hundred. Net. In a good month.

[SETTER]: Cool, so you know how expenses are nowadays, the way the economy is going, how much are you actually able to save from that every month?

[PROSPECT]: Four hundred, maybe five. In winter, zero sometimes.

[SETTER]: Okay. And I guess in total, how much have you been able to save?

[PROSPECT]: Why do you ask this? This is personal.

[SETTER]: Yeah man, but I'm sure you know, in trading it takes money to make money, right? So talk to me. How much have you saved?

[PROSPECT]: Okay. Okay. About six thousand. It is in the bank. It was for the car, for a new one. The taxi is old.

[SETTER]: Is that your car money, or is it free to use?

[PROSPECT]: The car I could wait for. If this works, I don't need the car. So I think, it is mine to use. I'd want to talk about it.

[SETTER]: That's fair. That's what the next call is for. Okay, that helps, man.

---

## Where the framework landed
- **Intent** gave me "time," right away, and the number of hours came as a plain fact.
- **There was no early-impact line.** He was already in it.
- **The recap was dropped.** The "circle" and the school sentence said it better than I could.
- **Why now** was asked as "what got you to book the call," and he answered with the school project.
- **The money ladder is word for word the version you wrote**, including the "why do you ask this" pushback and your answer to it.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"She just plays next to me."** The Sunday scene, offered without a question.
- **"My father drove a bus for thirty years."** The thing he promised himself he wouldn't repeat.
- **"It's a circle."** His shape for the problem. I said the word once, and he explained all of it.
- **"I will not cry to my wife about being tired."** The cost: no one to say it to.
- **"My dad works in the car."** His son's sentence. I let it sit and said one line.

## Why those questions and not the defaults
- **"How many are we talking?"** is a fact, but it followed his own mention of working a lot, so it kept the thread.
- **"How old are your kids?"** moved us from hours to people.
- **"What did you imagine?"** went to the gap between what he planned and what he has.
- **"Who do you talk to?"** came from "I am the man." It's the question under that sentence.
- **I asked if the car money was free** instead of assuming. He said it was, and he'd want to talk about it.
