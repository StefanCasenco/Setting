## Where the framework landed
- **Intent** told me almost nothing. A joke, then "bit of both."
- **I tried three doors:** the job, the flat, the TikTok guy. Each came back as "fine."
- **No recap, no timeline pressure, no money questions.** There was no reason to qualify someone I'm not going to book.
- **The call ended with a plain "there's nothing pushing you."** That's the honest summary, and he agreed.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"I'm not like, desperate."** and **"bit of both."** Low intent, said honestly. I didn't know that when I dialled.
- **"No stress. At two in the afternoon."** What the TikTok guy meant to him. An image, not a pain.
- **"I'm twenty-two, I'm not complaining."** He isn't hiding anything. He just isn't hurting.
- **"I haven't got a plan."** The honest end of the road.

## Why those questions and not the defaults
- **"Is it something you're actually looking into, or more just checking it out?"** is the polite version of the tire-kicker probe. His answer told me how hard to dig.
- **"Do you feel like you've got a lot of stress right now?"** was a direct test of the image he gave me. It wasn't real.
- **I stopped asking for pain at about 3:00.** Digging a happy 22-year-old for pain makes things up. Atefur: surface answers are normal, and don't force it.
- **No money questions.** No reason to book, so no reason to qualify.
