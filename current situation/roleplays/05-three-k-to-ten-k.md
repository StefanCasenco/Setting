# 05 · "I'm at 3k a month and I want 10k."

**Offer:** ecom · **Where it ended:** exit test met

## The call

[SETTER]: What had you interested in ecom in the first place?

[PROSPECT]: I've got a store doing about 3k a month. Revenue, I mean. Want to get it to 10.

[SETTER]: 10k. What are you hoping that gets you that you don't have right now?

[PROSPECT]: Everything. I could quit my job.

[SETTER]: Quit. What do you mean?

[PROSPECT]: I'm an accountant. It's fine. It's just the same every day.

[SETTER]: The same every day.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. Spreadsheets. I'm thirty and I already know what the next thirty years look like.

[SETTER]: What do they look like?

[PROSPECT]: *(laughs)* Promotion, bigger spreadsheet, pension.

[SETTER]: Promotion, bigger spreadsheet, pension. What's the hardest part of seeing it that clearly?

[PROSPECT]: Why does it matter what I feel? I just need the steps.

[SETTER]: Fair. I'm not asking just for the sake of asking. The better I understand you, the better I can help, like a personal trainer would.

[PROSPECT]: Okay. I guess I wanted to do something of my own by now.

[SETTER]: By now.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. I started the store two years ago. Still doing it nights.

[SETTER]: Nights. What's that like?

[PROSPECT]: Tiring. I'm at it till one, then up at seven.

[SETTER]: Got it, man.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"Revenue, I mean."** He corrected himself without being asked. He knows 3k isn't profit. That's for the money section later; I do not challenge it here.
- **"I could quit my job."** He gave me his own next topic. The job is now in the conversation because he put it there.
- **"It's fine. It's just the same every day."** A flat word ("fine") followed by a real one ("same"). Go for the real one.
- **"Promotion, bigger spreadsheet, pension."** He said it with a laugh. A laugh that's covering something.
- **"Why does it matter what I feel? I just need the steps."** He's a logic person. He isn't hostile. He needs a reason to go personal.

## Why I asked what I asked
- **"What are you hoping that gets you that you don't have right now?"** instead of "how much are you making now?" He volunteered the number. Asking what it gets him that he doesn't have now keeps it about the present, and the number tells me nothing yet.
- **"Quit. What do you mean?"** I followed the word he used, not the default job questions.
- **"What do they look like?"** is a scene, asked of the thing he said ("I know what the next thirty years look like").
- **The reason line** is exactly what Atefur gave for this moment. I said it flat and kept going.
- **I stopped at "nights... tiring."** I had the pain, a feeling and a cost.

## Where it could have gone wrong
- Going into numbers: "what's the profit, what's your margin, what's your ad spend?" He'd have loved that conversation, and we'd have stayed in logic.
- "How do you feel about the job?" He already told me: "fine." That question would have got "fine" again.

## Exit test
- **Pain, in his words:** "same every day… I already know what the next thirty years look like."
- **Feeling:** he wanted to have done something of his own by now.
- **Cost:** two years of nights, up at one, up again at seven.
