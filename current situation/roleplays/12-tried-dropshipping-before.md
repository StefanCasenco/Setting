# 12 · "I've tried dropshipping before. It didn't really work."

**Offer:** ecom · **Real length:** about 6 minutes · **Ends:** qualified, partner flagged

## The call

[SETTER]: Alright, so tell me man, what had you interested in ecom in the first place?

[PROSPECT]: Ah, it's not the first time. I tried dropshipping before, like two years ago. It didn't really work. Lost some money. So I'm a bit, I don't know. Back to it, I suppose.

[SETTER]: What happened with that?

[PROSPECT]: I spent about nine hundred on ads. I got maybe four orders. Sent them out, and two of them wanted refunds. I think I made fifty quid total. After nine hundred.

[SETTER]: Ouch.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. And I'd told my girlfriend, "I've got this, it's going to work." And then I had to tell her it hadn't. That was the worst bit.

*≈ 1:00*

[SETTER]: What did she say?

[PROSPECT]: She was fine about it. She said it happens. But she's careful with money, and that was money we were putting together for a holiday. We didn't go.

[SETTER]: Do you think she'd be up for you trying again?

[PROSPECT]: I haven't asked. That's the thing. I've been thinking about it for like three weeks and I haven't said anything.

[SETTER]: What's the hold-up with saying it?

[PROSPECT]: I don't want to be the guy who keeps coming back with an idea. "This time it's different." You know?

[SETTER]: Yeah. So what's different this time?

[PROSPECT]: Honestly? I think the first time I just copied a YouTube guy. Found a product, put it on a store, ran ads. I didn't know what makes it work. I know more now, I think. I just need someone to say if I'm right.

*≈ 2:30*

[SETTER]: What do you do at the moment?

[PROSPECT]: I work in a warehouse. Glasgow. Nights a lot of the time. It's okay. It's not going anywhere.

[SETTER]: Not going anywhere how?

[PROSPECT]: It's twelve-eighty an hour. There's a supervisor job that might open up. But that's another twenty pence. I'm twenty-five. I don't want to be doing this at forty.

[SETTER]: What would you want to be doing at forty?

[PROSPECT]: I don't even know. Not this. Something I picked.

[SETTER]: Do you think about it on shift?

[PROSPECT]: All the time. I'm stacking pallets and doing numbers in my head. Like, if I got one product that did fifty orders a week. I'm on the forklift going, "what if I'm not here in a year."

[SETTER]: And then?

[PROSPECT]: And then my supervisor shouts that I've missed a pallet. *(laughs)* Brings me back.

*≈ 3:15*

[SETTER]: And it's been two years since that attempt?

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. I've been looking at it again for about a year. Watching, not doing. I haven't spent anything since.

[SETTER]: What's been in the way?

[PROSPECT]: Fear. Honestly. I don't want to do it again and get it wrong.

[SETTER]: Okay man, I know it's been two years, life goes on. But why now? Why's this the time you want to do something about it?

[PROSPECT]: She got a promotion. And she said, out of nowhere, "I'm proud of us." And I thought, I want to be proud too. Like I'd be bringing something.

[SETTER]: Yeah. That's a good reason.

*≈ 4:30*

[SETTER]: Okay, so the money side. Mind if I ask a few things?

[PROSPECT]: Go on.

[SETTER]: What's the goal here? What are we looking to make from ecom every month, a year from now?

[PROSPECT]: Two grand a month profit would be amazing. That would change things.

[SETTER]: Okay, sweet. And how far are we from that goal right now, in terms of your current monthly income?

[PROSPECT]: About fifteen hundred, after tax.

[SETTER]: Cool. So you know how expenses are nowadays, the way the economy is going. How much are you actually able to save from that every month?

[PROSPECT]: Two hundred. Ish.

[SETTER]: Ish. Is it more like one-fifty or two-fifty?

[PROSPECT]: Two hundred, yeah. When I'm good.

[SETTER]: Okay. Two hundred. Is that usually a tight month, or is that your usual?

[PROSPECT]: That's usual.

[SETTER]: And I guess in total, how much have you been able to save?

[PROSPECT]: Why do you ask me that? That's personal.

[SETTER]: Yeah man, but I'm sure you know, with ecom it takes money to make money, right? So talk to me, how much do you have saved?

[PROSPECT]: Fine. About three thousand. I've been building it back up since the ads thing.

[SETTER]: Is that sitting there for anything, or is it yours to use?

[PROSPECT]: It's for the holiday we didn't go on, kind of. Well, I told myself it was. But it's mine. She doesn't touch my savings.

[SETTER]: Okay. Does she know you're thinking about doing this?

[PROSPECT]: Not yet. I'd tell her before I did anything.

[SETTER]: Good. I'd say that matters. Thanks for being open with me, man. I'll make a note of that for the advisor.

---

## Where the framework landed
- **Intent** came with "it's not the first time." That was the real subject.
- **What got in the way** was asked as "what happened with that?" He'd started and failed, so "what got in the way of starting" would have been the wrong question.
- **The job question** came late and fit, because he'd mentioned "something I picked."
- **Why now** used your wording close to word for word, once he'd named the fear.
- **The money part is your ladder** as you wrote it. His "why do you ask me that?" got your answer.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"It's not the first time."** He said it first, before the intent. What he fears most is a repeat.
- **"I made fifty quid. After nine hundred."** He knows the number exactly, which is how much it still bothers him.
- **"I had to tell her it hadn't."** The worst bit, in his words. The loss wasn't money.
- **"I don't want to be the guy who keeps coming back with an idea."** What's actually stopping him from telling her.
- **"I'm proud of us. I want to be proud too."** The why-now. Small and sincere.

## Why those questions and not the defaults
- **"What happened with that?"** is the follow-up for someone who's already tried.
- **"What's the hold-up with saying it?"** went straight at what he hadn't done.
- **"So what's different this time?"** is the pre-handle Atefur described: reinforcing the fear without handling it. His answer ("I copied a YouTube guy") is the best note for the closer.
- **I checked whether his girlfriend knows.** For the closer, and because it's honest.
