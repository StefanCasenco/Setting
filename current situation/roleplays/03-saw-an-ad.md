# 03 · "Idk, saw an ad. Thought I'd see what it was."

**Offer:** trading · **Where it ended:** stopped at L2 on purpose

## The call

[SETTER]: What was the main intent behind you wanting to get into trading?

[PROSPECT]: Idk, saw an ad. Thought I'd see what it was.

[SETTER]: Just to make sure, is this something you're still looking into, or not really?

[PROSPECT]: Kinda. My buddy's been doing it.

[SETTER]: Your buddy. How's he doing with it?

[PROSPECT]: Says he made like two grand last month. Might be lying.

[SETTER]: What did you think when he told you?

[PROSPECT]: Honestly, kinda jealous.

[SETTER]: Jealous. What do you mean?

[PROSPECT]: I don't know. He just bought a car. I'm in the same job since I was nineteen.

[SETTER]: Same job since nineteen. What's that like?

[PROSPECT]: It's whatever. It's fine.

[SETTER]: Fine.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. It's just a job.

[SETTER]: Got it, man.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"Saw an ad, thought I'd see"** is low intent. I didn't know that when I dialled. I only know it now because he said it.
- **"Kinda."** after the probe tells me he's half in. Not a no.
- **"My buddy."** The only real hook in the whole answer. Everything else was shrugs.
- **"Jealous"** is a real feeling, and he said it himself, without being pushed.
- **"It's whatever. It's fine."** is him closing the door. I tried once more with an echo and got the same. Two tries on the surface is the limit.

## Why I asked what I asked
- **The tire-kicker probe** (from the framework's curveballs) came first, because "idk" could mean "nothing to say" or "no intent". The probe separates them in one line.
- **"How's he doing with it?"** instead of "what do you do for work?" He had just given me a person. The person is the way in.
- **"What did you think when he told you?"** moves from the friend to him in one step, without a feelings word.
- **I stopped after "fine."** Pushing would have felt like an interrogation of someone who isn't invested yet.

## Where it could have gone wrong
- Treating him like a pain-rich lead and digging for scenes. He'd have hung up or given nothing.
- The default job chain. "How do you feel about the job?" gets "it's fine" and ends the call.

## Exit test
**Not fully met, and that's okay.** What I have:
- **Pain, in his words:** "same job since I was nineteen", and a friend who just bought a car.
- **Feeling:** jealous.
- **Cost:** not found.

I move on and note it for the closer: *low intent, jealous of a friend's results, same job since nineteen.* The rest of the call (timeline, why now, money) fills in the picture, and it tells me whether this one is worth booking.
