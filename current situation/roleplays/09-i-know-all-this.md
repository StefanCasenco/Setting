# 09 · "I've been trading six years, I know all this."

**Offer:** trading · **Where it ended:** exit test met

## The call

[SETTER]: What was the main intent behind you wanting to get into trading?

[PROSPECT]: I've been trading six years. I know all this already.

[SETTER]: Six years. What's still not where you want it?

[PROSPECT]: Consistency. I should've been funded by now.

[SETTER]: By now.

[PROSPECT]: Yeah. I'm profitable most months. Just not enough to quit.

[SETTER]: Quit. What do you mean?

[PROSPECT]: The day job. I'm an estimator. Fifty hours a week, then I trade after.

[SETTER]: After. What does that look like?

[PROSPECT]: Dinner, then the screen till midnight. Most nights.

[SETTER]: Who's up while you're on the screen?

[PROSPECT]: Look, I didn't book to talk about my family.

[SETTER]: Fair. I'm not asking just for the sake of asking. The better I understand you, the better I can help, like a personal trainer would.

[PROSPECT]: *(pause)* The kids were, when they were younger.

[SETTER]: Were.

[PROSPECT]: They're teenagers now. They don't ask me to come out anymore.

[SETTER]: *(pause)* What's the hardest part of that?

[PROSPECT]: That I was in the next room for most of it.

[SETTER]: Got it, man.

## What I should've picked up on
- **"I know all this already."** is a wall, not a fact. He is protecting his pride. I don't argue and I don't prove anything.
- **"I should've been funded by now."** The pride has a crack in it already. "By now" is a deadline he set, and he's behind.
- **"Profitable most months. Just not enough to quit."** I'm not going to check that. He might be wrong about his own numbers, and it doesn't matter at this point.
- **"I didn't book to talk about my family."** The first real resistance. It's also a sign that I'm close. People don't push back on questions that don't matter.
- **"The kids were, when they were younger."** Past tense. That's the cost.

## Why I asked what I asked
- **"What's still not where you want it?"** doesn't challenge him. It accepts his six years and asks about the gap, which respects his experience and keeps him talking.
- **"By now."** I repeated his phrase and said nothing else. He explained it himself.
- **"Quit. What do you mean?"** gets at the day job, using the word he'd used.
- **"Who's up while you're on the screen?"** The midnight screen is a scene. Asking who else is in it brings out the cost without asking about feelings.
- **The reason line** is what he needed after pushing back. I said it flat. I didn't apologise and I didn't back off.
- **"What's the hardest part of that?"** after the silence.

## Where it could have gone wrong
- Telling him what he's missing or proving that his trading has holes. That turns it into a debate he'll win.
- "Why aren't you funded after six years?" That's the "why aren't you" line. He'd have defended himself for ten minutes.
- Backing off completely when he pushed back. He needed one short reason and a calm tone.

## Exit test
- **Pain, in his words:** "I should've been funded by now… the screen till midnight… I was in the next room for most of it."
- **Feeling:** regret.
- **Cost:** the years his kids were younger.
