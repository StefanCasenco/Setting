# 03 · Following the thread

Each run starts after the intent question. Names are invented. Every setter line is built from the prospect's last words. The bold word in each answer is the one that carried the weight.

Two checks for every turn:
1. **What word carried the weight?**
2. **Am I asking about it, or about my own next topic?**

---

## Time
- **P:** Honestly I just want more **time**.
- **S:** Time for what?
- **P:** My family, mostly. I'm barely there.
- **S:** Barely there. What do you mean?
- **P:** I leave before they wake up. Home when they're asleep.
- **S:** What's the hardest part of that?
- **P:** My daughter calls me by my first name sometimes. As a joke. It doesn't feel like one.

*Stops here.* Pain in his words, a feeling, a cost. Next stop is the recap.

---

## Bills / paycheck to paycheck
- **P:** Money, man. I'm tired of being **broke**.
- **S:** Broke. What do you mean?
- **P:** Paycheck to paycheck. Rent comes out and I'm at zero.
- **S:** What's the end of the month like?
- **P:** I skip things. I tell my girlfriend I'm not hungry.
- **S:** What's the worst part of that?
- **P:** Lying to her. She doesn't know it's that bad.

---

## Out of the job
- **P:** I want out of my **9 to 5**.
- **S:** What makes you want out?
- **P:** Same thing every day. It's like a **cycle**.
- **S:** What makes you use the word cycle?
- **P:** Sunday night I already dread Monday. Then I do it again.
- **S:** What's the hardest part of that?
- **P:** I'm 31 and it feels like this is my whole life.

*Don't ask "why is that a problem"* (#2 flagged it: you get a defence).

---

## Family / providing
- **P:** I just want to **provide** better for my family.
- **S:** Provide better. What do you mean?
- **P:** Two jobs, and we're still behind.
- **S:** How's it at home with it like that?
- **P:** Tense. She's stressed and I'm never there.
- **S:** What's that been doing to the two of you?
- **P:** We don't really talk about it anymore.

---

## Own place / independence
- **P:** I want **my own place**.
- **S:** How's living at home been lately?
- **P:** It's fine. *(short laugh)* It's a bit… yeah.
- **S:** A bit…?
- **P:** Not what I thought I'd be doing at 27.
- **S:** What's the hardest part of that?
- **P:** My girlfriend won't move in there. I get it.

*The laugh and the trailing "a bit" were the door. Echo it, then wait.*

---

## Status / behind
- **P:** I want to make real money. Nice car, travel, the whole thing.
- **S:** What makes you feel like you're not as successful as you want to be right now? (#2)
- **P:** Everyone I know is moving ahead. I'm stuck.
- **S:** Stuck. What do you mean?
- **P:** Five years, same spot. I should be further.
- **S:** What's the hardest part of that?
- **P:** Feeling like I wasted my twenties.

---

## "Stuck" / plateau (already trading)
- **P:** I'm **stuck**. I keep blowing prop accounts.
- **S:** Stuck how?
- **P:** Two good trades, two bad ones, blow it. Over and over.
- **S:** What was the last one like? *(a scene, not the strategy)*
- **P:** Kitchen table, midnight. Everyone asleep.
- **S:** What's it like, going to bed after that?
- **P:** Staring at the ceiling. Then telling my wife.

Don't follow the setups, the risk or the psychology. Follow the kitchen table and the wife. (#3)

---

## Only tactical, with a pivot
- **P:** My **risk management** is the problem.
- **S:** I know you want to get better at trading, but what's the real motivation for even trying it? (#3)
- **P:** To make consistent income.
- **S:** Consistent income for what?
- **P:** To leave the job. I'm there fifty-five hours a week.
- **S:** Fifty-five hours. What does that do to your week?
- **P:** I see my daughter asleep. Weekends only.

---

## Happy with the job
- **P:** My job is fine. I just want more.
- **S:** More of what?
- **P:** More money, more choices.
- **S:** What's the real reason you started looking at this?
- **P:** I'm 38. If I don't build something now, I never will.

Don't ask about the job (#3). Take what they gave you.

---

## What went wrong in each run when it went wrong
- A fact question right after a feeling ("do you trade full time?").
- "Why" as a challenge.
- Asking for an "example".
- Jumping to what life would look like before the pain landed.
- Digging after the exit test.
