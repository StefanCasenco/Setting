# 02 · Start: intent, the first answer, going straight in

## The intent line (pick one per call, don't stack)
- **Trading:** "What was the main intent behind you wanting to get into trading?"
- **Ecom / other:** "What had you interested in ecom in the first place?"
- (Framework PDF version: "Okay, so tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?")

One question gives you their goal. Don't ask the goal a second time. (#2)

## Read the first answer
You're listening for something tangible: **money, time or freedom.** Most answers on bizopp calls will be money.

**Money by itself is not the pain.** It's a shallow tangible. Your next sentence turns it into a reason.

## Early impact (only if it fits)
Use their word:
- "What makes you feel like you don't have **[their word]** right now?"
- "What makes you feel like you're not as **[their word]** as you want to be right now?" (softer; #2)

## The first reply, by what they said

**"Just money, man."**
> "Sure, everyone wants more money. But money's just a tool, right? What's the real reason behind it for you?"

One question. The tool idea is yours; keep it short and end on the question.

**"I want more time."**
> "Time for what?" / "What's taking up your time right now?"

**"Freedom."**
> "Freedom from what?" / "What makes you feel like you don't have that freedom right now?"

**A number: "5k a month."**
> "5k. What are you hoping that gets you that you don't have right now?"

**"To be comfortable" / any soft word.**
> "Comfortable. What do you mean?"

**Lambo / status / "be the guy."**
> "What makes you feel like you're not as successful as you want to be right now?"

**Only tactical: "my risk management", "psychology", "strategy."**
> "I know you want to get better at trading, but what's the real motivation for even trying it?"

**"Idk" / "money."**
> Guess out loud: "Sounds like money's been tight lately?" Or give two options.

**"Just looking, saw an ad."**
> "Just to make sure, is this something you're still looking into, or not really?" Then keep it light.

## The model run: how the thread works
This is the pattern you described. Nothing is planned. Each question is built from the last answer.

- **S:** What had you interested in ecom in the first place?
- **P:** I just want more freedom, man.
- **S:** What makes you feel like you don't have that freedom right now?
- **P:** I've got this job. I never have any time.
- **S:** Never any time. What do you mean?
- **P:** Like I leave at 7 and I'm home at 7. Then I'm done.
- **S:** Home at 7 and you're done. What's the hardest part of that?
- **P:** I miss my kid growing up. She's asleep when I get home.

Four questions, and you have the scene, a feeling and a cost. No "what do you do for work?" was needed, because he told you.

## Getting at work without asking "what do you do for work?"
The job usually comes out on its own. If it doesn't, don't ask it as a fact question. Ask about what they just said:
- "What's taking up your time right now?"
- **[F]** "What keeps you busy right now?"

Only once they've said "my job", a plain "what kind of work?" is fine.
