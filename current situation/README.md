# CURRENT SITUATION

One job: get from **intent** to **their pain**, then hand off to TIMEFRAME.

This folder covers only that stretch of the call. The frame, timeframe, financial qualification, pitch and booking live in the framework PDF in the root of this repo, and they stay fixed. CURRENT is the only part that changes from prospect to prospect, because the conversation can't be scripted. What *can* be fixed is how you listen and how you follow a thread.

## What "done" looks like
You can say their pain **in their words**, with **one feeling** and **one cost**. Then you stop digging.

> "Stuck in the cycle, drained, no time with my daughter."

## The rules of this folder
- **Atefur first.** Flavius is used only where Atefur has nothing on the point, and marked **[F]**.
- **Go straight in.** No five questions to get to the one you want. Take their word and ask about it.
- **Follow the thread.** Their answer is the next question.
- **Nothing here is a script to read.** The lines are examples of a principle. Say them your way.
