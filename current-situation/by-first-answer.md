# By first answer: what they give → what you do

S = setter, P = prospect. Names are invented. Ladders are short on purpose.

| # | They give | Tells you | First move | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vague money | Nothing yet | Motivation question | Accepting "more money" as the goal |
| 2 | Vague freedom | A feeling, no scene | "Freedom from what?" | Defining freedom for them |
| 3 | "Get out of my 9-5" | Escape, maybe about time | Echo + scene | Attacking the job |
| 4 | "I'm stuck / plateaued" | A loaded word | Echo "stuck" | Skipping to the fix |
| 5 | "I blew accounts" | Tactical, maybe shame | Scene of the last blow-up | Talking strategy |
| 6 | Only tactical | Hiding behind method | Real-motivation question | Recapping the tactical goal |
| 7 | "Just curious" | Low intent | Probe once, then light CURRENT | Heavy digging on a tire-kicker |
| 8 | One word | Guarded | Guess out loud | Three open questions in a row |
| 9 | Closed yes/no | Short on words | Two-option question | Interrogating |
| 10 | Talker who dumps | Many threads | Warm interruption, one thread | Following every thread |
| 11 | Loaded drop (divorce, debt) | The real thing | Slow down, one human sentence | Pouncing on it as ammo |
| 12 | Numbers guy | Logic first | Ask what the number buys | Staying in numbers |
| 13 | Happy with job | Not the job | Motivation question | "You must like it then" |
| 14 | Ego / "I know all this" | Defensive | Don't argue; ask what's still not there | Proving them wrong |
| 15 | Skeptic | Burned before | Curveball line, then continue | Over-selling |
| 16 | Price-first | Looking for a number | Taster once, then back to them | Quoting a figure |
| 17 | Time-pressed | Busy | "I've got about five minutes" | Rushing past the pain |
| 18 | Family provider | Duty | Ask about the household scene | Guilt-tripping |
| 19 | Student / young | Little capital | Normal CURRENT; check ladder early-ish | Assuming they can't pay |

## Mini-ladders

**1 Vague money**
S: Sure man, most people want more money. What's the real reason behind it for you?
P: Just… not being broke all the time.
S: Broke all the time. What's that like?
P: I check my balance before I buy lunch.
S: Walk me through the end of the month.
P: Skipping stuff. Telling my girlfriend I'm "not hungry". [L3 → L4]

**2 Vague freedom**
P: Freedom, I guess.
S: Freedom from what?
P: Having to be somewhere at 7.
S: And what happens at 7?
P: Warehouse. Till 6, sometimes 8. Then I'm dead. [L3]

**3 Get out of 9-5**
P: I want out of my 9 to 5.
S: What do you mean?
P: Same thing every day.
S: What's the same?
P: Sit, ticket, ticket, ticket. Home. Repeat. I'm 31 and it's my whole life. [L4]

**4 Stuck**
P: I feel stuck.
S: Stuck how?
P: Like I've been trying for years and nothing moves.
S: What's the hardest part of that?
P: Watching my friends buy houses. [L4]

**5 Blew accounts**
P: I've blown four prop accounts.
S: What happened on the last one?
P: Revenge traded. Lost it in ten minutes.
S: Where were you when that happened?
P: Kitchen at 2 a.m. My wife was asleep. [L3]
S: What's it like, going back to bed after that?
P: Honestly, I lie there. [L4]

**6 Tactical only**
P: My psychology is the problem.
S: I know you want to get better at trading, but what's the real motivation for even trying it?
P: I want to quit my job.
S: What's the job doing to you?
P: Taking all my time. [L2 → L3]

**7 Just curious**
P: Just looking.
S: Just to make sure, is this something you're still looking into, or not really?
P: Kind of. I saw the ad.
S: What caught your eye in it?
P: Working from anywhere. [L2]

**8 One word**
P: Money.
S: Is it more about the money itself, or what it would let you do?
P: Do stuff.
S: Like what, for example, if you had it?  *(use only once pain landed; otherwise:)*
S: Sounds like money's tight right now. Close?
P: Yeah. Super tight. [L2]

**9 Closed**
P: Yeah.
S: Is it more that you want out, or more that you want something of your own?
P: Own thing.
S: Got it. What's in the way of that today?
P: I work 50 hours. [L3]

**10 Talker**
P: (long story: job, ex, loan, dad)
S: Hold on, back up. The part about your dad, what happened there?
P: He was always working. I swore I'd never be that.
S: And now?
P: I'm him. [L4]

**11 Loaded drop**
P: …since the divorce, money's been a mess.
S: Yeah. That sounds rough.
*(wait)*
P: I see my son twice a month. I can't even take him anywhere good.
S: What's the hardest part of that?
P: Him asking why. [L4]

**12 Numbers guy**
P: I make 2k, I want 10k.
S: What would 10k change?
P: Everything, I'd have options.
S: Options like what?
P: Not living with my mom at 29. [L4]

**13 Happy with job**
P: My job is fine, I just want more.
S: More of what?
P: More money, more choices.
S: What's the real reason you started looking at trading?
P: I'm 38. If I don't build something now, I never will. [L4]

**14 Ego**
P: I know all this already.
S: Fair. What's still not where you want it?
P: Consistency.
S: What does the inconsistency cost you?
P: Months. I should've been funded two years ago. [L4]

**15 Skeptic**
P: Is this a scam?
S: Fair worry. This call is just to see where you're at, nothing more. $CLOSER answers the detailed stuff. What made you ask?
P: I paid for a course before. Nothing.
S: What happened with that?
P: Lost 800 and my confidence. [L4]

**16 Price-first**
P: How much is it?
S: It's a four-figure investment, depending on the program, but we can't even know if we can help you yet. That's what the call with $CLOSER is for. So where are you at with trading right now?
P: Barely started.
S: What got you into it? [back to CURRENT]

**17 Time-pressed**
P: I've only got a couple of minutes.
S: I've got about five minutes. Cool?
P: Fine.
S: What's the main thing you're trying to fix?
P: My schedule. I work nights. [L2]

**18 Family provider**
P: I need to provide for my family.
S: What does that look like day to day?
P: Two jobs.
S: What's the hardest part of two jobs?
P: I see my kids asleep. [L4]

**19 Student**
P: I'm in uni, I want to start early.
S: What made you want to start early?
P: My dad's been in the same factory 30 years.
S: What's it like seeing that?
P: I don't want that. [L3]
