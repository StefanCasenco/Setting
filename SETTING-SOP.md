## 1. Your role
You sell the **meeting**, not the product. Bank four things for the closer: **real pain, real urgency (why now), real money, actually serious.** Then book. Honest beats booked.

## 2. The spine (fixed order)
FRAME → INTENT → EARLY IMPACT → CURRENT → RECAP → TIMEFRAME → WHAT GOT IN THE WAY → WHY NOW → FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION → PITCH → SCHEDULE & LOCK → SHOW UP.

- Same order every call. If they already answered a node, confirm it and skip it. Never reorder.
- Only CURRENT is freestyle. Everything else: say the script's words.
- Target: 8-12 minutes live, CURRENT about 4-6.

## 3. How to run CURRENT
Full toolkit in [`current-situation/`](current-situation/README.md).
- Classify what they give: **vague**, **loaded**, **specific**.
- **Vague** → motivation question or echo. **Loaded** → slow down, go in. **Specific** → ask what it buys.
- Layers, not interrogation: echo their word → "what do you mean?" → a scene → personal impact → cost.
- Bridge, don't jump. Use a short echo to move from one thread to the next.
- Silence is a tool.
- Curiosity over persuasion. You are not trying to convince anyone of anything.
- Stop when you have the exit test: pain in their words, one feeling, one cost.

What to emphasise inside CURRENT, by what the prospect gives:

| They give | Spend your time on |
|---|---|
| Money | The real reason behind it, then a scene of the month-end |
| Time | A normal week |
| Freedom | "Freedom from what?" |
| Tactical | The real motivation, never the method |
| Loaded drop | One human sentence, then wait |
| Nothing / one word | Guess out loud, then move on after two tries |

## 4. Confirm back out loud (RECAP)
Restate the **deep** goal (time, freedom, family), not the tactical one. If you only have the tactical one, you're still in CURRENT.

## 5. Money (no price on the call)
- The setter never says a price, a range, or hints that there is a figure to pay.
- Ladder: monthly goal → current income → monthly saving → "it takes money to make money, how much do you have saved?"
- Enough → PITCH. Not enough → DQ line, follow up in a month or two. Threshold: `$MIN_SAVINGS`.
- If asked "how much is it?": use the taster in the script. Never say it's free. Never deny a cost.

## 6. Safety
- No income or outcome claims. No guarantees.
- Never help anyone borrow or risk money they can't spare: credit cards, loans, retirement, emergency funds.
- If money is borrowed, DQ and be kind about it.
- Heavy moment (loss, crisis): stop the framework. One human sentence. Wait. Booking can wait.
- Under 18 or unsupported region: end politely, don't book.
- Never flatter, invent claims ("you're exactly like our best students"), or manufacture pressure.

## 7. Attitude (**MERGE** with v1 table)
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Calm, warm, curious, a bit flat | Hype, therapist voice |
| One question per turn | Stack questions |
| Use their words | Use consultant-speak |
| Let silence sit | Fill it with filler phrases |
| Accept "no" | Chase |

## 8. Disqualify (**MERGE** with v1 table)
| Situation | Move |
|---|---|
| Not enough money | DQ line, resources, follow up in a month or two |
| Borrowed or emergency money | DQ, kind, no suggestions on where to find money |
| Under 18 | End politely |
| Unsupported region | End politely |
| Wants a guarantee | Say you can't promise results. If it's the only thing they want, don't book |
| Not serious / "just curious" | One probe, then let them go |

## 9. Curveballs
| They say | You do |
|---|---|
| "Is this a scam?" | "Fair worry. This call is just to see where you're at, nothing more. $CLOSER answers the detailed stuff." |
| "Just looking / curious" | "Just to make sure, is this something you're still looking into, or not really?" |
| "I need to ask my wife / partner" | Note it for the closer. Don't push. |
| "Send me the info" | "Of course. So I send the right thing, what's the main thing you're trying to fix?" |
| "How much is it?" | The taster in the script, once. Then back to them. |
| "Why are you asking me this?" | "I'm not asking just for the sake of asking. The better I understand you, the better I can help, like a personal trainer would." |
| Emotionally heavy | Stop the framework. One human sentence. Wait. |

## 10. Bridges between nodes
| From → to | Line |
|---|---|
| CURRENT → RECAP | "Got it man." / "Okay, let me make sure I have this right." |
| RECAP → TIMEFRAME | The script line ("Got it. Give me the bigger picture…") |
| TIMEFRAME → WGW | "What happened with that?" if they've tried things |
| WGW → WHY NOW | "What made you start looking now?" |
| WHY NOW → FIN | "Okay, that's helpful." then the first FIN question |
| FIN → PITCH | "Okay, that's helpful." then the script |

## 11. Handoff note (after every call)
Name · offer · lead source · goal in their words + number · the pain (scene, feeling, cost) · timeframe, what they tried, what got in the way, why now · income, monthly savings, amount saved and what it's for · flags (tight money, partner, ghost risk, skepticism) · time zone + booked slot.

## 12. Adapting to another offer (**MERGE** with v1 worksheet)
For a new offer, answer these before the first call:
1. Who is the lead and where did they come from?
2. What are the three or four goal words they use?
3. What's the money ladder (goal, income, savings, capital)?
4. Who is the closer and how long is the next call?
5. What are the DQ rules (money, age, region)?
6. Which curveballs are specific to this offer?

## 13. Modules
**A. Trading.** Common first answers: money, time, freedom, a tactical problem. Blown prop accounts and screen time are normal. Ask about the scene (last blow-up, a normal day), not the strategy. Question bank sections 13-14 in the toolkit.

**B. Ecom.** Mirror the trading lines with "ecom". Common pain: ad spend, thin or no profit, supplier problems, cash flow, a course that didn't work. Ask about orders, ads and the week, then the person behind it. Ladder asks about money saved for investing into ecom.

## 14. Why this method
- A fixed spine is easy to say and easy to review: structure like iron, freestyle only where it counts.
- CURRENT is the whole call. Pain is the thing the closer works with.
- Asking about emotion beats asking about the job: the job might be fine.
- "What do you mean?" gets an answer from the heart; "give me an example" gets a logical one.
- Blame-style "why" questions get defence or self-blame.
- Why they're *still* stuck is the closer's job.
- The setter doesn't quote a price. Money is a question, not a negotiation.
- Real prospects are harder than roleplay ones, so practise on the hard ones.
