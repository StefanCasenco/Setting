# 04 · Your first two weeks on an offer

The goal of weeks 1 and 2 isn't to be the best setter on the team yet. It's to **sound like the offer owner, know the offer cold, and get your numbers moving every day.** The setters who get promoted are the ones who were obviously working hardest in week one (Scott's advice to Charlie on the Syndicate call: be the top setter, so the promotion is a no-brainer).

---

## Before your first dial (days 1–2)

**1. Study the offer owner until you sound like them** (Scott, tonality lesson):
- Watch 10+ of their videos and read their captions. Write down their go-to words and phrases, how formal they are, their slang (US vs UK), and how they talk about the problem their offer solves.
- Your phone tone stays *you*: familiar, curious, empathetic, assumptive. But use *their* words for the problem and the result. Leads have watched this person for weeks, so it should feel familiar.

**2. Know the offer cold.** Write one page, in your own words:
- Who it's for and who it's **not** for (your DQ list)
- What problem it solves, in the words their ads use
- Price range *(for you only, never to say on the call)*
- The closer's name, how they run their calls, and what they want flagged
- Is it a **capital** offer or a **program** offer? (Which money questions do you use? See `phone calls 2/00-read-first.md`, section 4.)

**3. Get every document:** script, objection doc, CRM guide, the booking link, and the reminder process.

**4. Listen to the best calls.** Ask for the top setter's three best recorded calls and the closer's three best closes. Listen for how *this* offer's leads talk, and for what the closer needs from you.

**5. Shadow the top setter's dials for an hour**, if you can.

## Every day, weeks 1–2

| Block | What |
|---|---|
| **Before dialling** (30 min) | Read your opener and money questions out loud 5 times (Atefur: read it out loud until it's muscle memory). Read yesterday's notes. |
| **Speed to lead** | New leads get called **within 5 minutes**, always. Calls 048 and 029 show why: the emotion is still live. |
| **Dial blocks** | Two or three focused blocks. Phone only, no scrolling. |
| **After each booked call** | Write the closer note straight away (template below). |
| **End of day** (15 min) | Fill in your numbers. Score one call with the scorecard from `01`. Write down one thing to fix tomorrow. |

**Time zone (US offers):** US Eastern is about 7 hours behind Moldova, and US Pacific about 10. Lead hours are roughly 3pm–midnight or 2am your time. Build your sleep around it from day one. Atefur: *"You get used to it after a week."* Block your mornings for sleep, gym and study, and protect them.

## Track your numbers (make a sheet)

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Dials | Effort |
| Conversations | How many leads you actually reach |
| Booked | How good your discovery and pitch are |
| Showed | How good your lock-in and reminders are |
| Closed | How qualified your bookings are |

**Watch the ratios, not just the totals:**
- Lots of conversations but few bookings → your discovery or pitch needs work (habits 3, 6 and 9 in `01`).
- Lots of bookings but few shows → work on the lock-in: invite accepted on the call, clash check, reminder texts, partner on the call.
- Lots of shows but few closes → you're booking people who aren't qualified. Disqualify more (habit 10), and write better closer notes.

Share your numbers with your manager every day without being asked. Nobody else does that, so you'll stand out.

## Closer note template (after every booking)

> **[Name], [age], [city], [job]**
> **Lead:** [source, e.g. app / webinar / old lead]
> **Pain (their words):** "[the line]"
> **Goal:** [number] → [what it means to them, in their words]
> **Roadblock (their words):** "[...]"
> **Why now:** [event / date]
> **Temp check:** [number], held back by "[...]"
> **Money:** [income] / [monthly save, usual or tight] / [total saved] / [credit Y/N] / [off-limits money, e.g. 401k, baby fund]
> **Decision maker:** [alone / with spouse, and if so, are they on the call?]
> **Flags:** [burnt before? sceptical spouse? price asked x times? medical / anything sensitive?]

The closer will love you for this. It's also how you learn: every flag you write is a question you remembered to ask.

## Reminders (cutting no-shows)

- **Accept the invite on the call.** Always.
- **Text right after:** "Hey [name], Stefan from [owner]'s team. You're locked in for [day] at [time] with [closer]. Link's in your email. Any issues, just text me here."
- **Text the evening before**, and **call or text 1–2 hours before.** Kevin's line works well here: *"For no reason whatsoever you won't be at the meeting?"*
- **No-show:** call within 10 minutes, with no guilt. *"Hey, just making sure you're good. What happened?"* (calls 013 and 056 in `phone calls 2`)

## Week 2 check-in with your manager

Ask for 15 minutes and bring your numbers. Ask:
1. "What's one thing I should do more of, and one thing I should stop?"
2. "Can you review one of my calls this week?"
3. "What separates your top setter from everyone else?"

Then do exactly what they say for week 3, and show them the result.
