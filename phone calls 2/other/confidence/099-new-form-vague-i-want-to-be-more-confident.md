**Prospect:** Mike, 30, IT support at a university, Columbus · **Lead:** form filled 20 minutes ago · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Mike, how's it going? It's Stefan from Ellis's team. You just put your info in. What made you fill it out?

**[M]:** I just want to be more confident.

**[S]:** More confident where?

**[M]:** I dunno. Everywhere?

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Fair. What do you do for work?

**[M]:** IT support. At Ohio State.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** When was the last time you felt not confident? Like, a specific moment.

**[M]:** *(pause)* …Yesterday, actually.

**[S]:** What happened yesterday?

**[M]:** There was a meeting. About a new system. And I had an idea, like a really good one, that would've saved us a ton of time. And I didn't say it. And then the guy next to me said basically the same thing ten minutes later, and everyone loved it.

**[S]:** He said your idea.

**[M]:** Basically my idea. And I just sat there.

**[S]:** How did that feel, sitting there?

**[M]:** *(pause)* Like a coward, honestly. That's the word that came to my head.

**[S]:** "A coward."

**[M]:** Yeah. It's not the first time. It happens all the time. And he got asked to lead the project. I've been there longer than him.

**[S]:** How long has it been happening like that?

**[M]:** My whole career, kind of. Eight years. I'm the guy who knows the answer and doesn't say it.

**[S]:** "The guy who knows the answer and doesn't say it." Is it just work, or outside too?

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Outside too. There's a girl at my climbing gym I've wanted to ask out for six months. Six months. I say "hey" and that's it.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be in six months?

**[M]:** Saying the idea. In the meeting. Out loud. And maybe asking her out. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[M]:** That I stop feeling like a coward. *(pause)* And that people actually know what I can do.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's been stopping you from saying it, in those moments?

**[M]:** I overthink. What if it's wrong? What if they laugh? By the time I've decided it's okay, someone else said it.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[M]:** Someone to work with on it. Like, practice, and figure out why I freeze. Books haven't worked. I've read like six.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[M]:** Seven.

**[S]:** What makes it a seven?

**[M]:** Yesterday. Him getting the project.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a nine?

**[M]:** It feels kind of… silly. Paying someone to help me talk. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Is it silly that a guy who's been there longer got your project with your idea?

**[M]:** *(pause)* …No. Okay. That's not silly.

## What to notice

- **"More confident everywhere" → "When was the last time you felt not confident? A specific moment."** Asking for a specific recent moment is the fastest cure for a vague goal.
- **"He said your idea."** Short and curious. Then "How did that feel?" got "like a coward", which is his word, and the North Star.
- **"Is it just work, or outside too?"** One question found the second thread (the climbing gym).
- **Embarrassment about paying ("silly"):** "Is it silly that a guy got your project with your idea?" Use his own pain to answer it.
- **"Accept it now, before you overthink it."** Using his habit as a light joke at the end.
