# 100 · Confidence coaching · Old lead, 3 months · Social anxiety, sees a therapist: fit check done with care

**Prospect:** Sarah, 27, graphic designer, works from home, Portland · **Lead:** downloaded a "social confidence" guide in August · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked, alongside her therapy and not instead of it

> **Know where coaching ends.** If someone mentions a mental health diagnosis, medication or therapy, coaching can sometimes work *alongside* it, never instead of it. The setter doesn't decide what's clinically right. Ask whether her therapist knows, and flag it for the coach. If someone mentions self-harm or a crisis, stop the sales call and point them to their therapist or a crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).

## What to notice

- **She brings up a diagnosis and a therapist: slow down and thank her.** Then ask, "Would they know you're looking at coaching?" The setter doesn't decide what's clinically right.
- **"Our coaches aren't therapists, anything clinical stays with her."** She said, "That's what I wanted to hear." Clear boundaries build trust.
- **Alongside, never instead of.** Say it in the pitch and put it in the notes for the coach.
- **"You sat outside for twenty minutes."** Repeat the moment quietly. Empathetic tone, no fixing.
- **If anyone mentions a crisis or self-harm, stop selling** and point them to their therapist or 988. That matters more than any booking.
