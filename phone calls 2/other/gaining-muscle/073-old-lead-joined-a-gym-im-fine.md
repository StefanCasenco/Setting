# 073 · Gaining muscle · Old lead, 2 months · "I joined a gym already, I'm good"

**Prospect:** Sam, 28, delivery driver for Amazon, Sacramento · **Lead:** downloaded the free 4-week plan in September · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Sam, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Dan's team. In September you grabbed our free 4-week plan. I'm just checking in. Did you give it a go?

**[SA]:** Oh, yeah, I did the first week. *(laughs)* Then I joined a gym. I'm good now, honestly.

**[S]:** Nice, good for you. Which gym?

**[SA]:** 24 Hour Fitness, by my place.

**[S]:** How's it going, two months in?

**[SA]:** Good. I go like three times a week. Well, I did. Last couple weeks it's been twice. Work's been nuts.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Nuts how?

**[SA]:** I'm an Amazon driver. Peak season. Ten-hour routes.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Ten-hour routes and still getting to the gym twice. That's not bad, honestly.

**[SA]:** *(laughs)* I guess.

**[S]:** When you grabbed the plan in September, what was the goal?

**[SA]:** Just look better. I let myself go. I played football in high school, I was in shape. Then I stopped and just… you know. Fast food in the van.

**[S]:** Fast food in the van.

**[SA]:** Every day, basically. McDonald's at lunch. I don't have time to stop.

**[S]:** And since joining the gym, have you seen a difference?

**[SA]:** *(pause)* Not really. Same weight. Maybe a little stronger.

**[S]:** What bugs you most about it?

**[SA]:** I used to be the fit one. On the football team. Now my old teammates post pics and I'm like… I don't post pics. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** "I used to be the fit one."

**[SA]:** Yeah. It's kind of an identity thing.

**[S]:** How long have you felt like that?

**[SA]:** Since my reunion. Last year. Ten years. Everybody looked the same except me.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be in six months?

**[SA]:** Down like twenty pounds. With some muscle. Look like I played football again.

**[S]:** What would that mean for you?

**[SA]:** Feeling like me again. Honestly. Not dreading photos.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** And Sam, I'm not here to tell you to quit 24 Hour, it's great that you're going. What do you think's been stopping the results, with the gym and everything?

**[SA]:** The food, probably. And I don't really know what I'm doing in there. I just do a bit of everything.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[SA]:** A plan that works around ten-hour routes. And food that isn't McDonald's.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[SA]:** Seven.

**[S]:** What makes it a seven?

**[SA]:** The reunion pics. I'm still thinking about them.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a nine?

**[SA]:** Time. I'm in the van ten hours.

**[S]:** If it's just time, why not just keep going to 24 Hour and figure it out yourself?

**[SA]:** *(pause)* …'Cause I've been doing that for two months and nothing changed. I need someone who gets my schedule.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. What are you spending now: gym, food, all of it?

**[SA]:** Gym's thirty. Food… probably two hundred a month on McDonald's alone. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's a lot of McDonald's. And what's coming in?

**[SA]:** About thirty-two hundred.

**[S]:** And savings?

**[SA]:** About three K.

**[S]:** And if Tom finds the right fit, is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do?

**[SA]:** Yeah, if it's not some crazy diet.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you used to be the fit one on the football team, and at your ten-year reunion everybody looked the same except you. You're doing ten-hour routes on McDonald's, you've been at 24 Hour two months, and the weight hasn't moved. You want twenty pounds off with some muscle so you feel like you again. What you said you need is a plan that works around ten-hour routes and food that isn't McDonald's. Next step would be a Zoom with Jack, one of Dan's coaches. He works with shift guys all the time, and you keep 24 Hour. When's a lighter day?

**[SA]:** Sunday.

**[S]:** Sunday at 11 or 3?

**[SA]:** 11.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[SA]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. And Sam, maybe one less McDonald's before Sunday. *(laughs)*

**[SA]:** *(laughs)* No promises.

## What to notice

- **"I joined a gym, I'm good."** "Nice, good for you." Then curiosity: "How's it going, two months in?" He told you himself it isn't working.
- **A compliment before the hard question.** "Ten-hour routes and still twice a week, that's not bad." It keeps him open.
- **"I used to be the fit one."** An identity pain. Repeat it once.
- **"I'm not here to tell you to quit 24 Hour."** Respect what they already chose.
- **Atefur's time move:** "Why not keep going and figure it out yourself?" He says why he needs help: "I've done that two months and nothing changed."
