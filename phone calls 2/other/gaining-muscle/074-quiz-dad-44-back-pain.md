## What to notice

- **"My wife sent it to me. Subtle, right?"** Laugh with it, then ask what it said. That's an easy way in.
- **"Scared to lift stuff. How does that show up day to day?"** It turns a physical problem into his life (his son).
- **"He goes to his mom." Pause.** Repeat it and stop. The silence did more than any question.
- **The medical line is said once, simply, before the money.** "Our coaches aren't doctors… cleared by your doctor." It's safe and honest.
- **"Already paying a chiropractor to fix it after."** He made the value argument himself. You just agree.
