## What to notice

- **"Why pay when I can get it free?"** Agree: "You can. There's a ton of good free stuff, including this challenge." Then the frame back: "So you signed up anyway. What had you looking?"
- **"How long, and what's happened?"** Let his own results answer the objection. Eight months stuck.
- **"Has someone said something?"** A guess that's often right in fitness. It got "do you even still go?"
- **"I guess that's what a coach does. You said it, not me."** He explained what a coach is for, so you never had to pitch it.
- **On a challenge lead, tell them to keep going** ("finish day 5 first"). The free thing builds trust, it doesn't compete.
