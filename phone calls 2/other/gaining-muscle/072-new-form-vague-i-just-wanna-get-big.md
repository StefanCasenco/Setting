## What to notice

- **"I just wanna get big" → "Like who? Is there somebody you look at?"** Asking for a picture turns a vague goal into a real person (his brother).
- **"Skinny Jaylen." Is that what people call you?** Repeat the nickname as a question. It opened the real pain.
- **Silence after "it's fine"** got "it's not fine. I'm tired of being the little brother."
- **The roadblock in his words:** no plan, eating once a day. That's knowledge.
- **Low money, said honestly.** "Tom will be straight with you about what makes sense with two K." Don't oversell to a 20-year-old.
