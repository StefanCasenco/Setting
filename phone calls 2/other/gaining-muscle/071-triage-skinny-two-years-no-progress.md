*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Owen, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Dan's team. I've got you down for 6 about building muscle. What got you booking in?

**[S]:** What made you start going two years ago?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's brutal. What's the worst part of it for you?

**[O]:** Feeling like a kid, honestly. Like I'm 24 and I look 16. My girlfriend says she doesn't care. I care.

**[S]:** How long has it bugged you like that?

**[O]:** Honestly, since high school. But the last two years, it's worse, because I'm trying and nothing's happening.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be in six to twelve months?

**[O]:** Like 165, 170. Actually look like I lift.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[S]:** Why's that important now?

**[O]:** My cousin's other brother is getting married in June. *(laughs)* Same family. Same photographer.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Revenge wedding.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's been stopping you from putting on size?

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[O]:** Somebody who actually looks at everything: food, training, all of it. And tells me what I'm doing wrong. Nobody's ever done that.

**[O]:** June. And being carded at 24. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **"What made you start, two years ago?"** The first moment that sent them to the gym is usually the pain. Here it was the wedding photo and "coat hanger".
- **"I laughed. But I didn't."** Don't laugh along at the pain moment.
- **"What's the worst part of it for you?"** Open, and he said it himself: "I look 16."
- **Health money: what they spend now, what's coming in, savings, then "in a position to invest?"** Simpler than bizopp, same principle.
- **"Bring what you eat"** gives the closer real data, and gets a laugh.
