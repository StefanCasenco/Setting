## What to notice

- **"Is this a bot?" "Say something a bot wouldn't say."** Play along with a real, slightly embarrassing detail (cereal for dinner). The test is passed in one line.
- **"What's a normal day?" then "And between breakfast and 3?"** Fact questions showed him the problem. He said it himself: "I don't eat a lot."
- **"So ten hours, twenty thousand steps, and nothing between 7 and 3."** Put his own facts side by side. Don't explain calories.
- **The muscle goal was really about money and status.** "Why does that matter to you?" got "number nine on the board."
- **"If I'm top three, it pays for itself."** He made the investment case himself.
