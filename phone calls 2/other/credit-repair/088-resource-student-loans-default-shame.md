## What to notice

- **"No judgment at all, I promise," at the very start.** With a quiet, ashamed prospect, say it before anything else.
- **"Nobody knows?"** A small surprise, then: "That's a lot to carry on your own."
- **"That's not you being careless. That's you being 20 and dealing with a lot."** A reframe built from the facts she gave you, not flattery.
- **"Is that bad?" "Not bad at all."** Respect her privacy, and book around it ("a time you can talk privately?").
- **Student loans: no advice.** "There can be specific programs, she'll explain." Point to the specialist. Don't guess.
