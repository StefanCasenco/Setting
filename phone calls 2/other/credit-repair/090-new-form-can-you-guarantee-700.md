## What to notice

- **"Can you guarantee 700?"** "Straight up back: no. Nobody honest can guarantee a score." In credit repair, a guarantee is a red flag, and legally risky.
- **"How did that go?"** about the last guy who guaranteed. His own story proves your point.
- **"Test." "Did I pass?"** Some prospects test your honesty on purpose. Honesty is the pass.
- **The deadline is a business deal** (HOA, January). Get the date. That's the why-now.
- **Point to the closer for other options** ("or what else might work for the business loan"). Don't play loan advisor yourself.
