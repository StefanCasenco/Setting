## What to notice

- **"I already got scammed by one of you people."** Don't defend: "I'd be angry too." Then: "You should be."
- **"I'm not going to defend them."** Never defend the industry. You lose trust straight away.
- **"I'm not going to ask you to trust me on this call."** With a burnt prospect, asking for trust raises the guard. Not asking lowers it.
- **"Two break-ins on your floor?"** Repeat the scary detail. The real pain was safety, not a score.
- **"If you don't like the answers, you walk."** Burnt prospects book when they feel in control.
