## What to notice

- **A couple where one partner's score is the problem.** Handle it carefully. "No judgment at all, most people have a story behind the number."
- **When they disagree about blame, don't take sides.** "Sounds like you've both been carrying it." It calms things without picking a side.
- **The NICU story:** acknowledge it ("Sorry, man. That's a lot"), and he said "I'd do it again." Don't dig into the medical part.
- **"That I'm not the reason we can't."** The quiet partner's North Star came out from "what would that mean for you both?"
- **"The real timeline."** In credit repair, what people really want is an honest answer, not a promise. The pitch offers exactly that.
