**[K]:** Some weeks. Yeah.

## What to notice

- **Let them vent fully when it's not their fault.** "Twenty-three thousand. In your name. That you didn't do." Repeat the unfairness slowly.
- **"Don't apologize. I'd be furious too."** When an angry prospect apologises for being angry, give them permission.
- **"More hours on hold than with your kids."** The impact, in his line. That's what makes it personal, not just paperwork.
- **"What changed since May?"** That's the old lead's why-now. Doing it himself for six months didn't work.
- **"I can't promise outcomes. But you won't be doing it alone."** Honest about limits, and clear about what the help is.
