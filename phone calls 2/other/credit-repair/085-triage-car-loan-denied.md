## What to notice

- **"Good for you for walking."** Praise the decision he's embarrassed about.
- **"Oh, you're one of those."** That's the shame moment. "That's a bad feeling." No more needed.
- **"So there's a real clock on this."** The dying car is the why-now. Name the deadline.
- **"Most people I talk to have a story behind the number."** The key lead-in for this niche. It turns shame into a story.
- **"I can't promise you any numbers, nobody honest can."** Said plainly in the pitch. It's legally safe and it builds trust.
