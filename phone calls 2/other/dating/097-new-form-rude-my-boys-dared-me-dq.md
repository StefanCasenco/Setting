## What to notice

- **Never take a prank personally.** Laugh along ("sounds like a good night"), and ask if you're on speaker so you know the room.
- **Give the honest exit fast:** "Is this actually something you want help with, or just the dare?" Then: "If it was just the dare, that's all good."
- **Keep it short and keep your dignity.** Two minutes max. Don't try to sell a guy surrounded by his friends daring him.
- **When he asked "what do you actually do?",** answer straight: "We help guys who are struggling, and it's a real investment." Don't flip into a pitch.
- **"You're cool, bro."** You left a good impression with a whole room of college guys. Some of them will need help one day.
