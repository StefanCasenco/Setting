# 092 · Dating coaching · Booked a slot (triage) · Divorced at 40, back on the apps, lost

**Prospect:** Neil, 40, insurance adjuster, shares custody of his daughter (9), Denver · **Lead:** booked an 8pm call from a YouTube ad · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

> **Dating calls are personal and often a bit embarrassing.** Keep it light where you can, and serious where they are. Never judge, and never joke about their looks or their past. Don't give dating advice on the call. That's the coach's job.

## What to notice

- **"Bit embarrassing, this." "Not at all, man. Loads of guys book in."** Normalise it straight away.
- **"That's really normal after twelve years. She was your whole life."** He made fun of himself, and you answered with kindness and truth.
- **"How are you doing with the divorce itself, if you don't mind me asking?"** One gentle question. He shared, and you didn't dig.
- **"The quiet weeks."** Repeating his phrase found the real pain: Sunday nights, the new guy in his old kitchen, the drive home.
- **Use his buddy's advice as the closing joke.** It shows you listened, and it ends warm.
