## What to notice

- **"I'm not great on the phone."** Give control first: "If it's too much, just say so and I'll let you go."
- **"Sorry, that's weird to say." "Not weird at all."** The biggest moment of the call. Normalise it and thank him.
- **"There's always an aunt."** A small, safe joke that got the first laugh.
- **"So it's not that you have nothing to say. It's that it locks up."** The setter said it back, and he said "that's exactly it."
- **Adapt the closer call to him** (audio only). Removing his biggest fear is what gets him to show up.
