## What to notice

- **Atefur's time move, from #4:** "If it's just a time thing, why not cut your hours, instead of getting help from someone like us?" Then: "So what were you hoping we could do for you?" Jason admitted himself that it's not just time.
- **"Hide behind the hours."** That came straight from the time challenge. Ask "what do you mean?" once.
- **"Eating dinner alone at 1am in Alfama."** Repeat the specific scene. That's the impact.
- **The goal under the goal:** "that the move was worth it", and his mom's question.
- **Time zones at the booking.** Confirm the time *in his zone*. Wrong-time no-shows are the easiest ones to avoid.
