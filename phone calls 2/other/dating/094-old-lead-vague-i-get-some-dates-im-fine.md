# 094 · Dating coaching · Old lead, 4 months · Vague: "I get some dates, I'm fine" (Atefur's contrast challenge)

**Prospect:** Matt, 29, owns a small HVAC business, tall, does well, Phoenix · **Lead:** applied in July, didn't book · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

> This is the prospect you played for Atefur in #3 and #4: tall, doing well, "I get some dates," vague about the problem. Here's how Atefur's moves crack it.

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Matt, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Rob's team. Random one. You applied back in July about dating. Where are you at with it now?

**[M]:** Oh. Yeah. Honestly, I'm fine. I get some dates. I was just curious back then.

**[S]:** Fair. What had you curious, in July?

**[M]:** I dunno. Just wanted more dates, like everyone.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** What do you do?

**[M]:** I've got an HVAC company. Two crews.

**[S]:** Doing well?

**[M]:** Yeah, business is good. Money's good. I'm 6'3", I lift. *(laughs)* I've got everything in check, except this.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** "Except this." Okay. And honestly, most guys in your position, business, money, tall, they're getting a few dates, some guys get none. So you're doing better than a lot of guys. Why is it still something you want to solve?

**[M]:** *(pause)* …Good question. I guess it's the quality. I go on dates, but they don't go anywhere. I never get a second date with the ones I actually like.

**[S]:** Never a second date with the ones you like. How many dates in the last three months, roughly?

**[M]:** Like… six?

**[S]:** And out of those six, how many were with someone you actually wanted to see again?

**[M]:** *(pause)* One. Maybe two.

**[S]:** And second dates?

**[M]:** Zero. *(laughs)* When you say it like that.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, being the guy who's got everything else in check?

**[M]:** Frustrating, honestly. Like, I can build a business from nothing, I can't get a second date. It doesn't make sense.

**[S]:** "It doesn't make sense."

**[M]:** And I'm 29. My buddies are all getting engaged. Three weddings next year. I'm going alone to all three.

**[S]:** Alone to all three.

**[M]:** Yeah. *(pause)* And honestly, I come home to an empty house that I paid for. Four bedrooms. Just me and the dog.

**[S]:** Four bedrooms, just you and the dog.

**[M]:** *(laughs, not really)* Yeah.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be?

**[M]:** With someone real. Someone who's not after the money, either. Somebody to actually fill the house.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[M]:** That I'm not just building a business. I'm building a life.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've figured out business and fitness. What do you think's stopping the second dates?

**[M]:** I don't know, honestly. I think I talk about work too much. Maybe I come off too intense. Nobody's ever told me.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[M]:** Someone to actually watch what I'm doing and tell me straight. Like a coach for the gym, but for this.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[M]:** Seven.

**[S]:** And when did you decide it's worth getting help, instead of figuring it out yourself?

**[M]:** I mean… in July, I guess.

**[S]:** And July to now, what's happened?

**[M]:** *(laughs)* Six dates, zero second dates.

**[S]:** So what's holding it back from a nine?

**[M]:** Pride. I figure stuff out myself. That's who I am.

**[S]:** And has figuring it out yourself worked, for this one?

**[M]:** *(pause)* No. It hasn't. Okay. Nine.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. What are you taking home personally?

**[M]:** About twelve K a month.

**[S]:** And are you comfortable putting some aside?

**[M]:** Yeah. That's not an issue.

**[S]:** And if Mark finds the right fit, is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do?

**[M]:** Yeah. I pay a trainer. I'd pay for this.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've got the business, the money and the gym in check, except this. Six dates in three months, one or two you liked, zero second dates, and it doesn't make sense to you. Three buddies are getting married next year and you're going alone to all three. You come home to a four-bedroom house with just you and the dog. You want someone real, not after the money, so you're building a life and not just a business. What you said you need is someone watching what you're doing and telling you straight, like a trainer for this. Next step would be a Zoom with Mark, one of Rob's coaches. He'll look at your profile and how the dates are going, and tell you straight. When are you free?

**[M]:** After 7.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[M]:** *(gives it)*

## What to notice

- **Atefur's contrast challenge:** "Most guys in your position are getting a few dates, some get none… so why is it still something you want to solve?" It makes him defend his own reason, and that cracked "I'm fine".
- **Atefur's quantify move:** six dates, one or two he liked, zero second dates. Numbers he can't argue with. Take them and move on (setter, not closer).
- **Repeat his word as a question,** Atefur-style. "Except this." "It doesn't make sense." "Alone to all three." "Four bedrooms, just you and the dog."
- **Pin the old lead's why-now:** "July to now, what's happened?" "Six dates, zero second dates."
- **Pride as the roadblock.** "Has figuring it out yourself worked, for this one?" One question, no lecture.
