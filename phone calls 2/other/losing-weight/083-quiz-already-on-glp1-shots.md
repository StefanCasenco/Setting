# 083 · Losing weight · Took the quiz · Already on GLP-1 shots: medical stays with the doctor

**Prospect:** Debbie, 47, real estate agent, Scottsdale · **Lead:** took the "why can't I lose weight after 40" quiz · **Money:** program (health) · **Ends:** booked, with clear medical boundaries

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hi Debbie, how are you? It's Stefan from Claire's team. You took our quiz earlier, the "after 40" one. What made you take it?

**[D]:** Oh, hi! Well, I'm actually on Ozempic. My doctor put me on it in March. And I've lost twenty-five pounds. But I'm scared.

**[S]:** Scared of what?

**[D]:** Of what happens when I come off it. My friend came off hers and gained it all back in four months. Plus some.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Okay. And just so I say it up front, Debbie, our coaches aren't doctors, so anything about the medication, dosing, or coming off it, that's between you and your doctor. Is that okay?

**[D]:** Oh, yes, of course. I wouldn't do anything without her.

**[S]:** Good. What do you do for work?

**[D]:** Real estate. Twenty years.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** What happened with your friend, coming off it?

**[D]:** She lost forty pounds, came off it, and then it all came back. And she was devastated. She said it was worse than before, because she'd felt what it was like to be thin, and then lost it.

**[S]:** And when you think about that happening to you, how does it feel?

**[D]:** Terrifying, honestly. I finally feel good. I'm showing houses and I'm not out of breath on the stairs. I bought jeans in a size I haven't worn in ten years. And I'm scared I'm just borrowing it.

**[S]:** "Borrowing it."

**[D]:** Like it's not really mine. Because I haven't changed anything except the shot. I still eat the same, I just eat less because I'm not hungry. I haven't learned anything.

**[S]:** How long have you been worried about that?

**[D]:** Since my friend. July.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be in six months?

**[D]:** Knowing how to eat and live so that when my doctor says it's time to come off, I keep it. That it's actually mine.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[D]:** Not being my friend. *(pause)* And honestly, I'm 47, I'm divorced, and I'm dating again. I want to feel confident without worrying it's going to disappear.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to make it yours?

**[D]:** Someone to teach me the habits. Like, what to eat, how to train so I keep muscle. My doctor said I should be lifting. I've never lifted anything heavier than a lockbox. *(laughs)*

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[D]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[D]:** My friend's face. When she told me.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Helen can build something realistic. What are you spending now, on the medication and anything else?

**[D]:** Insurance covers most of the shot. I pay about a hundred a month. Plus a gym I don't really use. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** And roughly what's coming in?

**[D]:** It varies with commissions. Last year I did about a hundred and forty.

**[S]:** And if Helen finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to make it last something you'd be in a position to do?

**[D]:** Absolutely. I'd rather invest now than lose it all.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've lost twenty-five pounds since March, you're not out of breath on the stairs at showings, and you bought jeans in a size you haven't worn in ten years. But your friend gained it all back in four months after coming off, and you're scared you're just borrowing it, because nothing's changed except the shot. You're dating again, and you want it to actually be yours. What you said you need is someone to teach you the habits, food and lifting, so you keep it. Next step would be a Zoom with Helen, one of Claire's coaches. She works with women on GLP-1s to build the habits alongside their doctor's plan. Anything medical stays with your doctor. When's good, between showings?

**[D]:** *(laughs)* Mornings before 10.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 8:30 or Wednesday at 9?

**[D]:** Wednesday.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[D]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it, and maybe dust off that gym card. *(laughs)*

**[D]:** *(laughs)* Okay, okay.

## What to notice

- **Medication comes up: say the medical line straight away, simply.** "Anything about the medication… that's between you and your doctor." Never give advice on it.
- **Her friend's story was her fear.** "When you think about that happening to you, how does it feel?"
- **"Borrowing it."** Her word, repeated. That's the North Star: she wants it to be hers.
- **She's a high earner,** in Scott's qualified box, so the money section can be short and direct.
- **The coaching works *alongside* the doctor, not instead of.** Make that clear in the pitch.
