# 081 · Losing weight · On the free 5-day challenge · "My husband says these things are a waste of money"

**Prospect:** Linda, 54, school cafeteria worker, Birmingham, Alabama · **Lead:** doing the free challenge, day 3 · **Money:** program (health) · **Ends:** booked with her husband

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hi Linda, how are you? It's Stefan from Claire's team. You're on day 3 of the challenge. How's it going?

**[S]:** So, your knees. What's going on with them?

**[S]:** How are the knees day to day?

**[L]:** It is. My friends call me. But it's not the same, honey.

**[S]:** No. It's not. How long has it been since you went?

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be in six months?

**[L]:** Walking up that hill. Back in the choir by Christmas, if I can.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[L]:** Having my people back. *(pause)* I didn't realize how lonely I'd gotten.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** Have you tried anything before?

## What to notice

- **"My husband says it's a waste of money" in the first minute.** "A lot of husbands say that, and he might be right about some of them." Agreeing takes the fight out.
- **"You stopped going to church because of the hill?"** Repeat the surprising detail as a question. It opened thirty-one years and the choir.
- **"I didn't realize how lonely I'd gotten."** She found the real goal herself, from "what would that mean for you?"
- **Never make the absent spouse the bad guy.** "If his brother got scammed, of course he's careful. He's looking out for you."
- **"Instead of convincing him, what if he was on the call?"** It takes the pressure off her and puts the real decision maker in front of the closer.
