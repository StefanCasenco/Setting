# 080 · Losing weight · Webinar attendee · Mom of three, her own wedding in 6 months, high energy

**Prospect:** Shanice, 31, part-time receptionist, three kids, Dallas · **Lead:** attended the webinar last night · **Money:** program, tight · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Shanice, how are you? It's Stefan from Claire's team. You were on Claire's webinar last night. What stood out to you?

**[SH]:** Oh my God, hi! I watched it in the car in the school pickup line! *(laughs)* The part about moms. Because I'm getting married! In May!

**[S]:** Oh, congrats!

**[SH]:** Thank you! Ten years together, three kids, and he finally asked. At Christmas. *(laughs)*

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Ten years. And what do you do for work?

**[SH]:** I'm a receptionist at a pediatrician's office. Part-time. With three kids, part-time is full-time. *(laughs)*

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** So what had you watching a weight loss webinar in the pickup line?

**[SH]:** *(pause)* Okay. So I went dress shopping last weekend with my mom and my sister. And it was awful.

**[S]:** Oh no. What happened?

**[SH]:** Nothing fit how I wanted. And the lady at the shop said, "We can always order a bigger size." And my mom said, "You've got six months, baby." And I just went quiet. I didn't cry in the shop. I cried in the car.

**[S]:** "We can always order a bigger size." That's not what you want to hear in a wedding dress shop.

**[SH]:** No! *(laughs)* I could've fought her.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I don't blame you. And outside the dress, how do you feel in yourself?

**[SH]:** Honestly? Since the third baby, I don't feel like me. I used to dance. I used to go out. I had confidence. Now I'm in leggings every day and I avoid photos. And I want my wedding photos to be the ones I put on the wall. Not the ones I hide.

**[S]:** "The ones I put on the wall."

**[SH]:** For the kids. When they're older.

**[S]:** That's beautiful. How long have you felt not like you?

**[SH]:** Since my youngest. She's two.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be by May?

**[SH]:** Feeling like me again. Going back to that shop and trying on the dress I actually want.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[SH]:** Walking down that aisle and not thinking about my arms. *(laughs)* Just thinking about him.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** Have you tried anything since the baby?

**[SH]:** A 30-day challenge on Instagram. I did like twelve days. *(laughs)* With three kids, it's impossible to cook separate meals and work out.

**[SH]:** Something that works with three kids and family dinners. And someone holding me to it.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** Totally understand, and thank you for saying it. Weddings are crazy expensive. That's exactly why I'm asking, so I don't book you into something that doesn't fit. What's coming in for the house, roughly?

**[SH]:** I'm part-time, about thirteen hundred. He's an electrician, about forty-five hundred.

**[SH]:** Everything's going in the wedding fund. Like eight hundred a month.

**[S]:** And how much is in the wedding fund?

**[SH]:** About nine thousand. The venue's paid.

**[S]:** Okay. If this was something for the wedding, would it come from the wedding fund, or separate?

**[SH]:** *(pause)* Honestly? It's for the wedding. *(laughs)* It's for the photos!

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's one way to look at it. And you'd decide with your fiancé?

**[SH]:** Yeah. He'll say yes. He says I'm beautiful anyway, but he knows how I feel.

## What to notice

- **Match high energy.** "Oh, congrats!" Real excitement. A flat setter kills an excited prospect.
- **"Oh no. What happened?"** When the story turns. Simple, warm, and it gets the whole dress-shop moment.
- **Side with her against the shop lady.** "That's not what you want to hear in a wedding dress shop." Shared outrage builds rapport.
- **"The ones I put on the wall."** Repeat her image. It's for the kids, which is deeper than the dress.
- **"I'm a little worried about this part."** Thank her, give the reason, then a gentle question: "from the wedding fund, or separate?" She decided it herself.
