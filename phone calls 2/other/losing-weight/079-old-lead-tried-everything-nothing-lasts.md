# 079 · Losing weight · Old lead, 5 months · "I've tried everything, nothing works for me"

**Prospect:** Tanya, 36, hairstylist, two kids, Tulsa · **Lead:** downloaded a free meal plan in June · **Money:** program (health) · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Tanya, how are you? It's Stefan from Claire's team. Back in June you grabbed our free meal plan. I'm checking in to see how it went.

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Oh. I did it for like five days.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Five days is five more than most people. What happened on day six?

**[T]:** My son's birthday. Cake. And then I was like, eh, I already messed up.

**[S]:** Ha, the "already messed up" thing. Everybody does that.

**[T]:** Honestly, I've tried everything. Keto, Weight Watchers, Noom, intermittent fasting, shakes. I even did the shots for a little bit. Nothing works for me.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** That's a lot of things. How long have you been trying?

**[T]:** Since my second kid. Seven years.

**[S]:** And you're doing hair full-time?

**[T]:** Full-time. Rent a chair. On my feet ten hours.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Seven years. That's exhausting.

**[T]:** It is. Honestly, I'm kind of done. Maybe this is just my body now.

**[S]:** Yeah, I can hear you're tired of it. And I'm not going to tell you this time is magic. Can I ask you something, though?

**[T]:** Sure.

**[S]:** All those things, keto, Weight Watchers, the shakes. Did any of them work at all, even for a bit?

**[T]:** Oh, yeah. They all worked for a bit. I lost twenty-two pounds on keto. Then it came back.

**[S]:** So it's not that nothing works. It's that nothing lasts.

**[T]:** *(pause)* …Huh. Yeah. That's actually true.

**[S]:** And when it comes back, what's usually the moment?

**[T]:** Life. A birthday, a bad week at the salon, the kids are sick. And I go back to how I ate before.

**[S]:** And in all seven years, has anyone ever helped you with the "after"? When life happens?

**[T]:** *(pause)* No. It's always a plan. You follow it or you don't.

**[S]:** What's the main reason you want it to last this time?

**[T]:** *(pause)* My son. He's seven. He said something last month. "Mommy, why is your tummy big?" He didn't mean anything. But it stuck.

**[S]:** Yeah. Kids say it straight.

**[T]:** They do. And my feet and knees are killing me at work. I'm 36.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If it lasted this time, where would you want to be in six months?

**[T]:** Not a number. I just want to feel good. Have energy for the kids after work. And my knees not hurting.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[T]:** That I'm not starting over every January. *(laughs)* Seven Januarys.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for it to last?

**[T]:** Someone to help when life happens. Not just a plan. Someone who says, "okay, the birthday happened, here's what we do Monday."

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[T]:** Seven.

**[S]:** What makes it a seven?

**[T]:** My son. And my knees.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a nine?

**[T]:** I've spent so much money on this stuff already. *(laughs)*

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** I believe you, and that's exactly why I'm asking, so I'm not putting you into another thing that doesn't fit. What have you been spending?

**[T]:** The shots were three hundred a month. I stopped. Noom was sixty.

**[S]:** And what's coming in each month, roughly?

**[T]:** About thirty-five hundred from the salon. My husband's about four K.

**[S]:** And are you able to save?

**[T]:** Some. Like five hundred.

**[S]:** And if Maria finds the right fit, is investing in yourself one more time something you'd be in a position to do?

**[T]:** One more time. If it's different.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, for seven years everything's worked for a bit, twenty-two pounds on keto, and then life happens, a birthday or a bad week, and it comes back. Nobody has ever helped with the after. Your son asked why your tummy's big, and your knees hurt after ten hours at the salon. You want energy for the kids and to stop starting over every January. What you said you need is someone who helps when life happens, not just a plan. Next step would be a Zoom with Maria, one of Claire's coaches. She'll look at all seven years and focus on why it didn't last. And if she thinks it's the same thing again, she'll tell you. When are you not at the salon?

**[T]:** Mondays. The salon's closed.

**[S]:** Monday at 10 or 1?

**[T]:** 10.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[T]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. And Tanya, no need to start a diet before Monday. *(laughs)* Just show up as you are.

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Oh, thank God.

## What to notice

- **"Five days is five more than most people."** It turns the old lead's failure into a small win.
- **"Nothing works for me" was never argued with.** "I'm not going to tell you this time is magic." Then a question: "Did any of them work at all?"
- **"So it's not that nothing works. It's that nothing lasts."** The reframe came from her own answer.
- **"Has anyone ever helped you with the after?"** It finds the gap without criticising any of the programs she tried.
- **"Don't start a diet before Monday."** It takes away the pressure to "prepare", which often turns into a no-show.
