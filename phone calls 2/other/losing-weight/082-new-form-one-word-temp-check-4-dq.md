## What to notice

- **One-word answers with no pain behind them aren't a puzzle.** It's just a person who isn't that bothered. Don't dig forever.
- **"Is there anything it's stopping you from doing?" "Not really."** That's the impact question. When it gets nothing, believe it.
- **Temp check 4 → "How come?" → binary challenge.** "Help now, or sort it out yourself?" She picked herself, out loud. Scott's rule.
- **Tell her why you're not booking, in her own facts.** Ten pounds, busy season over, a plan to hit the gym.
- **Being disqualified kindly makes people grateful** ("thanks for not pushing"). That's a good lead in a year, and a good word of mouth now.
