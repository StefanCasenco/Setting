# 077 · Losing weight · Booked a slot (triage) · Woman, 49, menopause, cries on the call

**Prospect:** Karen, 49, office manager at a law firm, Richmond, Virginia · **Lead:** booked a 7pm call from a Facebook ad for women over 40 · **Money:** program (health) · **Ends:** booked

> **Weight loss calls carry a lot of shame.** Go slower than on bizopp calls. Never comment on their body. Never promise a number of pounds. Medical stuff (menopause, medication, diabetes) is for their doctor and the coach. Your job is to listen.

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hi Karen, how are you? It's Stefan from Claire's team. I've got you down for 7 about some support with your weight. How's your evening going?

**[K]:** Oh, hi. It's fine. *(nervous laugh)* I almost cancelled, honestly.

**[S]:** I'm really glad you didn't. It's just a chat, nothing scary. What made you book in, in the end?

**[K]:** Well. I've always been a bit bigger, but it was manageable. And then the last two years, with menopause, everything changed. I've put on thirty pounds. And I'm doing the same things. Eating the same. And it just keeps going.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Thirty pounds, doing the same things. That sounds so frustrating. What have you tried?

**[K]:** Weight Watchers. Twice. Noom. I walk every day with my neighbor.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** And how has it been affecting you, day to day?

**[K]:** It's like my body's not mine anymore.

**[S]:** "Not mine anymore." What do you mean by that?

**[K]:** *(pause)* I don't recognize myself. In photos. In the mirror. I get dressed with my back to the mirror now.

**[S]:** With your back to it.

**[K]:** *(voice breaking)* Sorry. I didn't think I'd get upset. *(crying)*

**[S]:** *(gently)* Karen, please don't apologize. Take your time. There's no rush at all.

**[K]:** *(pause)* Sorry. It's just… nobody really asks. My husband's lovely, but he says "you look fine." And I'm not fine.

**[S]:** "You look fine," when you don't feel fine. That's lonely.

**[K]:** *(quiet)* It is. Yes.

**[S]:** Thank you for telling me.

**[K]:** *(small laugh)* I've told you more than I've told him.

**[S]:** That's okay. Sometimes it's easier with someone you don't know.

**[S]:** Is there something you're missing out on, because of how you feel?

**[K]:** My daughter's getting married in May. And I've been dreading dress shopping. I haven't even started. She keeps asking. I keep saying "next month."

**[S]:** "Next month."

**[K]:** I don't want to be the mother of the bride hiding at the back of the photos.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If this worked, where would you want to be by May?

**[K]:** Honestly? Just feeling like me again. I don't care about a number. I want to go dress shopping and not cry in the fitting room.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[K]:** Being in the photos. Next to her. Not at the back.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've tried Weight Watchers twice, Noom, walking. What do you think's been stopping it from working?

**[K]:** I think everything I've tried was made for thirty-year-olds. Nobody talks about menopause. It's like the rules changed and nobody told me.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[K]:** Someone who actually understands what's happening to my body now. And checks in on me. I give up when nobody's watching.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[K]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[K]:** May. And the mirror.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Has your doctor said anything about the menopause side?

**[K]:** I just started HRT. She said it might help. Early days.

**[S]:** Good that you've got her involved. Our coaches aren't doctors, so anything medical stays with her. Is that okay?

**[K]:** Yes, of course.

**[S]:** Couple money questions, just so Helen can put together something realistic. What are you spending now, on Noom, Weight Watchers, anything like that?

**[K]:** About sixty a month, between everything.

**[S]:** And between you and your husband, are you able to put something aside each month?

**[K]:** Yes, we're comfortable. Kids are grown.

**[S]:** And if Helen finds the right fit, is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do?

**[K]:** *(pause)* Yes. Actually, it's the first thing I've wanted just for me in a long time.

**[S]:** I love that.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, menopause changed everything two years ago, thirty pounds doing the same things, and you get dressed with your back to the mirror. Your husband says you look fine, and you're not. Your daughter's getting married in May, and you keep saying "next month" about the dress, because you don't want to hide at the back of the photos. Everything you've tried felt made for thirty-year-olds, and what you said you need is someone who understands what's happening now and checks in on you. Next step would be a Zoom with Helen, one of Claire's coaches. She's 54, and she went through this herself. She'll go through the HRT, what you've tried, and what's realistic before May. Evenings work?

**[K]:** Yes, after 6.

**[S]:** Monday at 6:30 or Wednesday at 7?

**[K]:** Monday. Before I talk myself out of it. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Best email?

**[K]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. And Karen, if you do start talking yourself out of it, text me first. I'll talk you back in. *(laughs)*

**[K]:** *(laughs)* Okay. Thank you, Stefan. Really.

## What to notice

- **"I almost cancelled." "I'm really glad you didn't."** It welcomes the courage it took. "Nothing scary" lowers the guard.
- **When she cried, the setter got quieter and shorter.** "Please don't apologize. Take your time." Then silence, until she came back.
- **"'You look fine,' when you don't feel fine. That's lonely."** One line naming what she didn't say. It's not therapy.
- **No body comments, no promises about pounds, and a medical line.** "Anything medical stays with her." Simple and safe.
- **"Before I talk myself out of it."** She named the risk. "Text me first. I'll talk you back in."
