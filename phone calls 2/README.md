## How to train with these

1. **Read 00-read-first.md out loud. Twice.**
2. **Pick the niche you're dialling.** Read three calls out loud, setter side only.
3. **Cover the setter lines.** At each one, say your line before you look. Then compare.
4. **Read "What to notice".** Find the stage, the context line, and the moment the setter didn't say the answer for them.
5. **Roleplay the punches.** Give Kevin or Atefur the "Find a call by what hits you" table and ask them to *be* that prospect. Then switch.
6. **Then take real calls.** These are the sparring. The leads are the fight.

All people, businesses and owner names are invented.
