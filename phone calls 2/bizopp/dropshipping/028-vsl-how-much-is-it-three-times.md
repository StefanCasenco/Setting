## What to notice

- **"How much?" #1, at the open:** "Straight to it, I respect that." Then it depends, that's what this call's for, and "If it's not a fit, I'll tell you in five minutes."
- **#2, in the middle:** "I can tell this is really bugging you." Name the frustration. Give a bit ("a real investment, not a fifty-dollar course"), then box the time: "Two more minutes."
- **#3, at the booking:** "He'll go through the price straight up. Promise." He gets what he wants, at the right time.
- **Don't make up a price range you don't know.** If your offer allows a range, give it at #2. If not, don't guess.
- **His little brother was the North Star**, not the money.
