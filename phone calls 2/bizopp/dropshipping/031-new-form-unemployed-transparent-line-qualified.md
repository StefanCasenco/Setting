# 031 · Dropshipping · New form · Unemployed 6 months: Scott's transparent line, and she qualifies

**Prospect:** Jasmine, 34, was a paralegal, unemployed since May, Philadelphia · **Lead:** filled out the form 20 minutes ago · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked (looked like a DQ, wasn't)

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Jasmine, how are you? It's Stefan from Nate's team. You put your info in about dropshipping a little bit ago. What made you fill it out?

**[J]:** Hi. Honestly, I need to figure something out. I've been out of work since May.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** I'm sorry to hear that. What were you doing before?

**[J]:** Paralegal. Eight years. The firm merged and they cut half the support staff.

**[S]:** Eight years. And have you done any dropshipping, or anything online?

**[J]:** No. I've been applying to paralegal jobs. Nobody's hiring. Or they want me to take thirty percent less.

**[S]:** Okay. Jasmine, I'll be transparent with you, just so I don't waste your time. If you're looking at our coaching, it's a mid four-figure investment, and dropshipping itself needs some money for testing. Is that something that would be viable for you right now, or is it completely out of the question?

**[J]:** *(pause)* No, that's fine. I got a severance package, and I've got savings. I'm not desperate-desperate. I'm just… lost.

**[S]:** Okay, that's really helpful to know. Thank you.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** "Lost." What do you mean?

**[J]:** Like, I did everything right. College, paralegal certificate, eight years, never late. And they cut me in a Zoom call that lasted four minutes. Four minutes.

**[S]:** Four minutes, after eight years.

**[J]:** They didn't even turn their camera on. *(laughs bitterly)* HR lady with a black square.

**[S]:** How did that feel?

**[J]:** Like I was nothing. Like eight years was nothing. And now every interview, they look at me like I'm the problem. Like, why'd you get cut?

**[S]:** How have the last six months been for you?

**[J]:** Honestly? I've been depressed. I'm not gonna lie. I sleep till 10. I apply to jobs, I don't hear back. My mom keeps sending me Indeed links. *(laughs)* I love her, but.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* The Indeed links.

**[J]:** Every day. "Jas, look at this one."

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If dropshipping worked, where would you want it in a year?

**[J]:** Honestly, I'd want it to replace my salary. Like fifty-five K. So, four and a half a month.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[J]:** That nobody can do that to me again. A black square on Zoom. I want to own the thing.

**[S]:** Why's it important now?

**[J]:** Because the severance runs out in January. And I don't want to take a job at thirty percent less and wait for the next black square.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped you from starting something like this already, in the last six months?

**[J]:** I kept thinking the right job would come. And I don't know anything about it. I'm a paralegal, I know contracts. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[J]:** A real plan. Week by week. I'm good with structure. I just need the structure.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[J]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[J]:** January. And the black square.

**[S]:** And what's keeping it from a ten?

**[J]:** Fear that I'll lose the savings too. Then I've got nothing.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Okay. You mentioned severance and savings. What's the severance looking like, monthly?

**[J]:** About thirty-eight hundred a month till January.

**[S]:** And with your bills, what's left over?

**[J]:** Maybe eight hundred.

**[S]:** And savings, outside the severance?

**[J]:** About twelve thousand. Plus a 401k I'm not touching.

**[S]:** Good, keep the 401k as the 401k. And in dropshipping it takes money to make money. Of the twelve, what would you be comfortable setting aside to get started properly?

**[J]:** Maybe four. Five max.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, after eight years as a paralegal, they cut you in a four-minute Zoom with the camera off. You've been applying for six months, everyone wants you at thirty percent less, and you feel like you did everything right and it didn't matter. You want to replace the fifty-five K with something you own, so nobody can black-square you again, before the severance runs out in January. What you said you need is a real plan, week by week. Next step would be a Zoom with Kyle, one of Nate's head coaches. He'll map out a structured plan around the four or five you'd set aside, and he'll be honest about whether January's realistic. I'm guessing you're flexible on time?

**[J]:** *(laughs)* Very flexible.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 11, or Thursday at 2?

**[J]:** Tomorrow.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 11. Best email?

**[J]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it. And Jasmine, tell your mom you're busy tomorrow at 11. *(laughs)*

**[J]:** *(laughs)* She'll be thrilled. Thank you.

## What to notice

- **"Out of work since May"** goes in the Miro "student/unemployed → hard qualify" box. So Scott's transparent line comes early: "Mid four-figure… viable for you right now, or completely out of the question?"
- **Unemployed doesn't always mean a DQ.** She had severance and twelve K. The line saved time *and* let you go deeper with confidence.
- **"A black square on Zoom."** Her image of the pain. Repeat it back once and use it in the recap.
- **"Fear that I'll lose the savings too."** That's what's holding back a 10. Flag it for the closer.
- **Protect the 401k.** Say it every time it comes up.
