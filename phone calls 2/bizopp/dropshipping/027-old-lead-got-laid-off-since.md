# 027 · Dropshipping · Old lead, 4 months · "I got laid off two weeks ago"

**Prospect:** Ryan, 30, was a logistics coordinator, Columbus, Georgia · **Lead:** opted in to a webinar in July, never booked · **Money:** capital (has severance) · **Ends:** booked, with no panic decisions

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Ryan, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Nate's team. Random one. You signed up for Nate's webinar back in July, and I'm just checking where you're at with dropshipping now.

**[R]:** *(laughs)* Bro. Funny timing.

**[S]:** Funny how?

**[R]:** I got laid off two weeks ago.

**[S]:** Ah, man. I'm sorry. What happened?

**[R]:** Company "restructured." Whole logistics team. Twelve of us. They're outsourcing to some 3PL.

**[S]:** And how are you holding up with it?

**[R]:** Honestly? Half freaking out, half kinda relieved. *(laughs)* I hated that job. But I liked the paycheck.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** How long were you there?

**[R]:** Five years.

**[S]:** And did they give you anything?

**[R]:** Three months severance.

**[S]:** Okay, so there's some runway. And did you ever try dropshipping after the webinar?

**[R]:** Nah. I said I would. Then work got busy. You know how it is.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** You said half relieved. What's the relieved part?

**[R]:** I was miserable, man. My boss was a micromanager. He'd Slack me at 9pm, "quick question." Every night. My girlfriend started calling him my side chick. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* The side chick. And what's the freaking-out part?

**[R]:** Rent. Car payment. Looking at Indeed and everything's like fifteen K less than I was making. And I'm 30, I feel like I should be further. My girlfriend's a nurse, she's been carrying us for the last two weeks and I hate that.

**[S]:** You hate that she's carrying you.

**[R]:** Yeah. She's not saying anything. But I feel it.

**[S]:** How's that been sitting with you?

**[R]:** Like a failure, if I'm honest. I'm applying to stuff all day and nobody's calling back.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If dropshipping went right, where would you want it in a year?

**[R]:** Honestly, matching what I made. Fifty-five K, so like four grand a month. Then I never have to deal with a 9pm Slack again.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[R]:** That I'm not depending on some company deciding to "restructure." And she's not carrying me.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've known about this since July. What do you think stopped you from starting back then?

**[R]:** Comfort. I had a paycheck. *(laughs)* Now I don't.

**[S]:** And now, what do you think it'd take to actually get it working?

**[R]:** Honestly, a plan. And someone telling me what to do in what order. 'Cause right now I have all day and I'm just… refreshing Indeed.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is this?

**[R]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[R]:** Three months of severance, and then it's on her.

**[S]:** And look, Ryan, I want to say this: the last thing I'd want is for you to jump into anything out of panic two weeks after a layoff. So would you do this instead of job hunting, or alongside?

**[R]:** *(pause)* Alongside, honestly. I'd be dumb to stop applying.

**[S]:** That's the smart answer.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, and I'll be careful, I know it's tight right now. What's the severance coming in at?

**[R]:** Like four K a month for three months.

**[S]:** And with rent and the car, what's left over?

**[R]:** Maybe eight hundred, if I'm careful.

**[S]:** And savings, outside the severance?

**[R]:** About eleven K.

**[S]:** And in dropshipping it takes money to make money. If you did it alongside job hunting, what would you set aside for testing?

**[R]:** Two, maybe three K. Not more. I'm not dumb.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you got laid off two weeks ago after five years, and part of you is relieved because of the 9pm Slacks. But your girlfriend's been carrying you, it feels like failure, and Indeed's all fifteen K less. You've got three months of severance and about eleven K. You want four grand a month so no company can restructure you again, and what you said you need is a plan and the right order to do things, alongside job hunting. Next step would be a Zoom with Kyle, one of Nate's head coaches. He'll look at what's realistic with two or three K and the three months you've got, and if he thinks you should just focus on a job right now, he'll tell you that too. And your girlfriend should be on, since she's carrying you right now. I'm guessing your days are free? *(laughs gently)*

**[R]:** *(laughs)* Very free. She's off Wednesday.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 11 or 2?

**[R]:** 11.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 11. Best email? Not the work one. *(laughs)*

**[R]:** *(laughs)* That one's deactivated. *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept and forward to her.

**[R]:** Done.

**[S]:** And Ryan, two weeks in, you're allowed to be freaking out. Speak Wednesday.

## What to notice

- **"Funny timing" means ask "funny how?"** Something always happened.
- **"Half freaking out, half relieved."** Ask about both halves separately. Each one gave a different pain (the boss, and his girlfriend carrying him).
- **Slow the panic down.** "The last thing I'd want is for you to jump in out of panic… instead of job hunting, or alongside?" He chose "alongside" himself. That protects him, and it cuts refunds later.
- **"What stopped you back in July?" "Comfort."** The old lead's real roadblock is often just that they had a paycheck.
- **"If he thinks you should focus on a job, he'll tell you."** It's honest and it lowers the pressure.
