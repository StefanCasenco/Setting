# 024 · Dropshipping · Booked a slot (triage) · 22-year-old, keen but fuzzy on why

**Prospect:** Ethan, 22, works at a UPS hub, Indianapolis · **Lead:** booked a 7pm call from a YouTube ad · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Yo Ethan, what's up, man? It's Stefan from Nate's team. I've got you down for 7 about dropshipping. What got you booking in?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's the video that gets everybody. What is it about it for you, though? What made you actually book?

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Fair. What are you doing for work right now?

**[S]:** What happened with it?

**[E]:** I got to the ads part and got scared. Everybody says you gotta spend like a grand to test. I was like, nah.

**[S]:** And preload, 4am, how's that?

**[E]:** It's money. Part-time though. Like twenty-five hours. They don't give full-time to new guys.

**[S]:** So what are you doing the rest of the day?

**[E]:** *(laughs)* Honestly? Sleeping. Gaming. Scrolling.

**[S]:** How do you feel about that?

**[E]:** *(pause)* Kinda bad, honestly. My mom keeps asking what I'm doing with my life. All my friends from high school are either in college or in the army. I'm just… loading boxes and playing Warzone.

**[S]:** "What am I doing with my life." How long you been feeling that?

**[S]:** What happened there?

**[E]:** I didn't like it. I was doing business and it was just… boring. And expensive. I had like eight grand in loans after one year. So I left.

**[S]:** And how's your mom been about that?

**[E]:** Disappointed. She doesn't say it. But she was the first in our family to graduate college. And I quit. *(quiet)*

**[S]:** Yeah. I hear you.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If dropshipping worked out, what would you want, a year from now?

**[E]:** Making like five K a month. Moving out. Paying off the loans.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[E]:** That I didn't mess up by leaving school. Like, I could show my mom I'm actually doing something.

**[S]:** Why's that important now?

**[E]:** Somebody who actually knows, telling me what to test and when to stop. So I don't burn a grand.

**[E]:** My mom. And December.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes in six months?

## What to notice

- **"I dunno, I just want that"** after the forty K video. Don't argue with the dream. Go to facts (work, the Shopify), and the real thing comes out.
- **"What are you doing the rest of the day?"** A simple, curious question that opened the real impact: scrolling, his mom, the dropout.
- **His mom was the North Star.** "She was the first to graduate, and I quit." The setter said "I hear you" and didn't add anything.
- **The loans gave a real why-now** (December). Ask "why's that important now?" and you often get a date.
- **Check new money facts that come up** ("how much a month?"). It's useful context for the closer and shows it's manageable.
