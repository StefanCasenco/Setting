## What to notice

- **"To see if y'all are any different from the last two clowns."** Laugh, then: "I'm not going to pitch you on this call. Walk me through it." A burnt prospect needs to vent before he can hear anything.
- **"Why keep going after two? Most people quit after one."** A compliment and an impact question in one. It opened the disc, his wife, and the house.
- **He set the test himself:** "If that's not what y'all do, tell me now." Don't promise, pass it to the closer: "That's exactly the right question for Kyle."
- **Low limits are fine.** "Fifteen hundred, tops." Write it down, don't push.
- **Old ad screenshots** give the closer the evidence of what went wrong.
