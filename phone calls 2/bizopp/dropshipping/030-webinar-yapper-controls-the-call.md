# 030 · Dropshipping · Webinar attendee · The yapper: talks over you, asks his own questions

**Prospect:** Tony, 46, owns a pressure washing business, Tampa · **Lead:** watched the webinar, asked six questions in the chat · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Tony, how you doing, man? It's Stefan from Nate's team. You were on Nate's webinar last night, I saw you in the chat a lot. *(laughs)* What stood out?

**[T]:** Hey, Stefan! Listen, okay, before you start, let me tell you about me so we don't waste time. I've had my own pressure washing business nine years. Three trucks, five guys. I know business, okay? I'm not some kid. So what I want to know is: what's your success rate? How many people actually make money? What's the timeline? Do I need my own products or is it all suppliers? And who runs the ads, me or you? 'Cause I'm not learning Facebook ads at 46, I'll tell you that right now—

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Tony, I love it, you've got a list. Can I make a suggestion?

**[T]:** Go ahead.

**[S]:** Every one of those, Kyle, the head coach, can answer with real numbers. I'd just be guessing, and you'd catch me. *(laughs)* My job is to figure out in ten minutes whether it's even worth you sitting down with him, because you're a busy guy. So let me ask you a few, and then I'll make sure every one of yours gets answered. Deal?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …Okay. Deal. Go.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Nine years, three trucks. That's a real business. So what's got a guy with a real business looking at dropshipping?

**[T]:** Okay, so here's the thing. The business is good, right, but it's all me. If I'm not there, the guys mess up. Last month one of them blew out a window on a house in Clearwater. Eighteen hundred bucks, out of my pocket. And I'm doing the quotes, the scheduling, the invoicing, I'm on the phone at 9pm, my wife's like—

**[S]:** "It's all me." Is that the main thing?

**[T]:** *(pause)* Yeah. Yeah, that's the main thing.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** What does "all me" look like in a normal week?

**[T]:** Up at 5:30. In the truck by 6:30. On jobs till 4. Then quotes, invoices, Facebook messages till 10. Six days. Seven in spring. Pollen season, everybody wants their house washed.

**[S]:** Sixteen-hour days, six days, nine years.

**[T]:** *(laughs)* When you say it like that, it's nuts.

**[S]:** And how has that actually been affecting you? Not the business. You.

**[T]:** *(long pause)* My doctor told me my blood pressure's 165 over 100. In September. He said, "Tony, you're a heart attack waiting to happen." And my dad had his first one at 51.

**[S]:** Your dad at 51.

**[T]:** Yeah. *(quiet)* I'm 46.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's five years.

**[T]:** That's what I keep thinking.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If dropshipping worked, where would you want it in a year?

**[T]:** If it made five K a month, I'd hire an operations guy to run the trucks. Then it's not all me. Okay, but real quick, do I need my own—

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's on the list, Tony, I promise. Kyle's got it. And what would having that operations guy mean for you?

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Okay, okay. It means I'm not on the phone at 9pm. I can walk my daughter's dog with her. She's 15, she's got like three years left at home.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You're clearly good at business. What's stopped you from setting something like this up already?

**[T]:** Time. And I don't know online. I know trucks and chemicals.

**[S]:** And if it's just time, why not just hire the operations guy now, out of the pressure washing money?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …'Cause a good ops guy's sixty, seventy K, and that eats my margin. I need a second stream to pay for him. That's the whole idea.

**[S]:** Makes sense. So what do you think it'd take to make the second stream work?

**[T]:** Somebody who knows online to give me a system I can follow. I'm good at following a system. I just need the system.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is this?

**[T]:** Nine. My blood pressure's a nine. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** What makes it that high?

**[T]:** My dad. And my daughter.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. What are you taking home personally, a month?

**[T]:** Seven, eight K. Good months twelve.

**[S]:** And how much are you putting aside each month?

**[T]:** Fifteen hundred, two grand.

**[S]:** And personal savings, outside the business?

**[T]:** About sixty K.

**[S]:** And in dropshipping it takes money to make money. What would you set aside as capital to get it going?

**[T]:** If it's legit? Fifteen, twenty K. I'm not scared of investing. I'm scared of wasting.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, the business is good, but it's all you, sixteen-hour days for nine years. Your blood pressure's 165 over 100, and your dad had his first heart attack at 51. You want five K a month from a second stream so you can hire an ops guy and walk the dog with your daughter while she's still home. What you said you need is a system you can follow. Next step would be a Zoom with Kyle, one of Nate's head coaches. And bring the list, Tony: success rates, timeline, suppliers, ads, all of it, with real numbers. When are you not on a truck?

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Sunday. Or after 8.

**[S]:** Sunday at 10 or 1?

**[T]:** 10.

**[S]:** Sunday at 10. Best email?

**[T]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it. And your wife's welcome, since she's the one watching you on the phone at 9pm.

**[T]:** She'll grill him. I'll have my list.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* He likes a list. And Tony, look after that blood pressure.

## What to notice

- **Don't answer the yapper's questions, and don't ignore them.** "I love it, you've got a list. Can I make a suggestion?" Then the trade: you answer mine, the coach answers yours, with real numbers.
- **When he rambles, pull out one phrase and ask if it's the main thing.** "'It's all me.' Is that the main thing?" He stopped and said yes.
- **"Not the business. You."** Business owners answer as the business unless you ask about them.
- **He tried to take the frame back mid-call.** "That's on the list, I promise" with a laugh. No "please let me finish."
- **Atefur's time move again:** "Why not hire the ops guy now?" He explained margins, so now the real reason is said out loud.
