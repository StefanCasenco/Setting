## What to notice

- **"I can't understand you, where are you from?"** Answer happy and short, with one fun fact ("little country, good wine"), and say you'll slow down. Then go straight back to the question. Never apologise for your English for more than one sentence.
- **Most of the time it's not your English.** It's their car, their headphones, their signal. In the end: "Bro, your English is fine. I was just driving."
- **When they ask you to repeat, say it shorter, not louder.** "Have you tried dropshipping yet? Like, have you made a store?"
- **Get emails spelled out loud.** It avoids a dead invite, and accents go both ways.
- **The joke about the closer's accent** turns your fear into a shared laugh. It's confidence, not insecurity.
