# 034 · Amazon FBA · Old lead, 10 months · His last Amazon account got suspended

**Prospect:** Luis, 32, HVAC tech, San Antonio · **Lead:** downloaded the "first product" guide in January · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Luis, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Adam's team. Kind of a random one. Back in January you grabbed our free guide on finding your first Amazon product. Where you at with it now?

**[L]:** *(laughs)* Bro. Don't even get me started.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Now I have to get you started.

**[L]:** Okay. So I actually launched. March. Silicone kitchen stuff, spatulas, the whole set. And it was going good, like six K a month in sales by June. And then one day, boom. Suspended. "Inauthentic" complaint. Some customer said it was fake. It was literally my own brand.

**[S]:** Your own brand, and they said it was fake.

**[L]:** I sent eleven appeals. Eleven. Every time, a bot. "Decision is final." They held three grand of my money for ninety days.

**[S]:** Eleven appeals to a bot. How did that feel?

**[L]:** Bro, I threw my phone. Like, actually. Cracked the screen. *(laughs)* I'd put like nine grand into it.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** And you're still doing HVAC?

**[L]:** Yeah. Full-time. Never stopped.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** So after that, why are you still thinking about it? Most people would never touch Amazon again.

**[L]:** 'Cause it worked, man. Before the suspension, it actually worked. I was in somebody's attic fixing a furnace and my phone's going off, "you made a sale." That never happened to me before. Money while I'm in an attic.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Money in the attic.

**[L]:** And then it got taken away. And now I'm just in the attic. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** What's HVAC like, day to day?

**[L]:** Summer, AC calls, 70 hours a week. San Antonio, 105 degrees in an attic. Spring and fall, dead. So I'm broke half the year. Feast or famine.

**[S]:** How's the famine part been?

**[L]:** Me and my wife fight about money every October. Like clockwork. It's not even our fault, it's the weather. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* It's October now.

**[L]:** *(laughs)* Bro. Yeah. We had the fight last week.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If you came back to Amazon and it worked, what would you want it doing?

**[L]:** Three, four K a month steady. So October's not October anymore.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[L]:** No more fights. And I could take my son fishing in the slow months instead of stressing about bills.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What's stopped you from relaunching since June?

**[L]:** Fear, man. Of it happening again. I still don't know what I did wrong. If I don't know what I did wrong, I'll do it again.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to come back safer?

**[L]:** Somebody who's been through a suspension, who knows how to set it up so it doesn't happen. Brand registry, all that. I didn't know any of that.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[L]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[L]:** October. And I know it works. I've seen it work.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a ten?

**[L]:** Losing another nine grand.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Fair. Couple money questions. What's HVAC averaging, monthly?

**[L]:** Like forty-eight hundred averaged out. Seven in summer, two-five in October.

**[S]:** And are you able to put aside in the good months?

**[L]:** I try. Then the famine eats it. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** And right now, in total, saved?

**[L]:** About eight K. Plus the three grand Amazon gave back. I never touched it.

**[S]:** You never touched it?

**[L]:** It felt like, that's Amazon money. For when I try again.

**[S]:** That's kind of meaningful, man. And in Amazon it takes money to make money. What would you put in to relaunch?

**[L]:** The three, plus maybe two more. Five. That's it.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you launched in March and got to six K a month. Then you got suspended on a fake "inauthentic" complaint about your own brand, sent eleven appeals to a bot, and lost nine grand. You still remember the sale notification in the attic. You're in 105-degree attics in summer, broke in October, and you and your wife fight like clockwork. You want three, four K steady so October's not October. What you said you need is somebody who's been through a suspension to set it up safer, brand registry and all. Next step would be a Zoom with Marcus, one of Adam's head coaches. He's actually been suspended himself, and he'll go through what probably triggered yours. Your wife on the call too, since she lives through October. *(laughs)* When are you free?

**[L]:** It's October. Too free. *(laughs)* Weekday mornings.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 10 or Wednesday at 10?

**[L]:** Wednesday. She's off.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 10. Best email?

**[L]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it, and bring the suspension emails and the appeals.

**[L]:** Oh, I got all of them. Trust me. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I believe you.

## What to notice

- **"Don't even get me started."** Laugh: "Now I have to get you started." Let the old lead tell the story they've been dying to tell someone.
- **"A bot deciding on nine grand, I'd throw my phone too."** Join his anger. Don't calm him down.
- **"Why are you still thinking about it? Most people would never touch Amazon again."** A compliment and an impact question in one. It opened the money-in-the-attic moment.
- **"It's October now."** Connect the pain to today. He laughed, then admitted the fight was last week.
- **"Never touched it."** Notice the emotional detail and say so. The untouched Amazon money tells you how much he wants it.
