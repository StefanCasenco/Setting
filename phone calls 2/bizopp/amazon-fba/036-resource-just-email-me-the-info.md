## What to notice

- **"Can you just email me the info?"** "For sure, I can. Honestly though, info without knowing where you're at is just a brochure." Agree, then give a reason, a time box and an exit.
- **Keep the five-minute promise, or close to it.** She's between patients, so the questions get shorter.
- **"All on ibuprofen."** Another person's pain is often how prospects show you their own fear.
- **The roadblock had accountability in it:** "We watch YouTube together at night and then just go to bed."
- **Call back to her objection at the booking.** "Instead of a brochure, you get an actual plan."
