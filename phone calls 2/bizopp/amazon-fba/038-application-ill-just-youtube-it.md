# 038 · Amazon FBA · Application · "Honestly, I could just learn it on YouTube"

**Prospect:** Trevor, 28, IT help desk, Salt Lake City · **Lead:** application last night · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Trevor, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Adam's team. You put an application in last night about Amazon. What made you apply?

**[T]:** Hey. Honestly, I kind of applied on a whim. I'm not sure I need a program. I feel like I could just learn it on YouTube.

**[S]:** That's fair, man, there's a ton of free stuff out there. So what had you applying, if you could learn it on YouTube?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …Good question. *(laughs)* I guess I've been watching YouTube for like a year.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** A year. And what do you do for work?

**[T]:** IT help desk. For a hospital.

**[S]:** And in that year, have you launched anything?

**[T]:** No. I've done a lot of research.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** A year of research. How's that been?

**[T]:** Honestly? Kind of embarrassing. I tell my friends I'm "starting an Amazon business." They've stopped asking. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** They've stopped asking.

**[T]:** Yeah. Like, my buddy Jake asked in July how it's going and I said "still researching." He just went "ah." *(laughs)* That "ah" was rough.

**[S]:** And the help desk, how's that?

**[T]:** It's fine. It's "have you tried turning it off and on again" eight hours a day. Doctors yelling at me because their computer's slow. I've been there four years. They promoted someone who started after me.

**[S]:** How did that feel?

**[T]:** Like I'm invisible. That's kind of why I want my own thing. I'm tired of waiting for someone to notice me.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If Amazon worked, where would you want it in a year?

**[T]:** Like three K a month. Then I'd feel like I actually did something.

**[S]:** What would actually doing something mean for you?

**[T]:** That I'm not the "still researching" guy. *(laughs)* And that I'm not just waiting on a promotion.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** So you've done a year of YouTube and you know a lot. What do you think's actually stopped you from launching?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …I keep thinking I need to know more first. Like, one more video.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for you to actually launch?

**[T]:** *(long pause)* Honestly? Someone who'd make me do it. Like, a deadline. If it's just me, I'll research forever.

**[S]:** So it's less about learning more.

**[T]:** Yeah. I know enough. I just don't do it.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[T]:** Six.

**[S]:** How come only a six?

**[T]:** I don't know. I guess I'm comfortable. The job's fine. Nobody's forcing me.

**[S]:** So is this something you actually want to do now, or something you'll keep researching for another year?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …Now. I don't want to be "still researching" next July.

**[S]:** So where's that number really?

**[T]:** Eight. Honestly. I just needed somebody to ask me like that.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. What's the help desk paying you?

**[T]:** About thirty-eight hundred.

**[S]:** And how much are you putting aside each month?

**[T]:** Like six hundred. I live pretty cheap.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[T]:** About fourteen K. I've been saving for this, actually. For a year.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* For the business you've been researching.

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Yeah.

**[S]:** And Amazon takes money to make money. What would you put toward launching?

**[T]:** Eight, maybe. I've been planning that number.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've done a year of YouTube and you know enough, but you keep thinking you need one more video. Your friends stopped asking, and your buddy's "ah" was rough. At the help desk they promoted someone who started after you, and you feel invisible. You want three K a month so you're not the "still researching" guy. What you said you need is someone who'll make you do it, with deadlines. Next step would be a Zoom with Daniel, one of Adam's head coaches. **[T]:** After 5. Tomorrow?

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 5:30 or 6:30?

**[T]:** 5:30.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[T]:** *(gives it)*

## What to notice

- **"I could just learn it on YouTube."** "That's fair, there's a ton of free stuff." Then Scott's frame back: "So what had you applying, if you could learn it on YouTube?"
- **"What do you think it'd take?"** replaced "do you think you could do it alone?". He came to accountability himself: "Someone who'd make me do it."
- **A temp check of 6 → "How come?" → binary challenge.** "Something you want to do now, or keep researching another year?" The 6 became a real 8, in his words: "I just needed somebody to ask me like that."
- **"Still researching" became the recap and the joke.** His phrase, used three times, always lightly.
- **"If YouTube is genuinely all you need, he'll tell you."** An honest out for the person who raised the objection.
