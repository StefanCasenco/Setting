## What to notice

- **The wrong person answers.** Be polite, say who you are and what it's about simply, and offer to call back. Don't pitch the spouse.
- **"Of course he did."** Don't defend. "Sounds like this isn't the first one." Agree with the spouse lightly. She'll matter later.
- **Use what she said, with a laugh.** "That this is idea number three." It's honest and it breaks the ice with Greg.
- **His past failures told you the roadblock:** start, get excited, stall, quit. That's accountability, in his words.
- **"That's a her question."** Money is a joint decision, so the spouse goes on the call. Hand her the hardest question to ask the closer.
