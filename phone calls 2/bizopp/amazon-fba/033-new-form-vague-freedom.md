# 033 · Amazon FBA · New form · Vague: "I just want freedom"

**Prospect:** Heather, 35, pharmaceutical sales rep, Charlotte · **Lead:** form filled 30 minutes ago · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Heather, how are you? It's Stefan from Adam's team. You put your info in about Amazon a little bit ago. What made you fill it out?

**[H]:** Hi! Honestly, I just want freedom.

**[S]:** Freedom from what?

**[H]:** *(laughs)* Everything? I don't know. Just freedom.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Okay. What are you doing for work right now?

**[H]:** Pharma sales. I drive around to doctors' offices.

**[S]:** And have you sold anything online before?

**[H]:** No. Never.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** So when you say freedom, what would that look like, day to day?

**[H]:** Not having a territory. *(laughs)* Not having a manager tracking my car's GPS.

**[S]:** They track your car?

**[H]:** It's a company car. They track mileage, call times, how long I'm in each office. If I'm parked at a Starbucks for more than twenty minutes, my manager texts me. "Everything okay?"

**[S]:** "Everything okay?"

**[H]:** Every time. *(laughs)* It's not a question. It's a warning.

**[S]:** How long has it been like that?

**[H]:** The new manager started last year. Before that it was fine.

**[S]:** And how's it been affecting you?

**[H]:** I feel like a kid. I'm 35. I have a master's. And I can't get a coffee without someone checking on me. And honestly, it's affecting my marriage a little. I come home and I'm just… snippy. My husband asked me last week if I was okay. And I just started crying. In the kitchen. Over nothing.

**[S]:** Over nothing.

**[H]:** Well. Not nothing. *(pause)* It's the GPS. It's the "everything okay." It builds up.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If Amazon worked out, where would you want to be in a year?

**[H]:** I make good money in pharma. Like ninety-five. So I'd need something real. Like five thousand a month, to make the jump.

**[S]:** And what would making that jump mean for you?

**[H]:** That nobody tracks my car. *(laughs)* No, honestly, that my time is my time. If I want to get coffee for an hour, I get coffee for an hour. That's it. That's freedom.

**[S]:** Why's that important now?

**[H]:** Because we're trying for a baby. And I don't want to be pregnant, driving two hundred miles a day, with someone texting me "everything okay."

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped you from starting something like this already?

**[H]:** Honestly, I don't know where to start. Amazon seems huge. There's a million YouTube videos, and they all say something different.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[H]:** Someone to tell me what to do first. And second. And third.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[H]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[H]:** The baby. And the crying in the kitchen. That's not me.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes in the next six months?

**[H]:** Same car, same GPS, maybe pregnant. *(pause)* No. That can't be it.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. You said about ninety-five. What's that monthly, after taxes?

**[H]:** About sixty-two hundred.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[H]:** About fifteen hundred. We're good savers.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[H]:** About forty thousand, between us.

**[S]:** And with Amazon, it takes money to make money: inventory, launch, ads. What would you feel okay setting aside?

**[H]:** Ten? Maybe. I'd want to talk to my husband.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, freedom means nobody tracking your car. You've got a master's, your manager texts "everything okay?" if you're at Starbucks over twenty minutes, and it built up until you cried in the kitchen. You're trying for a baby, and you don't want to be pregnant driving two hundred miles a day. Next step would be a Zoom with Daniel, one of Adam's head coaches. He'll map that out with you, step by step, and your husband should be on it too. Evenings?

**[H]:** After 6. Not from the car. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Not from the car. Tuesday at 6:30 or Wednesday at 7?

**[H]:** Wednesday.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 7. Best email? Personal one.

**[H]:** Definitely personal. *(gives it)*

## What to notice

- **"I just want freedom" → "Freedom from what?"** Three words, and the vague goal becomes something real. Then "What would that look like, day to day?"
- **"They track your car?"** React like a normal person to a mad detail. That's curious tone, and it opened the GPS story.
- **"Over nothing." "Well. Not nothing."** Repeating her own downplaying word made her correct it herself.
- **For a high earner, the goal has to be real (five K), and the money question can be direct.** She's in Scott's "higher income" qualified box.
- **The why-now was a life event** (trying for a baby). "Why's that important now?" finds it.
