## What to notice

- **First pushback ("why do you need to know that?"): give the reason, calm and short.** "So I know what's realistic. I want to understand the gap."
- **Second, stronger ("not your business"): don't back down, and don't get defensive.** "None taken, I get it." Then Atefur's reason lines: not asking for the sake of it, the personal trainer, "it takes money to make money", the person with $200 and debt. Then ask again, smaller: "Just a rough figure."
- **Third ("why does total matter?"): explain cash flow vs baseline.** That's Atefur's #5 wording. "Some people come in with fifty K, some with two."
- **Tone stays the same all the way through.** No stutter, no apology. Atefur: don't ask it like you're scared.
- **He got all the way through money because the pain (the house, her birthday) came first.** If the money fight is huge, you probably skipped the discovery.
