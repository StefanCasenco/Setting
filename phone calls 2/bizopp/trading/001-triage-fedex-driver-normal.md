# 001 · Trading · Booked a slot (triage) · FedEx driver, normal but all over the place

**Prospect:** Marcus, 34, FedEx delivery driver, Houston · **Lead:** booked a 3pm call from an Instagram ad · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Marcus, how you doing, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. I got you down for 3 o'clock about trading, so perfect timing. What got you booking in?

**[M]:** Oh, hey, yeah, my bad, hold on— *(truck beeping)* —I'm finishing my route, I got like three stops left. I can talk though. Uh, yeah, honestly I been watching Ray's stuff for a minute. He breaks it down simple, you know? Not like these other dudes yelling in a Lambo.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* No Lambos. So what's got you watching trading content in the first place?

**[M]:** Man, I just want something that's mine. I been driving for FedEx six years. It's cool, the money's decent, but I'm not trying to be throwing boxes at 50.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Six years. And is FedEx full-time for you?

**[S]:** Have you tried any trading yet, or is it all been watching so far?

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** He thinks Daddy doesn't want to play.

**[M]:** Yeah. *(pause)* That messed with me, for real.

**[S]:** How did that leave you feeling, laying there?

**[M]:** Like… like everything's riding on my body. If my back goes for real, what we got? Nothing. That's the scary part.

**[S]:** How long have you been feeling like that?

**[M]:** Since August, for real for real. Before that I was just kinda like, yeah, I'll figure something out.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** So if trading went the way you want, where would you want to be six to twelve months from now?

**[S]:** Why's that important right now, not in a couple years?

**[S]:** Oh, congrats, man!

## What to notice

- **The triage open is easy, so don't overcomplicate it.** "I got you down for 3 o'clock. What got you booking in?" No frame speech. He was already talking.
- **The Peloton story came from one impact question:** "What's been going on that's got you thinking about that?" The setter never said "your back must hurt". Marcus said "he thinks Daddy don't wanna play."
- **The goal had a "why now" built in.** "Why's that important right now?" got the baby. That's the event-based turning point.
- **The roadblock in his words:** "somebody showing me, step by step." That's guidance. The setter asked "what do you think it'd take?" and didn't suggest anything.
- **The money question was asked confidently, with a reason** ("with a baby on the way"). The nine grand being baby money got noted, not pushed. It's the closer's conversation, so the wife was invited.
