## What to notice

- **Soft qualification caught it in under a minute.** "Senior in college, or high school?" A neutral follow-up, no accusation.
- **"Appreciate you being honest, that's actually a good sign."** He lied on the form. Thank him for coming clean anyway, don't scold him.
- **Age is a hard line. Say why simply:** "It's a legal thing, with contracts and payments." Not "you're too young to be serious".
- **"Even if my parents say it's okay?" "Even then."** Don't negotiate on it, and don't sound like you're looking for a way around it.
- **Leave him better off.** A paper-trading journal, the free playlist, and a check-in after his birthday. That's a real lead in four months.
