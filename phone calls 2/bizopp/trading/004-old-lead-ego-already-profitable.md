# 004 · Trading · Old lead, 8 months · Ego: "I'm already profitable"

**Prospect:** Jake, 31, sells HVAC systems, Denver · **Lead:** applied in March, never booked · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked
**The punch:** he says he doesn't need help. The setter doesn't argue, and lets Jake's own numbers make the point.

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Jake, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. This is kind of a random one. You applied back in March about getting help with trading and never got a proper call. Where are you at with it now?

**[J]:** Oh, man. *(laughs)* March. Yeah, honestly, I'm good now. I'm profitable. I figured it out.

**[S]:** Oh nice, congrats, man. That's more than most people can say. What are you trading?

**[J]:** Futures, mostly. NQ. Scalping the open.

**[S]:** I'm gonna be honest, I'm not the trader on the team, so I won't pretend I can talk NQ with you. *(laughs)* How long you been profitable?

**[J]:** Like… the last two months. Green two months straight.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Two months green. And are you trading full-time, or alongside work?

**[J]:** Alongside. I sell HVAC. Residential. Commission.

**[S]:** What does profitable look like for you, like a rough number?

**[J]:** I mean, I'm up like eleven hundred over the two months. On a 50K funded account.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Okay, solid. And before those two months, how was it going?

**[J]:** *(laughs)* Rough. I blew a couple funded accounts. Like… four. Over the summer.

**[S]:** Four. And what happened with those?

**[J]:** Same thing every time. I'm up, I'm up, and then one day I size up 'cause I'm feeling good, and I give it all back in an hour. Classic.

**[S]:** So what's different about the last two months?

**[J]:** I've just been… tighter. Smaller size.

**[S]:** And how confident are you that it holds, if you had a bad week?

**[J]:** *(pause)* Honestly? Not super. That's always when it happens. A bad day, and I go revenge mode.

**[S]:** When's the last time you went revenge mode?

**[J]:** *(laughs)* …Last Thursday. But I didn't blow it. I stopped at the daily limit.

**[S]:** And how did that feel on Thursday?

**[J]:** Like crap. I was sitting in my truck in the parking lot of a sales call. I was trading on my phone before the appointment. Lost three hundred. Then I went in and bombed the sales call, 'cause I was so in my head.

**[S]:** You bombed the sales call too.

**[J]:** Yeah. That's like a twelve-thousand-dollar job. My manager was not pleased.

**[S]:** How long has trading been messing with your head like that?

**[J]:** Honestly, since I started. Two years.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** So where do you actually want this to be, six to twelve months from now?

**[J]:** I want to be pulling like five K a month in payouts. Consistent. Then I quit sales.

**[S]:** And what would quitting sales mean for you?

**[J]:** No more sitting in people's kitchens telling them their furnace is dying. *(laughs)* And no more being on the road. I'm in the truck like fifty hours a week. My girlfriend says she's dating my F-150.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Dating the F-150. Why's it important now?

**[J]:** We just got engaged. We want to buy a house next year. And my income's all over the place. Lenders hate commission.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** So you've had two good months, and four blown accounts before that. What do you think's actually been stopping you from getting to that five K consistently?

**[J]:** Me. *(laughs)* Psychology. I know the setups. I just don't follow my rules when I'm down.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for you to actually follow them?

**[J]:** *(pause)* Somebody watching, honestly. Somebody I gotta answer to. 'Cause on my own, I'll just tell myself it's fine.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is it to you to get that consistent before the house?

**[J]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[J]:** Because I'm getting married and I don't want to be the guy who's gambling the house money on his phone in a parking lot.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a ten?

**[J]:** Pride, honestly. I feel like I should be able to do it myself. I applied in March and then I didn't book, 'cause I was like, nah, I got this.

**[S]:** And eight months later?

**[J]:** *(laughs)* Two months green, four accounts blown. Yeah. Okay. Point taken.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** So, five K a month in payouts. What's HVAC bringing in for you, on average?

**[J]:** Averaged out, like seven K. Some months ten, some four.

**[S]:** And how much are you putting aside each month?

**[J]:** Probably a grand. Minus challenge fees. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* How much have you spent on challenges this year?

**[J]:** Ugh. Probably twenty-five hundred.

**[S]:** And in total, what have you got saved?

**[J]:** Like twenty-two grand. Most of it's house down payment.

**[S]:** Okay. And outside of house money, what's set aside for trading capital or getting help with it?

**[J]:** Maybe five. I could do five.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've had two green months, but four funded accounts blown this year, and last Thursday you went revenge mode in a parking lot and bombed a twelve-thousand-dollar sale because of it. You know the setups. What you said you need is somebody watching, somebody you have to answer to, so you follow your rules before the house and the wedding. Next step would be a Zoom with Kev, one of Ray's head coaches. He was a funded trader himself. He'll look at your journal and the blown accounts and show you what accountability would actually look like. Does tomorrow evening work, or is Saturday better?

**[J]:** Saturday morning. Before the futures close.

**[S]:** Saturday's markets are closed, man. *(laughs)*

**[J]:** *(laughs)* See, this is why I need help. Saturday at 10.

**[S]:** Saturday at 10. Best email?

**[J]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Check it and hit accept for me.

**[J]:** Done.

**[S]:** And Jake, bring the journal, including Thursday. Speak Saturday.

## What to notice

- **"I'm already profitable."** "Congrats, that's more than most can say," then curious questions: how long, what before, what's different. He told on himself with two months green and four blown.
- **No arguing, no "are you sure?"** The question that cracked it was soft: "How confident are you that it holds if you had a bad week?"
- **The parking lot story is the North Star.** Trading cost him a twelve-thousand-dollar sale. One real pain, dug straight down.
- **Pride as the roadblock.** "What's holding it back from a 10?" → "Pride… I didn't book in March." Then "And eight months later?" He made the point himself.
- **Don't fake expertise with an ego trader.** "I'm not the trader on the team." Honesty beats bluffing, and he respected it.
