## What to notice

- **No pain, no urgency, nothing to fix.** He's 24, likes his job, lives at home, and could already afford Bali. There's nothing for a closer to work with.
- **"Can you not go to Bali now?"** A light test that shows the goal isn't blocked by anything. He laughed and admitted it.
- **A temp check of 4 → "How come it's only a 4?" → binary challenge.** "Something you're looking to change now, or someday?" He picked someday, out loud.
- **The future-pace confirmed it.** "Honestly? Fine." That's Scott's "is that what you're willing to settle for?", and the answer was yes. So let him go: no boxing match.
- **An interest DQ, said kindly.** It protects the closer's calendar and your show rate, and the door stays open.
