## What to notice

- **Callbacks start confused, so re-establish fast.** Your name, the owner's full name, what they filled out, when. "Ray who?" is normal. Don't take it personally.
- **"I thought you was the dentist."** Laugh with him, then get straight back to the open question.
- **The strike story is the impact.** One question ("what's going on that's got you watching trading at lunch?") and he told the 401k story himself.
- **"What are people saying on the line?"** A curious question about his world, not about feelings. Some guys open up more with facts than with "how does that feel."
- **He protected the emergency money himself.** Always write down what's off-limits for the closer.
