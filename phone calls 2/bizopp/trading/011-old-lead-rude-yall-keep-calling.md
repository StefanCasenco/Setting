# 011 · Trading · Old lead, 3 months · Rude: "Y'all keep calling me"

**Prospect:** Rick, 45, truck dispatcher, Dallas · **Lead:** opted into a masterclass in August. Other companies have been blowing up his phone · **Money:** n/a · **Ends:** not booked, clean exit. He texts back later.

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Rick, how's it going? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You signed up for Ray's trading masterclass back in Aug—

**[R]:** Man, are you kidding me? Y'all keep calling me! This is the fifth one this week! I signed up for ONE thing!

**[S]:** Rick, I hear you, man. Five in a week, I'd be pissed too. I'm only calling from Ray's team, I don't know who the others are. If you want me to take you off right now, I'll do it, no problem.

**[R]:** Yeah, take me off. And tell whoever's selling my number to quit.

**[S]:** Done, you're off our list. And we don't sell numbers, but I get why it feels like it. Sorry you've been getting hammered.

**[R]:** *(pause)* …Yeah. Whatever. It's not you, it's just… every day. One guy called me at 10 at night.

**[S]:** 10 at night is crazy. Honestly, that's not okay.

**[R]:** *(sighs)* Look, I'm at work. I got trucks on the road. I don't have time for this.

**[S]:** No problem at all. I'll let you go. I'm going to send you one text with my name, so if you ever want to talk about the trading stuff, it's me and nobody else. And if not, you'll never hear from us again. Cool?

**[R]:** Fine. Whatever.

**[S]:** Have a good one, Rick.

*(Call ends.)*

---

*(The setter sends one text:)*
> "Hey Rick, Stefan from Ray's team. You're off our call list like you asked. Sorry about all the calls. If you ever want to chat about the trading stuff, text me here. If not, all good. Take care."

*(Two days later, Rick texts back:)*
> "appreciate that. you were the only one that didnt argue. what does rays thing actually cost"

*(The setter replies:)*
> "Appreciate you saying that Rick. Honestly depends on where you're at, that's what the call's for. No pressure. Want me to give you a ring this week when you're not dispatching? What time's quiet for you?"

---

## What to notice

- **Never defend against anger.** "Five in a week, I'd be pissed too." Agree with the feeling straight away.
- **Do what he asks, immediately.** "Done, you're off our list." Don't try to sneak in a question first. That's what the other five callers did.
- **"It's not you."** That's the moment the anger drops, and you still don't push. He's at work and said he doesn't have time, so let him go.
- **One text, with a clear opt-out.** It's respectful and short. That text is what brought him back two days later.
- **Sometimes the win is the exit.** The setter who didn't argue is the only one he called back.
