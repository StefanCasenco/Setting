## What to notice

- **When they lie, don't call it out. Give them a soft way to correct it.** "Did it say 'waiting for host,' or did it just not load?" A specific, neutral question. He came clean by himself.
- **"Appreciate you telling me, I've done it to my dentist."** No judgment, so he gave the real reason (his wife didn't know).
- **Recap the first call in his words.** Seven years as a teller, loans he'll never qualify for, his wife going part-time. He doesn't have to explain again, and he remembers why he booked.
- **The real objection was the spouse.** Her own line ("I want to hear it from them") became the plan: she's on the call.
- **One-word texts as the escape hatch.** It makes it easy to reschedule instead of ghosting again.
