## What to notice

- **"I'm just looking" got Scott's exact check:** "Is trading something you're actually looking to get into, or just having a look around?" That binary is on purpose. It makes her pick a side, and she picked "get into it".
- **The coworker was the way in.** "How did that land with you?" got jealousy, five years vs two, and the fifty-cent raise. That's the impact, in her words.
- **"Not for people like me."** That's a limiting belief. Ask "what do you mean?", let her say it, then a light reframe ("Jess is a girl from Columbus who cleans teeth"). Don't lecture.
- **"Like a 7."** Get her reason for the 7 first, then what's stopping a 9. The thing stopping a 9 is what you flag for the closer (fear of losing money).
- **The "car dies" money was respected.** "I'm guessing it's strictly car money?" She didn't commit, and that's fine. The setter's job is to know, not to win.
