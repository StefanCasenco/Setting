# 008 · Trading · Application · "Are you a bot? Are you reading a script?"

**Prospect:** Kevin O., 28, bartender, Chicago · **Lead:** application last night · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Kevin, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You put an application in last night about trading. What made you apply?

**[K]:** *(pause)* Wait. Is this a real person?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Last I checked. Real guy, sitting at a desk with a coffee that's gone cold.

**[K]:** *(laughs)* Bro, I've had like three AI calls this week. One of them asked me about my car's extended warranty and I don't even have a car.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Chicago, no car. I respect it. Nah, I'm real. So what made you apply?

**[K]:** Honestly, I bartend. I make good tips, but I'm 28 and I'm like, what am I doing.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Where at?

**[K]:** River North. Cocktail bar.

**[S]:** Nice. And have you done any trading yet?

**[K]:** Yeah, some. Options, mostly. I'm kinda all over the place.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** "What am I doing." What do you mean by that?

**[K]:** Like… okay, you're gonna think this is dumb.

**[S]:** Try me.

**[K]:** I went to my ten-year high school reunion in August. And everyone's like, "I'm a nurse, I'm in marketing, I just bought a condo." And I'm like, "I make really good old fashioneds." *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* And how did that feel, standing there?

**[K]:** Honestly? Like crap. I laughed it off. But I went home and didn't sleep.

**[K]:** Okay, wait. Are you reading off a script or something? You keep asking "how did that feel."

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I've got notes, man, I'm not gonna lie to you. Everybody on the team does. But I'm not reading you a script. I'm just actually trying to understand what's going on with you before I put you in front of someone. If I booked you in without knowing any of this, I'd be wasting your time.

**[K]:** *(pause)* …Okay. Fair. That's actually a better answer than I expected.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* So you didn't sleep after the reunion.

**[K]:** Nah. And it's not even about them. It's that I'm working till 3am four nights a week, and I'm good at it, but it's not going anywhere. There's no promotion at a bar. You're a bartender or you own the bar.

**[S]:** And the options trading, how's that been going?

**[K]:** Up and down. I made like two grand in March. Then I lost three in April. Then I made some back. Right now I'm down like fifteen hundred on the year.

**[S]:** How long you been at it?

**[K]:** Year and a half.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If it went the way you want, where would you be in a year?

**[K]:** Making like four, five grand a month. Out of the bar. Or at least down to two nights.

**[S]:** And what would that actually mean for you?

**[K]:** Normal hours. I could actually date someone who doesn't work nights. *(laughs)* Every girl I meet works in a bar. And I want to be able to say I do something. Like, at the next reunion.

**[S]:** Why's it important now?

**[K]:** 'Cause I'm turning 29 in January, and I told myself I'd be out of bartending by 30.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've been trading a year and a half, and you're down fifteen hundred. What do you think's been stopping you?

**[K]:** I learn from TikTok. *(laughs)* That's the problem. I see a guy hit on a call, I copy it. I don't have, like, a system.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for you to actually have one?

**[K]:** Someone to actually teach me one. And tell me when I'm being an idiot.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is it to you to be out of the bar by 30?

**[K]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[K]:** The reunion, honestly. And I'm tired of being the fun guy at 4am.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes in the next six months?

**[K]:** I'm 29, still pouring drinks, still down on options. Yeah. No.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money ones, then. What do you make bartending a month, roughly?

**[K]:** Depends. Like five grand in a good month. Four in a slow one.

**[S]:** And you know how it is in Chicago, rent's crazy. How much are you able to put aside each month?

**[K]:** Like a grand. Then I lose it on options. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* And in total, saved?

**[K]:** Like six K. Plus whatever's in the brokerage, like three.

**[S]:** And you know in trading it takes money to make money. If you did it properly, how much would you set aside as capital?

**[K]:** Probably the three in the account, plus some.

**[S]:** So from what you said, you're working till 3am four nights a week and you're good at it, but there's nowhere to go. At the reunion everybody had something and you went home and didn't sleep. You've been trading options a year and a half off TikTok and you're down fifteen hundred, and you want to be out of the bar by 30. What you said you need is someone to teach you an actual system and tell you when you're being an idiot. Next step would be a Zoom with Kev, one of Ray's head coaches, a real person too. *(laughs)* He'll look at your trades and map out what a system would look like for you. Since you work nights, is early afternoon better, like 1 or 2?

**[K]:** 2. I'm up by noon.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 2, or Friday at 2?

**[K]:** Tomorrow.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 2. Best email?

**[K]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** Sent. Check it and hit accept, and I promise it's not from a bot. *(laughs)*

**[K]:** *(laughs)* Got it. Accepted.

**[S]:** Anything that could come up tomorrow at 2?

**[K]:** Nah. I'll be up. I'll be on coffee.

**[S]:** Perfect. Bring your brokerage history. Speak tomorrow, Kev.

## What to notice

- **"Is this a real person?"** Answer with a joke and a human detail ("a coffee that's gone cold"). Never get offended.
- **"Are you reading off a script?"** Be honest: "I've got notes… everybody does. But I'm not reading you a script." Then the reason for the questions. He said, "That's a better answer than I expected." Denying it makes you sound more scripted.
- **After the interruption, go back to his exact words.** "So you didn't sleep after the reunion." It shows you were really listening.
- **The reunion was the pain**, and the 30 deadline was the why-now, both in his words.
- **Booking around his schedule:** a bartender gets an afternoon slot, not 9am.
