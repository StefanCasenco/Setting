# 006 · Trading · Registered for the webinar, didn't show · "I forgot, my bad"

**Prospect:** Carlos, 38, roofer, owns a small crew, Phoenix · **Lead:** signed up for Tuesday's webinar, didn't join · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Carlos, what's up, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You signed up for Ray's webinar Tuesday, and I didn't see you on. *(laughs)* What happened?

**[C]:** Ah, man, my bad. I completely forgot. We had a job go long. I was on a roof till like 7.

**[S]:** On a roof till 7, in Phoenix? That sounds brutal.

**[C]:** It's October, so it's only like 95. *(laughs)* Summer's the real one.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Only 95. So what made you sign up for a trading webinar, in between roofs?

**[C]:** Honestly, my nephew. He does it. He's 22, and he's always showing me his phone, "Tío, look." And I'm like, okay, maybe there's something there.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** And you've got your own roofing business?

**[C]:** Yeah, small crew. Me and four guys. Eleven years.

**[S]:** Eleven years running your own crew. And have you tried trading yourself yet?

**[C]:** Nah. My nephew set me up an app. I haven't put nothing in.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** So what's going on with the roofing that's got you looking at something else?

**[C]:** It's good money. Don't get me wrong. Good years, I clear a hundred. But it's my body, man. I'm 38 and my knees sound like Rice Krispies. *(laughs)* And if I'm not on the roof, the guys mess it up. I can't be off.

**[S]:** "If I'm not on the roof, the guys mess it up." What happens when you're off?

**[C]:** I took a week in June for my daughter's quinceañera. One week. Came back, two callbacks for leaks and one guy quit. I lost like eight grand that week.

**[S]:** Eight grand for one week off. How did that feel, at your daughter's quinceañera?

**[C]:** *(pause)* I was on the phone half the party. My wife was so mad. My daughter didn't say nothing, but… yeah. I was in the parking lot yelling at a guy about flashing while she was doing the father-daughter dance practice.

**[S]:** You missed the practice.

**[C]:** I missed the practice. I made the real one. But yeah.

**[S]:** How long have you felt like the business owns you like that?

**[C]:** Honestly? The last three, four years. Since I got bigger.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If trading worked out, what would you want it doing in a year?

**[C]:** Honestly, I don't need it to replace roofing. If it made like three, four grand a month, I could hire a foreman. Pay a real guy to run the crew. Then I'm not on the roof every day.

**[S]:** And what would having a foreman mean for you?

**[C]:** I could be home. My son plays baseball. Saturdays. I've been to like two games this year.

**[S]:** Why's that important right now?

**[C]:** He's 12. In like three years he's not gonna want me there anyway. *(laughs)* You know how it is.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** So what do you think's been stopping you from setting that up already?

**[C]:** Time. I'm on roofs. When am I gonna learn trading?

**[S]:** That's fair. And if it's just a time thing, why not just hire the foreman out of the roofing money, instead of getting into trading at all?

**[C]:** *(pause)* …'Cause the margins are thin, man. If I pay a foreman sixty, seventy K, that's my profit. I need something on the side to cover it.

**[S]:** Makes sense. So it's not just time. What do you think it'd take for you to actually make trading work with the time you've got?

**[C]:** Somebody who knows what they're doing to show me something simple. Not my nephew. He's on his phone eight hours a day. I can't do that.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is getting that foreman money?

**[C]:** Like a 7.

**[S]:** What makes it a 7?

**[C]:** The knees. And the baseball.

**[S]:** And what's stopping it from being a 9 or 10?

**[C]:** I don't know if I even have the time to learn. Like, for real.

**[S]:** What changed now that made you sign up for the webinar, if time's always been the problem?

**[C]:** *(pause)* My doctor. Last month. He said if I keep going like this I'm looking at knee surgery by 45.

**[S]:** That's a big thing to hear.

**[C]:** Yeah. It woke me up.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple quick ones on the money side. What do you take home a month from the business, on average?

**[C]:** Me personally? Like seven, eight K.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[C]:** Like two grand. Some months nothing, when materials go up.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[C]:** Personally, like forty K. Plus the business account.

**[S]:** And in trading it takes money to make money. How much would you say you'd set aside in terms of capital to get started?

**[C]:** Ten, fifteen K, if I knew what I was doing.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've got your own crew, eleven years, good money. But it's all on your body and your knees, and your doctor said surgery by 45. You took one week for the quince and lost eight grand, and you were in the parking lot during the dance practice. You've been to two of your son's games this year. You want three, four K a month from trading so you can hire a foreman and be home, and what you said you need is somebody to show you something simple that fits around roofs. Next step would be a Zoom with Kev, one of Ray's head coaches. He'll look at your schedule and map out something realistic for the time you've actually got. Is evenings better for you, or Sundays?

**[C]:** Sunday. No roofs Sunday.

**[S]:** Kev's got Sunday at 11 or 1.

**[C]:** 1. After church.

**[S]:** Sunday at 1. Best email?

**[C]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it for me. And since you forgot the webinar *(laughs)*, I'm gonna text you Saturday night too.

**[C]:** *(laughs)* Yeah, please. Do that.

**[S]:** Anything Sunday at 1 that could come up? Emergency leak?

**[C]:** If it's Sunday, they can wait till Monday.

**[S]:** Love it. Speak Sunday, Carlos.

---

## What to notice

- **The no-show open is light, with a laugh.** "I didn't see you on. What happened?" No guilt. The answer (on a roof till 7) became the first thing to dig into.
- **Atefur's time move:** "If it's just a time thing, why not just hire the foreman out of the roofing money?" He had to explain the real reason (thin margins). It wasn't only time.
- **"What changed now… if time's always been the problem?"** That's the why-now when they've wanted it a while. It got the doctor and knee surgery by 45.
- **Business owners often have more money than you think.** Ask the capital question straight. He said ten to fifteen K without blinking.
- **Name the no-show risk with a laugh.** "Since you forgot the webinar, I'm gonna text you Saturday."
