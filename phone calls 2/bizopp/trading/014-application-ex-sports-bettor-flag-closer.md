# 014 · Trading · Application · Ex sports bettor, 2 years clean

**Prospect:** Luis, 35, blackjack dealer at a casino, Las Vegas · **Lead:** application from a Facebook ad · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked, with a flag for the closer

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Luis, how you doing, man? It's Stefan from Ray's team. You put an application in about trading. What made you apply?

**[L]:** Hey. Yeah. Honestly, I've been thinking about it for a while. I'm a dealer, I see money all day, I understand odds. Thought I'd be good at it.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** A dealer, where at?

**[L]:** Downtown. Fremont Street. Eight years.

**[S]:** And have you traded at all yet?

**[L]:** Not really. Some paper trading. I want to do it right.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** "Do it right." What do you mean by that?

**[L]:** *(pause)* Okay, I'm just gonna be upfront with you, 'cause I'd rather say it now. I used to have a problem with sports betting. Bad. Like, I lost about forty grand over four years. I've been clean two years. GA meetings, all that.

**[S]:** Luis, I really appreciate you telling me that. Seriously. Two years is a big deal.

**[L]:** Thanks. It is. My wife… she stayed with me through it. Which she didn't have to.

**[S]:** And how's she feel about you looking at trading?

**[L]:** Nervous. Honestly. That's why I want to do it right. Not the "YOLO options" stuff. I know that's just betting with a different app.

**[S]:** You can tell the difference.

**[L]:** I can tell the difference better than anybody. *(laughs)* I see it at my table every night. Guy's up three grand, he doubles. Every time.

**[S]:** So what's got you looking at it now, two years in?

**[L]:** We're still paying off the debt from back then. Like twelve grand left. And I'm 35, we want a kid, and the dealer money's tips, it's up and down. I want something steady that I built the right way. Not the casino. Not betting. Something with rules.

**[S]:** How long have you been thinking about it?

**[L]:** About six months. My sponsor said wait till two years before anything like this. So I waited.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If you did it the right way, where would you want to be in a year?

**[L]:** Honestly, small. A thousand a month. Consistent. Help pay the debt off faster.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[L]:** That I fixed what I broke. *(pause)* That's the real answer.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's a big one.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for you to do it the right way?

**[L]:** Rules. Somebody who sets hard rules with me, risk limits, and actually checks I follow them. I can't be my own accountability on money. Not yet. Maybe not ever.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is this to you?

**[L]:** Seven. I'm not in a rush. Rushing's how I got in trouble.

**[S]:** That makes complete sense. What makes it a seven and not lower?

**[L]:** The baby. And the debt. I want it gone before we have a kid.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, and with what you told me I'll be careful. What's coming in each month, roughly?

**[L]:** Base plus tips, like forty-five hundred. My wife's a teacher, another thirty-two.

**[S]:** And are you able to put anything aside, with the debt?

**[L]:** Like five hundred after the debt payment.

**[S]:** And savings?

**[L]:** Six grand. My wife controls it. *(laughs)* On purpose. That's part of my recovery.

**[S]:** That's smart. Honestly. So if you did start trading, it'd be a decision with her?

**[L]:** Has to be. She's on everything.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you're two years clean from sports betting, still paying off about twelve grand, and you want a thousand a month, steady and built the right way, to clear it before a baby. You know YOLO options is just betting on a different app. What you said you need is hard rules and somebody checking you follow them. Next step would be a Zoom with Kev, one of Ray's head coaches. He'll tell you straight if trading is a good idea for you right now, and if it isn't, he'll say. And I'd like your wife on the call, since she's on the money. How does that sound?

**[S]:** Nothing weird about it, man. When are you both home?

**[L]:** I'm on swing shift. Afternoons before 3, Tuesday or Wednesday.

**[S]:** Kev's got Tuesday at 11 or Wednesday at 1.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 1. Best email?

## What to notice

- **When someone shares something heavy, thank them and slow down.** "I really appreciate you telling me. Two years is a big deal." No therapy, no skipping past it.
- **His own line was the impact:** "That I fixed what I broke." The setter said "That's a big one" and moved on.
- **Flag it to the closer and tell the prospect you're doing it.** It protects him, it protects the business, and it builds trust ("thank you for not being weird").
- **A temp check of 7 isn't low here.** "Rushing's how I got in trouble." Some 7s are healthy. Don't push a recovering gambler to say 10.
- **The setter doesn't decide if trading is safe for him.** That's for the closer, with his wife on the call. "If it isn't, he'll say."
