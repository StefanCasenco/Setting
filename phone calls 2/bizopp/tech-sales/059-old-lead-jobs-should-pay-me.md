## What to notice

- **"Jobs should pay me."** Agree first ("a lot of people feel that way"), then ask permission: "Can I ask you something, though?"
- **Use her numbers, not your argument.** "How many weekends off since August?" "Two." "How many interviews from the free applications?" "Zero." She sees the cost of the free way herself.
- **Then say what actually costs money,** and give the out: "If Jess thinks you can do it without that, she'll tell you."
- **"High-five in the hallway."** A funny line with real pain in it. Use it in the recap.
- **Expect retail managers to get called in on days off.** "Text me and we'll move it" saves the booking.
