# 058 · Tech sales · New form · Laid-off tech recruiter, proud and a bit defensive

**Prospect:** Marcus R., 35, tech recruiter for 7 years, laid off 4 months ago, Atlanta · **Lead:** form filled 25 minutes ago · **Money:** program, careful · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Marcus, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Chris's team. You just put your info in about tech sales. What made you fill it out?

**[M]:** Hey. Honestly, just exploring options. I'm between roles right now.

**[S]:** Totally get it. What were you doing before?

**[M]:** Tech recruiting. Seven years. I placed SDRs and AEs, actually. So I know the space. *(laughs)* I probably know what hiring managers want better than most people in your program.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* You probably know more about SDR hiring than I do. So what had you filling out the form, if you know the space that well?

**[M]:** *(pause)* …I've been thinking about switching to sales myself. Before the layoffs. I saw the AEs I placed making double what I made.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** When did the layoff happen?

**[S]:** And how's the search going?

**[M]:** It's fine. Tough market. I'm being picky.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Picky how?

**[M]:** *(pause)* …Honestly? It's not going great. Recruiting jobs don't exist right now. And sales jobs see "recruiter" and think I'm an HR guy, not a closer. Which is crazy, because recruiting is sales. I sold candidates on jobs every single day.

**[S]:** Recruiting is sales. I agree with you completely. That's got to be frustrating.

**[M]:** It's maddening. I've placed people who were worse than me in jobs I can't even get an interview for.

**[S]:** How are you holding up, honestly?

**[M]:** *(long pause)* My severance ran out last month. My wife's a nurse. She's picking up extra shifts.

**[S]:** And how does that feel?

**[M]:** Bad, man. I'm supposed to be the one who… *(stops)* Yeah. It's bad.

**[S]:** Yeah. I hear you.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If you landed in sales, where would you want to be?

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[M]:** That she can drop the extra shifts. And I'm not the guy at the barbecue saying "I'm exploring options."

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You know the market. What do you think's actually stopping you from getting those interviews?

**[M]:** Positioning. My resume says recruiter. I need it to say "seven years in a quota-carrying role." Which it was, I just never framed it like that. And I've never done a sales interview from the candidate side. *(laughs)* I've only been on the other side of the table.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[M]:** Someone to reposition me, and run mock interviews. Honestly, I'm too close to it to see my own resume clearly.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[M]:** Her extra shifts.

## What to notice

- **"Just exploring, between roles."** That's pride. Let him be the expert ("you probably know more about SDR hiring than I do"), then: "So what had you filling out the form, if you know it that well?"
- **"Picky how?"** A gentle follow-up on his cover word. He admitted the truth himself.
- **"Recruiting is sales. I agree with you completely."** Agreeing with his frustration puts you on his side.
- **"I'm supposed to be the one who…"** He didn't finish, and the setter didn't make him. Some pain doesn't need to be said in full.
- **Name the care before the money:** "I'll be careful here, I know it's tight." Saying it out loud is the defuse.
