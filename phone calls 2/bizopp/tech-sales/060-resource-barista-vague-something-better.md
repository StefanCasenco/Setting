## What to notice

- **"Something better" → "Better than what?"** Two words turn a vague want into a fact.
- **"It's fine… it's a job"** got silence, then the truth: "Okay, it's not fine."
- **"Give me a number."** Scott: "I want to make more money" is useless. Pull them to a tangible number, then "what would that change?"
- **"Wasted four years."** That's the North Star, said in his words.
- **"Comfortable" is the limiting belief.** The binary challenge ("change now, or think about it another three years?") used his own timeframe, so it landed.
