## What to notice

- **"Call me back in 10."** Set an exact time ("2:40?") and call at exactly that time. "You actually called back. Respect." That's trust built before a single question.
- **On the callback, start with what you know:** "You were filling out a form while your boss was around?" Curiosity about the situation got the whole story.
- **"I didn't move in with you to live alone."** Her line, repeated once. That's the North Star.
- **Respect the short break out loud.** "Quick money ones, 'cause I know your break's short." Then book on a day off, not another break.
- **The closet fund** counts too. Ask about every bit of money they mention, without pushing.
