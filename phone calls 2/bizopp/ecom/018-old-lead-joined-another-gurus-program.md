# 018 · Ecom · Old lead, 6 months · "I already joined another guy's program"

**Prospect:** Brandon, 29, mechanic, Tulsa · **Lead:** applied in May, never booked · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Brandon, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Leo's team. Random one. You applied back in May about starting an online store and never got a proper call. Where you at with it now?

**[B]:** Oh, yeah. Actually, I joined somebody else's program. In June.

**[S]:** Nice, good for you for jumping in. Most people who apply just keep scrolling. Whose program?

**[B]:** Some guy on YouTube. Fifteen hundred bucks. Dropshipping and Shopify, the whole thing.

**[S]:** And how's it going, five months in?

**[B]:** *(pause)* …Honestly? Not great.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Not great how? Where's the store at?

**[B]:** I launched in August. I've done like eleven sales. I spent about two grand on ads.

**[S]:** And are you still working as well?

**[B]:** Yeah, I'm a mechanic. Full-time. At a Firestone.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Eleven sales, two grand in ads. What's the program telling you?

**[B]:** That's the thing. It's videos. And a Discord. And when I post my ads in there, it's like… crickets. Or some guy says "test more creatives." Thanks, bro. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* "Test more creatives." Look, I'm not here to trash anybody's program, if it's working, keep going. I'm just curious. When you applied back in May, what was the reason you wanted a store in the first place?

**[B]:** *(pause)* Honestly, I'm tired of being under cars. I'm 29 and my hands look like I'm 50. And Firestone, they push you to upsell people stuff they don't need. I hate that. Old lady comes in for an oil change, and I gotta tell her she needs a four-hundred-dollar brake job she doesn't need. Or my manager writes me up.

**[S]:** You get written up for not upselling?

**[B]:** Yeah. Got written up in September. For "low ticket average." Because I wouldn't lie to a guy about his rotors.

**[S]:** How did that feel?

**[B]:** Like crap, man. I'm getting punished for being honest. My dad was a mechanic, he had his own shop, and he never did that. He'd be ashamed of that place.

**[S]:** How long has that been bugging you?

**[B]:** Like two years. Since the new manager.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** So if the store worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[B]:** Two, three K a month profit. Enough to leave Firestone. Maybe one day open my own shop like my dad. An honest one.

**[S]:** What would that mean for you?

**[B]:** That I'm not lying to old ladies for a living. *(laughs)* For real, though. I want to be proud of what I do.

**[S]:** Why's it important now?

**[B]:** 'Cause I'm burning money. I already spent thirty-five hundred between the program and ads. If I don't figure it out soon, I'm just the guy who wasted money.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've got a store, you've got some sales. What do you think's been holding it back?

**[B]:** I think my product's okay. Car stuff, like detailing kits. I think my ads suck. But nobody's ever told me why.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to fix it?

**[B]:** Somebody to actually look at it. My ads, my store, my numbers. And tell me straight what's wrong. Not a video. Not a Discord.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is it to fix this?

**[B]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[B]:** The write-up. And the thirty-five hundred I don't want to waste.

**[S]:** And what's keeping it from a ten?

**[B]:** Scared to spend more and get burned again.

**[S]:** That makes total sense.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, and I know you've already spent a lot, so I'll keep it simple. What's Firestone paying you a month?

**[B]:** Like thirty-six hundred.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside?

**[B]:** Like four hundred. It's been going into ads.

**[S]:** And in total, what have you got saved?

**[B]:** Like five grand left.

**[S]:** And in ecom it takes money to make money, you know that better than anybody now. *(laughs)* If you kept going, what would you set aside for the store?

**[B]:** *(laughs)* Yeah. Maybe two more. But that's it. I'm not draining it.

**[S]:** Smart.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you joined a program in June, launched in August, and spent about two grand on ads for eleven sales. The Discord tells you to "test more creatives." You're at Firestone getting written up for not lying to people about brakes, and your dad would be ashamed of that place. You want two, three K a month so you can leave and one day open an honest shop. What you said you need is somebody to actually look at your ads and your store and tell you straight what's wrong. Next step would be a Zoom with Nina, one of Leo's head coaches. She'll go through your store and ads live, and if what you need is just a tweak to what you're already doing, she'll tell you that too. Do evenings work, or your day off?

**[B]:** I'm off Sundays.

**[S]:** Sunday at 12 or 2?

**[B]:** 12.

**[S]:** Sunday at 12. Best email?

**[B]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it, and have your Shopify and ads manager open on the call.

**[B]:** Will do. Thanks for not trashing the other guy, honestly.

**[S]:** No need, man. Speak Sunday.

## What to notice

- **"I already joined someone else's program."** Praise the action first ("good for you for jumping in"), then ask "how's it going?" Five months and eleven sales told the story.
- **"I'm not here to trash anybody's program… I'm just curious."** That takes the frame back without attacking his choice. Then go back to the original why.
- **The Firestone write-up was the real pain.** He was punished for being honest, and his dad's shop is the identity piece. The store was never the point.
- **Money for someone already burnt:** "I know you've already spent a lot, so I'll keep it simple." Then let him set the limit himself.
- **"If what you need is just a tweak, she'll tell you."** An honest out lowers the "burned again" fear.
