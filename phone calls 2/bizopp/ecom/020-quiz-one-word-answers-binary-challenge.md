## What to notice

- **One-word answers: easy fact questions first.** "Do you have a pet? What kind? What's his name?" It warms him up without pressure. "Tank" got the first laugh.
- **Silence after "it's fine"** got "It's heavy, it's lumber." Then "If you had to guess?" after "dunno" got the real answer (his dad).
- **The binary only came at urgency, as a challenge.** "Is this something you actually want to change, or are you good just doing lumber?" That's Scott's rule: binary to make them pick a side. The 5 became a real 7.
- **Never fill in his answers for him.** It's tempting with one-word people. Scott: force them to say it.
- **Using Tank in the booking** shows you remembered and keeps it light for a nervous prospect.
