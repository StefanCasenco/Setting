## What to notice

- **When a second person joins, ask them directly.** "Mike, what did you think?" The quiet partner gets a voice and doesn't feel ambushed.
- **"Somebody in the house needs to be careful."** It respects the sceptic in one line.
- **Separate goals and separate temp checks.** Lisa's 9 and Mike's 6 are different problems. "What would get you to an eight?" told you exactly what the closer needs to show him.
- **"Lost my job as a mom."** An empty-nest identity pain. Repeat it, let him add to it, and don't push further.
- **Capital for a couple:** "A few thousand. Not twenty-two." Let them set their own limit.
