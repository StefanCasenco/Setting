# 021 · Ecom · On day 3 of the free 5-day challenge · Engaged, did the homework

**Prospect:** Angela, 40, medical biller, works from home, Grand Rapids, Michigan · **Lead:** signed up for Leo's free 5-day store challenge, did days 1 to 3 · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Angela, how are you? It's Stefan from Leo's team. I saw you're on day 3 of the challenge. How's it going?

**[A]:** Oh, hi! It's going good! I actually did the homework. *(laughs)* I picked a niche yesterday.

**[S]:** Love that, most people don't do the homework. What niche?

**[A]:** Organization stuff. Like, for small apartments. Drawer dividers, under-bed bins. I'm a little obsessed with organizing. My husband says I label the labels.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Labeling the labels. So what do you do for work?

**[A]:** Medical billing. From home. Full-time.

**[S]:** And before the challenge, had you ever sold anything online?

**[A]:** No. This is my first time trying anything like this.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** What made you sign up for the challenge?

**[A]:** Honestly, I've been doing billing for twelve years. From home since COVID. And I thought working from home would be the dream. *(laughs)* It's not. I'm in the spare bedroom, staring at insurance codes, nine hours a day. I don't talk to anybody. Some days I don't leave the house.

**[S]:** Some days you don't leave the house.

**[A]:** Like, at all. I realized last week I hadn't left in four days. Four days. I didn't even go get the mail.

**[S]:** How did that feel when you realized?

**[A]:** Kind of scary? Like I'm disappearing. My kids are teenagers, they're busy. My husband works long hours. It's just me and the codes.

**[S]:** And the billing itself, how is it?

**[A]:** Boring, and they're replacing us. They brought in AI software for coding in September. They said it's to "help" us. But two girls on my team are already gone.

**[S]:** How long have you felt like you're disappearing?

**[A]:** Probably the last year. It got worse when the girls left.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If the store worked out, where would you want it in a year?

**[A]:** Making like three thousand a month. Enough that if they let me go, we're okay.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[A]:** That I'm building something. I'm using the organizing stuff, which I actually love. And honestly? A reason to talk to people. Customers, suppliers. I'd love to do a booth at a market someday.

**[S]:** Why's it important now?

**[A]:** Because of the AI thing. I could be next. And I don't want to be 40 and starting over with nothing.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've done three days and picked a niche, so you're moving. What do you think could stop you from getting to the three thousand?

**[A]:** Days 4 and 5 are about ads and suppliers. *(laughs)* That's where I'll get stuck. I already got stuck finding a supplier yesterday. There were like a thousand on Alibaba and I don't know who's real.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to get past that?

**[A]:** Someone who's done it to tell me, "this supplier, not that one. These ads, not those." I can follow instructions, I'm a biller. *(laughs)* I just need the right ones.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is this?

**[A]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[A]:** The AI thing, and the four days.

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a ten?

**[A]:** Money, a little. And I've never done anything like this. What if I'm bad at it?

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Fair. Couple money questions. What's billing paying you a month?

**[A]:** About thirty-nine hundred.

**[S]:** And with teenagers, how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[A]:** Teenagers eat everything. *(laughs)* Maybe five hundred.

**[S]:** Usual?

**[A]:** Usual.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[A]:** About fourteen thousand. Some of that's for my daughter's car next year.

**[S]:** And in ecom it takes money to make money, for inventory and ads. Have you thought about what you'd put in?

**[A]:** Maybe three or four thousand. Not the car money.

**[S]:** Makes sense. Is this a decision with your husband?

**[A]:** He'd want to know. But he's supportive. He's the one who said, "you need something, babe."

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've been doing billing twelve years in the spare bedroom, and last week you realized you hadn't left the house in four days. You feel like you're disappearing. The AI software already took two girls on your team. You've done three days of the challenge and picked organization stuff, which you love, and you want three thousand a month so you're okay if they let you go. What you said you need is someone who's done it to tell you which supplier and which ads. Next step would be a Zoom with Nina, one of Leo's head coaches. She'll pick up where day 3 left off and map out the supplier and ads part with you. Your husband's welcome too. When's good?

**[A]:** After 5, any weekday. I'm literally always home. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Thursday at 5:30 or Friday at 5?

**[A]:** Thursday.

**[S]:** Thursday at 5:30. Same email as the challenge?

**[A]:** Same one.

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it. And keep doing day 4 and 5, so you show up with even more for Nina.

**[A]:** I will. Thank you!

**[S]:** And Angela, go get the mail today. *(laughs)*

**[A]:** *(laughs)* I will!

## What to notice

- **A challenge lead is engaged. Open on their progress.** "How's it going?" plus praise for doing the homework. She opened up straight away.
- **"Some days I don't leave the house" → "four days" → "I'm disappearing."** The impact came in three steps, each one her words.
- **Ask what *could* stop her, not "what's stopped you".** She's already moving, so the roadblock is ahead of her (days 4 and 5, suppliers).
- **"I can follow instructions, I'm a biller."** Guidance, in her words.
- **"Keep doing days 4 and 5."** Don't let the booking replace the challenge. A prospect who's moving shows up warmer.
