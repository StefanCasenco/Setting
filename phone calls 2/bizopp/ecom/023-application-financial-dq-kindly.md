# 023 · Ecom · Application · Financial disqualification, done kindly

**Prospect:** Shonda, 31, home health aide, two kids, Memphis · **Lead:** application from a TikTok ad · **Money:** capital, not there · **Ends:** financial DQ with real help

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Shonda, how are you? It's Stefan from Leo's team. You put an application in about starting an online store. What made you apply?

**[SH]:** Hi! Yeah. I been seeing these women on TikTok selling hair bundles and lashes from home, and I'm like, I need that. I need something from home.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** From home. What are you doing for work right now?

**[SH]:** Home health aide. I take care of elderly people. Like four clients.

**[S]:** And have you sold anything online before?

**[SH]:** No, ma'am. I mean, sir. *(laughs)* Sorry. No.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* No worries. So what's going on that's got you wanting something from home?

**[SH]:** My kids. They're 6 and 9. My mama watches them, but she's getting older. She's 70. She can't keep up with them anymore. And I'm driving all over Memphis to clients. I'm gone 7 to 7.

**[S]:** 7 to 7. How's that been on you and the kids?

**[SH]:** My 9-year-old, she's starting to act out at school. The teacher called me twice. And I can't even go to the meetings 'cause I'm with a client.

**[S]:** How long has that been going on?

**[SH]:** Since school started. September.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If a store worked, what would you want it to do?

**[SH]:** Even fifteen hundred a month. So I can drop two clients and be home after school.

*— ROADBLOCK / URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is that?

**[SH]:** Ten. My baby's struggling.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** That makes total sense. Let me ask a couple money questions, just so I know what's realistic for you. What's coming in each month, roughly?

**[SH]:** Like nineteen hundred. Their daddy don't help.

**[S]:** And with two kids, are you able to put anything aside?

**[SH]:** Honestly? No. Some months I'm short. I'm on SNAP right now.

**[S]:** Thank you for being honest with me. And have you got anything saved at all?

**[SH]:** Like three hundred. That's it.

**[S]:** And credit, anything available?

**[SH]:** No. My credit's messed up.

**[S]:** Okay. Shonda, I'm going to be straight with you, because you've been straight with me, cool?

**[SH]:** Okay.

**[S]:** Starting a store takes real money, for stock and ads, before it makes anything. And our coaching is a real investment on top of that. With nineteen hundred, two kids, three hundred saved and money short some months, I'd be doing you wrong if I booked you in. You'd either stress yourself out or put yourself in debt, and your kids need you not stressed.

**[SH]:** *(pause)* Yeah. I figured. I just hoped.

**[S]:** I know. And look, wanting to be home for your daughter is the right reason. It's not a "no" forever. It's "not like this, right now."

**[SH]:** Okay.

**[S]:** Can I give you a couple ideas that don't cost money?

**[SH]:** Please.

**[S]:** One: ask your agency if any of your clients could be closer to the kids' school, or if there's a split shift that gets you home by 3:30. A lot of agencies will do that if you ask. Two: Leo's got free videos on YouTube about how people start with almost nothing, like selling stuff you already have or doing pre-orders. Watch those first. And three: when things get a little steadier, call me back, and we'll talk again. I mean that.

**[SH]:** Thank you. Really. Everybody else just tries to get my card.

**[S]:** Not me. Can I check in with you in a few months?

**[SH]:** Yes. Please.

**[S]:** I'll text you the YouTube link now. And Shonda, go to the next school meeting if you can. That matters more than any store.

**[SH]:** *(quiet)* I will. Thank you, Stefan.

## What to notice

- **Run the questions even when you can feel the DQ coming.** The answers decide, not your gut.
- **"Thank you for being honest with me."** SNAP, being short some months, three hundred saved. She's telling you hard things, so make it safe.
- **"I'm going to be straight with you, because you've been straight with me."** Ask permission, then give the real reason, which is about *her* (stress, debt, the kids). Not "you can't afford it".
- **"It's not a 'no' forever. It's 'not like this, right now.'"** Hope without a lie.
- **Give her something real for free.** An agency shift change, the free videos, a check-in date. Scott: a DQ respects your time, the closer's time *and* the prospect's. "Everybody else just tries to get my card" is why she'll come back.
