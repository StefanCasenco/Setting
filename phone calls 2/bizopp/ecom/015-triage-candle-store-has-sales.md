# 015 · Ecom · Booked a slot (triage) · Candle store doing sales, no idea about profit

**Prospect:** Priya, 37, HR coordinator by day, runs a Shopify candle store, New Jersey · **Lead:** booked a 12:30 lunch slot · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked with her husband

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Priya, how are you? It's Stefan from Leo's team. I've got you down for 12:30. Are you on your lunch?

**[P]:** Yes, hi! I'm in my car. *(laughs)* The only private place at my office.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* The car office. So what got you booking in?

**[P]:** So I have a candle store. On Shopify. It's been going about a year and a half. And it's doing sales, but I honestly cannot tell you if I'm making money. Leo had a video about "revenue isn't profit" and I was like, oh no, that's me.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That video gets a lot of people. What's it doing a month, roughly?

**[P]:** Around six thousand. Holiday months, like eleven.

**[S]:** That's real sales. And the HR job is full-time?

**[P]:** Full-time. Nine to five. Then candles from like 8 to midnight.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Eight to midnight. What does that look like?

**[P]:** Pouring, labeling, packing orders, answering emails, posting on TikTok. My kitchen smells like vanilla bean all the time. My husband says he can't eat dinner without tasting candle. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Vanilla bean dinner. And how has that schedule actually been affecting you?

**[P]:** Honestly, I'm exhausted. I'm doing two jobs and I don't know if the second one's even paying me. Last month after ads and supplies and shipping, I think I made… four hundred dollars? For like eighty hours of work.

**[S]:** Four hundred for eighty hours.

**[P]:** Five dollars an hour. *(laughs)* I could work at Starbucks.

**[S]:** What did you feel when you worked that out?

**[P]:** I cried a little, honestly. In this car. *(laughs)* It's a very emotional car.

**[S]:** How long have you felt like that?

**[P]:** Since summer. When I finally sat down and did the numbers.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If the store was working properly, where would you want it in a year?

**[P]:** Making like four thousand profit a month. Real profit.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[P]:** I could quit HR. *(pause)* I hate HR. I'm the person who has to sit in when people get fired. I sat in on three last month.

**[S]:** Three last month.

**[P]:** One woman had been there nineteen years. She was crying, and I had to hand her the box for her desk. I went home and poured candles till 2am because I couldn't sleep.

**[S]:** That's a lot to carry home.

**[P]:** It is.

**[S]:** Why's it important to fix it now?

**[P]:** Because if I'm going to keep doing eighty hours, it has to be for something. Or I need to stop.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's been stopping it from being profitable already?

**[P]:** I have no idea, honestly. That's the problem. Is it the ads? The shipping? The jars cost more since the tariffs. I just don't know.

**[S]:** What do you think it would take to find out?

**[P]:** Somebody who knows ecom to look at my numbers. Like, actually look. Not a course. I bought a course. It was about dropshipping, and I don't dropship. *(laughs)*

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is it to you to figure this out?

**[P]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[P]:** The HR stuff. And I'm 37. I don't want to be handing people boxes at 50.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes over the next six months?

**[P]:** I probably quit the candles. And stay in HR. *(pause)* That makes me so sad saying it.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Okay, couple of money questions. You said six thousand a month in sales. What's HR paying you, after taxes?

**[P]:** About forty-eight hundred. My husband's an engineer, so we're okay.

**[S]:** And you know in ecom it takes money to make money: stock, ads, all of it. How much is sitting in the store's account right now?

**[P]:** Maybe two thousand. It goes right back into jars and wax.

**[S]:** And personally, between you and your husband, how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[P]:** Probably fifteen hundred.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[P]:** About thirty thousand. That's ours, though. Not candle money.

**[S]:** Of course. So putting more into the store would be a joint decision?

**[P]:** Definitely. Raj would want to be part of it.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, your store's doing six K a month but last month you made about four hundred, five dollars an hour for eighty hours. You're doing HR all day, sitting in on firings, then pouring candles till midnight. You want four K profit a month so you can quit HR, and what you said you need is somebody to actually look at your numbers and find where the money's going. Next step would be a Zoom with Nina, one of Leo's head coaches. She works with stores that already have sales, and she'll go through your Shopify, your ads and your costs with you, live. And since it'd be a joint decision, Raj should be on too. Evenings, or your lunch again?

**[P]:** Evening. Raj gets home at 6:30.

**[S]:** Nina's got Tuesday at 7 or Thursday at 7.

**[P]:** Tuesday.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 7. Best email?

**[P]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it and forward it to Raj. And have your Shopify and ads manager open on the call, Nina will want to look at real numbers.

**[P]:** I will. Thank you, Stefan. This is the most hopeful I've felt in this car. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Big upgrade for the car. Speak Tuesday.

## What to notice

- **Store owners with sales: get the numbers early.** "What's it doing a month?" in soft qual tells you she's sophisticated (Scott's "already doing it" box).
- **"Four hundred for eighty hours."** She worked it out herself. The setter just repeated it.
- **The goal led to the real pain.** "I could quit HR" → "I hate HR" → the woman with the box. That's the North Star, and it wasn't even about candles.
- **"What happens if nothing changes in six months?"** "I probably quit the candles… that makes me so sad saying it." Let her hear herself say it.
- **Capital question for an existing store:** ask what's in the store's account, then personal savings, then whether it's a joint decision.
