# 017 · Ecom · Downloaded the "winning products" list · Nail tech, talks while working

**Prospect:** Tasha, 32, nail tech, rents a booth, Atlanta · **Lead:** downloaded the free "50 winning products" list yesterday · **Money:** capital · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Tasha, how you doing? It's Stefan from Leo's team. You grabbed our winning products list yesterday. What made you grab it?

**[T]:** Heyyy. Hold on, girl, let me— *(to client)* hold still, baby, I'm almost done with this one. *(back)* Okay. I'm doing a set right now, but I can talk. I got you on speaker.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Multitasking. I can call you back after your client if that's easier?

**[T]:** Nah, you good. She don't mind. She's nosy anyway. *(client laughs in background)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Hi, nosy client. So what made you grab the list?

**[T]:** 'Cause I'm tired of doing nails twelve hours a day, honestly. My hands hurt. My back hurts. And I'm seeing all these girls on TikTok selling lash glue and press-ons and making bank.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** You do nails full-time?

**[T]:** Full-time. I rent a booth in a salon. Seven years.

**[S]:** And have you sold anything online before?

**[T]:** I sell press-ons I make on Instagram sometimes. Like, here and there.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Twelve hours a day. What's that been doing to you?

**[T]:** My wrists, honestly. I got carpal tunnel starting. My doctor said I need to cut back. But how? I pay booth rent whether I work or not. Three hundred a week.

**[S]:** Three hundred a week whether you work or not. So if you take a day off…

**[T]:** I lose money. Last time I was sick, I lost like eight hundred that week between no clients and the rent.

**[S]:** How did that week feel?

**[T]:** Scary. I got a son. He's nine. I was laying there thinking, if my hands go, what we got? *(to client)* Girl, I'm good, I'm good. *(back)* She's asking if I'm crying. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Are you?

**[T]:** Not yet. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** How long has it been like that with your wrists?

**[T]:** Since like spring. It's getting worse.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If the online thing worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[T]:** Making like three K a month from press-ons and products. So I can do nails three days instead of six.

**[S]:** What would three days instead of six mean for you?

**[T]:** My hands get to rest. And I could pick my son up from school. He's been asking me to come to his basketball games, and they're at 4. I'm always with a client at 4.

**[S]:** Why now?

**[T]:** 'Cause my doctor said if I don't cut back, I might need surgery. And then I can't do nails at all.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've already sold press-ons on Instagram. What do you think's stopped it from being more than here and there?

**[T]:** I don't know how to do the website stuff. Or ads. I just post and pray. *(laughs)* And I don't have time, I'm doing nails.

**[S]:** If it's just a time thing, why not raise your prices on nails and work less, instead of doing ecom?

**[T]:** *(pause)* …I did. Last year. I lost like five clients. Atlanta's got a nail tech on every corner. So no, I can't. It's my hands, period. I need something that ain't my hands.

**[S]:** Okay. So what do you think it'd take to make the online side real?

**[T]:** Someone to show me how to set it up right. The site, the ads. I know my product's good. My clients love my press-ons.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is this?

**[T]:** Ten. My hands, baby. Ten.

**[S]:** What makes it a ten?

**[T]:** 'Cause if I lose my hands, I lose everything. And my son.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions. What are you clearing a month from nails, after booth rent?

**[T]:** Like thirty-eight hundred.

**[S]:** And with your son, and everything going up, how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[T]:** Like three hundred. On a good month.

**[S]:** And on a tight month?

**[T]:** Nothing. Like when I was sick.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[T]:** Like four thousand. That's my "hands" money. For if something happens.

**[S]:** And you know in ecom it takes money to make money, for stock and ads. Is the four thousand something you'd use, or is it strictly emergency?

**[T]:** I'd use some. If it got me off my hands.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've been doing nails twelve hours a day for seven years. Your wrists are starting carpal tunnel and your doctor said you might need surgery. You pay three hundred a week whether you work or not, and you're missing your son's 4 o'clock games. You already sell press-ons and your clients love them. You want three K a month online so you can drop to three days, and what you said you need is someone to show you how to set up the site and ads right. Next step would be a Zoom with Nina, one of Leo's head coaches. She'll look at your press-ons and map out how to sell them properly. When are you not with a client?

**[T]:** Mondays. Salon's closed.

**[S]:** Monday at 11 or 2?

**[T]:** 11.

**[S]:** Monday at 11. Best email?

**[T]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Check it when your hands are free. *(laughs)* And send me a pic of your press-ons so I can pass it to Nina.

**[T]:** Oh, I got you! Tell her they're fire.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I will. Bye, nosy client!

**[CLIENT]:** *(in background)* Byeee!

## What to notice

- **They're with someone (client, kid, coworker)?** Offer a callback once. If they say "you good," keep going, and keep it light. The nosy client became part of the rapport.
- **"Three hundred a week whether you work or not."** Repeat the fact, then let her finish the thought: "So if you take a day off…"
- **Atefur's time move:** "Why not raise your prices and work less?" She'd already tried it and lost five clients. Now the reason is real: "I need something that ain't my hands."
- **Ten is ten.** A health deadline plus a kid. Get the reason for the 10 and don't oversell.
- **"Send me a pic"** gives the closer real context and the prospect something to look forward to.
