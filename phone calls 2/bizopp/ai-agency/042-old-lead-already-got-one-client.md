# 042 · AI agency · Old lead, 4 months · "I already started, I've got one client"

**Prospect:** Danielle, 26, quit her call centre job, works part-time at Target, Phoenix · **Lead:** applied in July, didn't book · **Money:** program, tight · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Danielle, how's it going? It's Stefan from Zain's team. Random one. You applied back in July about starting an AI agency. Where you at with it now?

**[D]:** Oh! Actually, I started! I've got a client!

**[S]:** No way, that's awesome, congrats! How'd you land them?

**[D]:** *(laughs)* My aunt has a dental office. I built her a chatbot that answers questions and books appointments. She pays me five hundred a month.

**[S]:** Five hundred, recurring. That's real. How's it going?

**[D]:** She loves it. She says it saves her receptionist two hours a day.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** So what's next for you?

**[D]:** *(pause)* That's the thing. I've been trying to get a second client for two months. Nothing.

**[S]:** What have you tried?

**[D]:** I emailed like fifty dentists. Two replied. One said "not interested," one asked for a price and ghosted.

**[S]:** Ghosted after the price. What did you quote?

**[D]:** Fifteen hundred setup, five hundred a month.

**[S]:** And did you show them your aunt's results?

**[D]:** *(pause)* …No. I didn't think of that.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* That's okay, that's what the next step's for.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** And you said you left the call centre?

**[D]:** Yeah, in September. I'm part-time at Target so I have time for this.

**[S]:** You quit to do this. That's brave.

**[D]:** Or stupid. *(laughs)* My mom thinks it's stupid.

**[S]:** What does she say?

**[D]:** "You had a stable job, Danielle." She worked at the same hospital twenty-five years. She doesn't get it.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, hearing that?

**[D]:** It makes me want to prove her wrong. But also, what if she's right? I'm making less now. I'm folding shirts at Target.

**[S]:** Folding shirts, after quitting to build your own thing.

**[D]:** Yeah. It's humbling. *(laughs)* I saw a girl from high school at Target last week and I hid in the stockroom.

**[S]:** You hid in the stockroom.

**[D]:** Behind the paper towels. *(laughs)* Very professional.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If the agency worked, where would you want it in a year?

**[D]:** Like six clients. Three K a month. Quit Target.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[D]:** That my mom's wrong. *(laughs)* And that I don't hide behind paper towels.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've proven it works with your aunt. What do you think's stopping clients two, three, four?

**[D]:** I don't know how to sell it. I can build. I don't know how to get people to say yes.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[D]:** Somebody to show me how to pitch. And what to charge. Fifty emails and nothing is killing me.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[D]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[D]:** My savings are going down every month. And the stockroom. *(laughs)*

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, just so Leon can build a plan that's realistic for where you're at. With Target and your aunt, what's coming in each month?

**[D]:** About nineteen hundred.

**[S]:** And are you able to put anything aside right now?

**[D]:** Not really. Like a hundred. I live with my boyfriend, we split rent.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[D]:** About four K, from the call centre. It's going down.

**[S]:** Thank you for being straight. And if Leon finds the right fit, is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do right now, or is it genuinely too tight?

**[D]:** I mean… it's tight. But if it gets me the next five clients, I'd find a way.

**[S]:** That's honest. Leon will be straight with you about what makes sense with four K.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've got one client paying five hundred a month, and it saves your aunt's receptionist two hours a day. But fifty emails got you nothing, and you never showed anyone her results. You quit the call centre, your mom thinks it was stupid, and you hid behind the paper towels at Target. You want six clients and three K a month to quit Target and prove her wrong, and what you said you need is somebody to show you how to pitch and what to charge. Next step would be a Zoom with Leon, one of Zain's head coaches. He'll look at your aunt's chatbot and your emails and map out how to land the next client. When are you off?

**[D]:** Wednesdays.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 10 or 1?

**[D]:** 10.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[D]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Bring your aunt's numbers, the two hours a day. That's your proof.

**[D]:** Okay! And no stockroom on Wednesday. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **An old lead who's already started is a great lead.** "No way, that's awesome, congrats!" first. Then "what's next for you?" finds the gap.
- **Small, specific questions about what she tried** ("what did you quote?", "did you show them her results?") found the problem without a lecture.
- **"I hid in the stockroom. Behind the paper towels."** A funny detail that carries the real pain. Repeat it and let her laugh.
- **Tight money: ask honestly, and let the binary do its job.** "Is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do, or is it genuinely too tight?" She said tight. Don't push. "Leon will be straight with you."
- **Bring the proof** (the two hours a day). It helps her close her own next client too.
