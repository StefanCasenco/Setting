*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Arjun, how's it going? It's Stefan from Zain's team. You grabbed our AI agency starter kit a couple days ago. What made you grab it?

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** So the building's not the problem.

**[A]:** I don't know how to sell. I'm a developer. I hate calls. *(laughs)* Again, no offence.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I'm on calls all day, I get it.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** What had you building things on the side in the first place?

**[A]:** I'm bored at work, and I'm underpaid. I'm on one-forty. Contractors on my team doing the same work are on like a hundred an hour. That's two-ten a year.

**[S]:** Same work, way more.

**[A]:** And they're not better than me. They just took the risk.

**[S]:** How does that sit with you, sitting next to them?

**[A]:** *(pause)* It's annoying. More than annoying. I'm 31. I told myself I'd have my own thing by 30.

**[S]:** By 30.

**[A]:** Yeah. I had a plan. *(laughs)* I missed it.

**[S]:** What happened to the plan?

**[S]:** If the agency worked, what would you want in a year?

**[A]:** Every single one.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to get those first clients?

**[A]:** Someone to teach me the sales side. Positioning, pricing, outreach. The stuff I avoid.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[A]:** Seven.

**[S]:** What makes it a seven?

**[A]:** Missing 30. And the contractors.

**[S]:** And what's keeping it from a nine or ten?

**[A]:** Yeah. That's the plan. Build it on the side, jump after the vest.

## What to notice

- **"I could build it in a weekend."** "I believe you, you're probably more technical than anyone who downloads it." Then: "So what had you downloading it, if you could build it?" He has to say the real reason.
- **"By 30."** A missed self-deadline is a strong pain for driven people. Ask "what happened to the plan?" and you get "golden handcuffs".
- **"Every Diwali."** His dad's question was the identity piece. Repeat it once.
- **"Would you do this alongside the job until March?"** If the vesting is the blocker, let him set the timeline. Don't fight it.
- **Brief the closer to skip what he knows.** It handles his objection before the next call.
