# 045 · AI agency · Application · 19-year-old student: Scott's transparent line, financial DQ

**Prospect:** Zach, 19, college sophomore, computer science, works 10 hours a week at the campus library, Gainesville, Florida · **Lead:** application from TikTok · **Money:** program, not there · **Ends:** financial DQ, kindly

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Zach, what's up, man? It's Stefan from Zain's team. You put an application in about starting an AI agency. What got you into it?

**[Z]:** Yo! Bro, I've been building agents in Python for like six months. I think I'm actually kinda good. And I want to sell it.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Six months building agents, nice. Where you based, and what are you doing right now, work or school?

**[Z]:** Gainesville. UF. CS sophomore.

**[S]:** Are you working alongside school?

**[Z]:** Like ten hours a week at the library. *(laughs)* Shelving books.

**[S]:** Okay. Zach, I'll be transparent with you, just so I don't waste your time. If you're looking at our coaching, the program's a mid four-figure investment. Is that something that'd be viable for you right now, or is it completely out of the question?

**[Z]:** *(pause)* Oh. Uh. Yeah, no, that's out of the question. I've got like six hundred bucks. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Totally fair, and honestly, thanks for being straight. That's the right answer for a sophomore. I'd much rather say it now than have you sit through a 45-minute call.

**[Z]:** Yeah, appreciate that. Kinda bummed, though.

**[S]:** I get it. Can I give you a couple things that'll actually help, for free?

**[Z]:** Yeah, please.

**[S]:** You've got a skill most people buying AI courses don't have. You can actually code. So, one: build one agent for a real local business, for free or cheap. A dentist, a gym, a pizza place near campus. Get a real result, like "saved them five hours a week." That's your case study. Two: Zain's YouTube has a whole series on pricing and outreach. Watch that, because that's the part coders skip. And three: UF has an entrepreneurship center. Go talk to them. They have money for student startups sometimes.

**[Z]:** Wait, for real? The entrepreneurship center?

**[S]:** For real. Most students never walk in.

**[Z]:** Bro. Okay. That's actually helpful.

**[S]:** And when you've got that first case study, or you've got a summer internship and some income, hit me up. Can I check in with you in the spring?

**[Z]:** Yeah, for sure.

**[S]:** Perfect. Go build the pizza place a chatbot. *(laughs)*

**[Z]:** *(laughs)* Bet. Thanks, man.

## What to notice

- **Soft qual in under a minute:** "Work or school?" then "Are you working alongside it?" That's Scott's step 2, with the Miro "student → hard qualify" box.
- **Scott's transparent line, word for word:** "Mid four-figure investment. Is that viable for you right now, or completely out of the question?" Binary on purpose. You want a fast answer.
- **A financial DQ isn't a skill DQ.** He's actually good, so tell him that ("a skill most people buying AI courses don't have").
- **Leave him better off:** a free case-study plan, the right free videos, and a campus resource. That's a future lead, and a future referral.
- **Two minutes, not twenty.** Protecting the closer's calendar also protects your own dials.
