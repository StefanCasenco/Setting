## What to notice

- **She's sophisticated** (already building), so the call goes fast. Scott's "already doing it" box.
- **"A 'great job' on Slack. With a thumbs up emoji."** The detail is the pain. Ask "how did that feel?" and let her tell you about the bathroom.
- **The roadblock is knowledge on the business side, in her words.** "The building I've got."
- **Program-only money.** No "capital", because the business barely needs any. Instead: grow income → current income → monthly savings → total → "is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do right now?" It's smooth and it never mentions price.
- **"Free client, real case study."** Tell them to bring their proof. It makes the closer's job easier.
