## What to notice

- **A paying member gets a warm open:** "How are you finding it so far?" Ask about progress before problems.
- **Never make the low tier sound bad.** "The community's great, but I need someone on my stuff" came from him. The setter only asked where he's stuck.
- **"I said 'let me get back to you.' That was three weeks ago."** A specific stuck moment beats a general "I need help." Repeat the time.
- **The upgrade bridge is guidance, in his words:** "actual feedback on my stuff, not a group post."
- **"Everything you're doing in the community keeps going."** It reassures him the upgrade adds to what he has and doesn't replace it.
