# 046 · AI agency · New form · Plumber who tells a story with every answer

**Prospect:** Bobby, 42, owns a two-truck plumbing business, Jacksonville · **Lead:** form filled 15 minutes ago · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Bobby, how's it going? It's Stefan from Zain's team. You just put your info in about AI. What made you fill it out?

**[B]:** Oh, man, okay, so funny story. *(laughs)* So I'm at a job this morning, right, lady's got a water heater going out, and she's on her phone the whole time, and she goes, "Bobby, you should get one of those AI things that answers your phone, 'cause I called you three times yesterday and you didn't pick up." And I'm like, lady, I was under a house! I was literally under your neighbor's house! *(laughs)* And then I'm in the truck after, scrolling, and boom, there's Zain's ad about AI answering phones for contractors. And I'm like, okay, God's talking to me. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* God and the water heater lady. So is it more for your own business, or doing it for other contractors?

**[B]:** Both, honestly! 'Cause here's the thing, okay, I've got like six buddies with trade businesses, roofers, HVAC guys, an electrician, and every single one of them misses calls. Every single one. We all complain about it at the bar. *(laughs)*

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** How long you had your plumbing business?

**[S]:** And have you built anything with AI?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* So fifteen, twenty calls a week, at three or four hundred each.

**[B]:** *(pause)* …Oh, man. When you say it like that. That's like… five grand a week?

**[S]:** You tell me.

**[B]:** That's like five grand a week I'm not getting. Holy crap.

**[S]:** And how's missing those been affecting you?

**[B]:** Honestly, I feel like I'm always behind. I'm under a house, my phone's buzzing, I know it's a job, and I can't get it. Then I'm calling people back at 8pm while my kids are doing homework, and my wife's giving me the look.

**[S]:** The look.

**[B]:** You know the look. *(laughs)* The "you said you'd be done at 6" look.

**[S]:** How long has it been like that?

**[B]:** Since we got the second truck. Three years.

**[B]:** That I'm home at 6. For real. And honestly, my knees. I'm 42. I can't be under houses forever. If I had the AI business on the side, I could start backing off the crawlspaces.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped you from setting it up already?

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[B]:** Somebody to teach me how to build it, like, simple. Plumber-simple. And then how to sell it to the guys at the bar. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* You said it. What makes it a nine?

## What to notice

- **Let the rambler tell the first story fully.** The water heater lady told you the problem, the timing and the why-now in one go.
- **Pull the number out of the ramble.** "How many calls do you think you're missing a week?" Then repeat it with the price: "Fifteen, twenty calls, at three or four hundred each." He did the maths himself: "five grand a week. Holy crap."
- **"You tell me."** Don't do the maths for him. Scott: you don't get points for knowing the answer.
- **"The look."** Repeat his phrase. Every married business owner knows it, and it's the real cost.
- **When he quotes the number back at urgency** ("five grand a week, you just said it"), say "you said it." It keeps the ownership with him.
