# 044 · AI agency · Webinar attendee · "Isn't AI just a bubble?"

**Prospect:** Greg, 50, operations manager at a trucking company, Atlanta · **Lead:** attended the webinar, wrote "isn't this all hype?" in the chat · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Greg, how you doing? It's Stefan from Zain's team. You were on Zain's webinar Tuesday. I saw your message in the chat: "isn't this all hype?" *(laughs)*

**[G]:** *(laughs)* Yeah, that was me. I've been around long enough to see the dot-com thing, the crypto thing. Everybody's selling AI courses now. So yeah. I'm skeptical.

**[S]:** Honestly, Greg, that's fair. There's a lot of hype, and a lot of people selling courses who shouldn't be. I'm not going to argue with you on that. But you stayed for the whole webinar anyway, so something caught your eye. What was it?

**[G]:** *(pause)* …Fair point. The dispatch example. When he showed that bot that matches loads to drivers.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Why'd that one get you?

**[G]:** 'Cause that's my job. I've got three dispatchers doing that by hand. Phone, spreadsheet, whiteboard. And I watched that thing do it in seconds, and I thought, if that's real, there's money in that. Every small trucking company in America has that problem.

**[S]:** That's a real problem you know from the inside. How long you been in trucking?

**[G]:** Twenty-four years.

**[S]:** And have you built anything with AI yourself?

**[G]:** My son set me up with ChatGPT. I use it for emails. *(laughs)* That's it.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** So why would a guy who's been in trucking twenty-four years think about doing this himself, instead of just telling his boss?

**[G]:** *(laughs)* Because my boss would take it, roll it out, get a bonus, and I'd get a pat on the back. I've had twenty-four years of pats on the back.

**[S]:** Twenty-four years of pats on the back. How do you feel about the job these days?

**[G]:** Tired. Drivers quitting, brokers screwing us, fuel prices, me getting yelled at by the owner every time a load's late. I'm 50. I see the owner's my age, and he's on a boat in the Keys while I'm here at 6am.

**[S]:** And how does that feel, at 50?

**[G]:** Like the window's closing. If I don't do something in the next few years, I'm doing this till 67.

**[S]:** Till 67.

**[G]:** Seventeen more years of late loads. *(laughs)* No thanks.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If you built this for small trucking companies and it worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[G]:** Five, six companies paying me. Enough to match my salary. About six grand a month.

**[S]:** And what would matching it mean for you?

**[G]:** That I built something on twenty-four years of knowing this business. Instead of the owner getting the boat. *(laughs)*

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped you from doing anything with it so far?

**[G]:** I don't know how to build it. My son could, maybe, but he's at college. And I don't know how to sell software. I know how to sell to drivers.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[G]:** Somebody to show me the build, and how to package it. And honestly, somebody to tell me straight if it's real or hype.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[G]:** Seven.

**[S]:** What makes it a seven?

**[G]:** The window. And the boat. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** And what's holding it back from a nine?

**[G]:** Not knowing if it's real.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Fair. Couple money questions, just so Leon can build a realistic plan. What's trucking paying you, monthly?

**[G]:** About sixty-two hundred.

**[S]:** And how much are you putting aside each month?

**[G]:** About fifteen hundred. Kids are mostly grown.

**[S]:** And savings, outside retirement?

**[G]:** About forty.

**[S]:** And if Leon thinks it's real and it fits, is investing in yourself to build this something you'd be in a position to do?

**[G]:** If it's real. Yeah. I'm not throwing money at hype.

**[S]:** Totally fair.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've had twenty-four years in trucking and twenty-four years of pats on the back. You've got three dispatchers matching loads by hand, and when you saw that bot do it in seconds, you thought every small trucking company has that problem. You're 50, you feel the window closing, and you don't want seventeen more years of late loads. You want five or six companies paying you to match your salary. He'll look at the dispatch idea specifically, and if he thinks it's hype, he'll say so. Bring your son if he wants. Evening?

**[G]:** Thursday evening.

**[S]:** Thursday at 7 or 8?

**[G]:** 7.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[G]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it for me. And bring your whiteboard, metaphorically. *(laughs)*

## What to notice

- **Use their own chat message at the open.** "I saw your message: 'isn't this all hype?'" He laughs, and the call starts honest.
- **Don't argue about the market.** "There's a lot of hype, I'm not going to argue with you." Then the frame back: "But you stayed for the whole webinar anyway, so something caught your eye."
- **His industry knowledge is the gold.** "That's my job" came from one curious question: "Why'd that one get you?"
- **"Twenty-four years of pats on the back."** His joke, repeated. It turned into the pain (the window closing).
- **"If he thinks it's hype, he'll say so."** For a sceptic, permission to hear "no" is what makes him book.
