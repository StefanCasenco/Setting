## What to notice

- **"AI's the future."** It's a market opinion, not a reason. "Everybody's saying it. What's going on with *you* specifically?" That's Scott's "not everyone" move.
- **Then the money loop: current income → target → "what would ten K change?"** "I'd get my mom out." A vague trend became the North Star in four questions.
- **"Every night at 10."** Repeat it and stop. Empathetic tone, no more words.
- **Break the big number into a first milestone.** "What gets her out?" Three K is realistic, and it's the real goal.
- **Program money, said smoothly:** savings, total, then "is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do right now, or is money genuinely tight?" He answered with the reason: "For her, I'd do it."
