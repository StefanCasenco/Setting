# 070 · Online fitness coaching · Webinar attendee · ICU nurse who lost 60 pounds and wants to coach other nurses

**Prospect:** Monique, 38, ICU nurse, 12-hour shifts, Houston · **Lead:** attended the webinar on her night off · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Monique, how are you? It's Stefan from Mia's team. You were on Mia's webinar Tuesday. What stood out to you?

**[M]:** Hi! Honestly, the woman who coaches moms. Because I want to coach nurses. Nobody coaches nurses. *(laughs)* We're the most unhealthy healthcare workers on the planet.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Nurses coaching nurses. Why nurses?

**[M]:** Because I was one of them. I lost sixty pounds in the last two years, working nights in the ICU. And everyone on my unit keeps asking me how.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Sixty pounds, working ICU nights. That's not easy. Are you coaching anyone yet?

**[M]:** Four nurses on my unit. For free. *(laughs)* They bring me snacks.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Paid in snacks. And you're full-time ICU?

**[M]:** Three twelves. Nights.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** How's ICU nights treating you these days?

**[M]:** Honestly? I'm burnt out. Fourteen years in. Since COVID it's been… a lot. We're short-staffed every shift. Last week I had three patients when I should've had two, and one of them coded.

**[S]:** That sounds really heavy.

**[M]:** It is. I come home at 8am and I just sit in my car. Like, I can't go inside yet. I need ten minutes.

**[S]:** Ten minutes in the car.

**[M]:** Every shift. *(pause)* My husband knows. He leaves the porch light on.

**[S]:** How long has it been like that?

**[M]:** Since 2020. But the last year's been the worst.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If coaching nurses worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[M]:** Like forty nurses, online, at a hundred and fifty a month. Six K. Then I could drop to one shift a week. Or leave.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[M]:** Not sitting in my car. *(laughs)* And honestly, being the person who helped other nurses not feel how I felt two years ago. I was two hundred and forty pounds and exhausted, and nobody helped me.

**[S]:** Why's that important now?

**[M]:** Because I don't know how many more years of ICU I have in me. My body's telling me. And my mind.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've got four nurses already and real results. What's stopped you from turning it into a business?

**[M]:** I don't know the business side at all. Instagram, payment, programs that work for twelve-hour shifts. I just text the girls on my unit. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[M]:** Someone to help me build it into a real program, and show me how to reach nurses outside my hospital.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[M]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[M]:** The car. And the code last week.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Sean can build a realistic plan. What's nursing bringing in, monthly?

**[M]:** About seventy-two hundred with night differential.

**[S]:** And how much are you two able to put aside each month?

**[M]:** About fifteen hundred.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[M]:** About thirty K. Plus retirement, which I'm not touching.

**[S]:** Good, keep retirement as retirement. And if Sean finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to build this something you'd be in a position to do?

**[M]:** Yes. I'd rather invest in this than in more years of nights.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've done fourteen years of ICU, short-staffed since COVID, and a patient coded last week when you had three. You sit in your car for ten minutes after every shift and your husband leaves the porch light on. You lost sixty pounds on nights, four nurses already ask you for help, and you want forty nurses online, six K a month, so you can drop to one shift and help nurses the way nobody helped you. What you said you need is someone to help you build a real program and reach nurses outside your hospital. Next step would be a Zoom with Sean, one of Mia's head coaches. He'll map out a nurse-coaching program around your shifts. When are you off?

**[M]:** Monday and Tuesday this week.

**[S]:** Monday at 2 or Tuesday at 2?

**[M]:** Tuesday. Monday I'm sleeping. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Best email?

**[M]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Bring your before and after. And Monique, thank you for what you do.

**[M]:** *(quiet)* Thank you. That means a lot.

## What to notice

- **She has the niche, the proof and the demand** (four nurses, sixty pounds). This is the best kind of fitness-coach lead.
- **"That sounds really heavy."** An empathetic tone when she shares something hard (the code). Short, no fixing.
- **"Ten minutes in the car" and "He leaves the porch light on."** The impact is in small details. Repeat them, don't explain them.
- **"Being the person who helped other nurses."** The goal under the money. Scott: "the meaning behind the number is the motivation."
- **Protect retirement**, every time it comes up.
