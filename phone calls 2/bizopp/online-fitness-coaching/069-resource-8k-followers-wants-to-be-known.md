# 069 · Online fitness coaching · Downloaded the free "first 10 clients" guide · 8K followers, wants to be known

**Prospect:** Bri, 23, works at a smoothie bar, posts workouts on TikTok, Orlando · **Lead:** downloaded the guide 2 days ago · **Money:** program, tight · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Bri, how are you? It's Stefan from Mia's team. You grabbed our "first 10 clients" guide a couple days ago. What made you grab it?

**[B]:** Hiii! So I post workouts on TikTok, I have like eight thousand followers, and people keep DMing me asking for programs. And I'm like… how do I actually make money from this? *(laughs)*

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** People are DMing you asking. How many?

**[B]:** Like five or ten a week. "Can you make me a plan?" "Do you coach?"

**[S]:** And what do you tell them?

**[B]:** *(laughs)* "Not yet!" Every time.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* And what do you do for work?

**[B]:** I work at a smoothie bar. Tropical Smoothie.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Five to ten people a week asking to pay you, and you're saying "not yet." Why "not yet"?

**[B]:** I don't know how! Like, how much do I charge? How do I make a program? What if they don't get results? I'm 23, I'm not a "real" coach. I'm just a girl who works out.

**[S]:** "Just a girl who works out."

**[B]:** Yeah. *(laughs)* That's what my mom says. "You're not a coach, Bri, you're a girl with a phone."

**[S]:** How does that feel, when she says that?

**[B]:** *(pause)* Kinda bad, honestly. She wants me to go to nursing school. Like my sister.

**[S]:** And what do you want?

**[B]:** I want this. I want to be, like, known. Like the girls I watch, who have their own apps and brands. I know that sounds dumb.

**[S]:** It doesn't sound dumb. A lot of the biggest coaches started with exactly that. And the smoothie bar?

**[B]:** It's fine. Minimum wage plus tips. I'm making like fifteen hundred a month. I live with my mom. *(laughs)* Which is why I hear about nursing school every day.

**[S]:** How long have people been DMing you?

**[B]:** Since one of my videos got like two hundred thousand views. In August.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If coaching worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[B]:** Like thirty clients online. At like a hundred and fifty each. That's… four and a half K? *(laughs)* That would be insane.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[B]:** Moving out. Proving my mom wrong. *(laughs)* And being the girl other girls watch.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've got people asking and eight thousand followers. What do you think it'd take to say "yes" instead of "not yet"?

**[B]:** Someone to show me how to make programs that actually work, what to charge, and how to, like, sell it without being weird.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[B]:** Nine!

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[B]:** Every week I'm saying "not yet," I'm losing money. And nursing school applications are due in January. *(laughs)* My mom already printed them.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* She printed them. Okay, couple money questions, just so Sean can build a realistic plan. From the fifteen hundred, how much can you put aside each month?

**[B]:** Like four hundred. I don't pay rent.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[B]:** Like twenty-two hundred.

**[S]:** Thank you for being straight. Bri, I'll be transparent: our coaching is a real investment, and with twenty-two hundred saved, Sean's going to be honest about whether it makes sense right now, or whether there's a smarter first step. Is that okay?

**[B]:** Yeah. Totally. I'd rather know.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, a video hit two hundred K views in August, and since then five to ten people a week ask you to coach them, and you keep saying "not yet." Your mom says you're a girl with a phone and she's already printed the nursing applications. You want thirty online clients, about four and a half K, to move out and be the girl other girls watch. What you said you need is someone to show you how to build programs, price them, and sell without being weird. Next step would be a Zoom with Sean, one of Mia's head coaches. He'll look at your TikTok and those DMs, and be honest about what makes sense with where you're at. When are you not making smoothies? *(laughs)*

**[B]:** *(laughs)* Mornings! I work 2 to 10.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 11 or Friday at 11?

**[B]:** Tomorrow!

**[S]:** Best email?

**[B]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. And screenshot a few of those DMs. They're your proof.

**[B]:** Okay! Thank you so much!

## What to notice

- **"Five to ten a week asking."** Get the demand number early. It's the strongest fact on the call.
- **"Why 'not yet'?"** A curious question that went straight to the limiting belief: "just a girl who works out."
- **Her mum's line is the impact.** "A girl with a phone." "How does that feel when she says that?"
- **Wanting to be known is normal in this niche** (Scott). "It doesn't sound dumb. A lot of the biggest coaches started with exactly that."
- **Tight money with a young prospect: be transparent before booking.** "Sean's going to be honest about whether it makes sense, or whether there's a smarter first step."
