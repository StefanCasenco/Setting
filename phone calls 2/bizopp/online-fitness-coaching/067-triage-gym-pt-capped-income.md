# 067 · Online fitness coaching (bizopp) · Booked a slot (triage) · Personal trainer at a big-box gym, capped

**Prospect:** Tyler, 27, personal trainer at an LA Fitness, Scottsdale · **Lead:** booked a 1:30 call from Instagram · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

> **Scott on this niche:** sell the **financial upside**. A PT's income is capped by hours, but an online coach's isn't. Scott knows online coaches doing 300 to 400K a month. And be honest that **a bit of fame** is part of it for most of them. Passion alone isn't enough to invest.

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Tyler, what's up, man? It's Stefan from Mia's team. I've got you down for 1:30 about online coaching. What got you booking in?

**[T]:** Yo! Between clients right now. Honestly, I'm a PT at LA Fitness and I'm capped, bro. I'm booked solid and I'm still broke. *(laughs)*

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Booked solid and still broke. How many clients?

**[T]:** Twenty-two. Like thirty-five sessions a week.

**[S]:** And are you doing any online coaching yet?

**[T]:** Three people. Friends of clients. I charge like a hundred a month. I send them a spreadsheet. *(laughs)*

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Thirty-five sessions a week. What's LA Fitness taking?

**[T]:** Bro. They charge the client ninety an hour. I get twenty-eight.

**[S]:** Twenty-eight of ninety.

**[T]:** Twenty-eight. And I'm there 5am to 8pm with gaps. I eat chicken and rice out of Tupperware in my car between sessions.

**[S]:** And how is that schedule affecting you?

**[T]:** I'm exhausted. My own training's suffering. *(laughs)* A PT who doesn't have time to train. And my girl's always like, "you're never home." I'm literally at the gym fifteen hours a day.

**[S]:** "You're never home."

**[T]:** And I'm 27. The guys I came up with on Instagram, the ones who went online two years ago, one of them's doing like forty K a month. Same certs as me. And I'm here making twenty-eight an hour.

**[S]:** How does that feel, watching him?

**[T]:** Honestly? It eats at me. 'Cause I'm a better coach than him. I just never made the jump.

**[S]:** How long have you been feeling that?

**[T]:** Since he posted his first Lambo. *(laughs)* Like eight months.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If online coaching worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[T]:** Like fifty clients online at two hundred a month. That's ten K. And I drop the gym, or just keep a few in-person.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[T]:** Being home. Training myself again. And, I'm not gonna lie, I want people to know my name. Like, I want to be the guy, not just the guy at LA Fitness.

**[S]:** Nothing wrong with that. Most of the big coaches started exactly there.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've got three online clients and you're a great coach. What do you think's stopped you from scaling?

**[T]:** Content, honestly. I don't know how to grow an audience. I've got like twelve hundred followers. And the business side: sales calls, pricing, onboarding. I send people a Google Sheet.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[T]:** Someone to show me how the guys doing forty K actually do it. Content, funnels, how to sell. The coaching part I got.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[T]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[T]:** Twenty-eight an hour. And my girl.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes in six months?

**[T]:** I'm still in the car eating Tupperware and watching that guy post Lambos. *(laughs)* Nah.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Sean can build a realistic plan. A big part of what we do is grow your income. What are you making a month right now, gym plus online?

**[T]:** Like forty-two hundred. Plus three hundred online.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside?

**[T]:** Like six hundred.

**[S]:** And in total, saved?

**[T]:** Like nine K.

**[S]:** And if Sean finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to build the online side something you'd be in a position to do?

**[T]:** Yeah, bro. That's why I booked.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've got twenty-two clients and thirty-five sessions a week, LA Fitness charges ninety and you get twenty-eight, you're eating Tupperware in your car, and your girl says you're never home. A guy you came up with is doing forty K online with the same certs, and it eats at you, because you're the better coach. You want fifty online clients, ten K a month, to be home, train again, and be the guy. What you said you need is someone to show you content, funnels and selling. Next step would be a Zoom with Sean, one of Mia's head coaches. He'll map out how to go from three spreadsheet clients to an actual online business. When's your gap?

**[T]:** Between 12 and 3, any day.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 1 or Thursday at 1?

**[T]:** Tomorrow.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[T]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it between sets. *(laughs)* And bring your client results, before and afters.

**[T]:** *(laughs)* Bet.

## What to notice

- **This is a sophisticated lead.** He already coaches online, a little, so it goes fast. Scott's "already doing it" box.
- **"Twenty-eight of ninety."** Pull the number that shows the cap. That's Scott's ROI angle in his own words.
- **"I want people to know my name."** Scott said a lot of them want a bit of fame. Don't judge it: "Nothing wrong with that."
- **The comparison is the pain.** "Same certs as me… I'm a better coach."
- **The roadblock is knowledge on the business side.** "The coaching part I got."
