## What to notice

- **Passion is not pain, and it's not a reason to invest.** She loves fitness, likes her job, and "doesn't care about the money." Scott: go do it yourself.
- **Ask the money goal directly, once.** "Money-wise, what would you want it making?" "I don't really care." That's your answer.
- **A temp check of 5 → "How come?"** "It's more of a fun idea." That's an interest DQ.
- **Tell her why you're not booking her, using her facts.** She likes her job, money's not the goal, so she doesn't need the program.
- **Give her free first steps.** She's a happy person and a good word of mouth. And if her goal changes, she'll call back.
