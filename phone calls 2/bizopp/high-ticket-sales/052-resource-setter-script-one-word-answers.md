# 052 · High-ticket sales · Downloaded the free setter script · One-word answers the whole way

**Prospect:** Will, 22, stocks shelves at Walmart overnight, Little Rock · **Lead:** downloaded the free setter script yesterday · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Will, what's up, man? It's Stefan from Luke's team. You grabbed our free setter script yesterday. What made you grab it?

**[W]:** Saw it.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Fair. Did you read it?

**[W]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** What'd you think?

**[W]:** It was good.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Where you based?

**[W]:** Little Rock.

**[S]:** And what do you do?

**[W]:** Walmart.

**[S]:** What department?

**[W]:** Overnight stocking.

**[S]:** Overnights. What time?

**[W]:** 10 to 7.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** How long you been doing overnights?

**[W]:** A year.

**[S]:** How is it?

**[W]:** It's fine.

**[S]:** *(two seconds of silence)*

**[W]:** …I mean, it's quiet. Nobody talks. *(laughs)* That's kinda the problem.

**[S]:** Nobody talks?

**[W]:** Like, I'm just stocking cereal by myself all night. Headphones in. Then I sleep all day.

**[S]:** And how's that been for you?

**[W]:** Lonely, I guess.

**[S]:** Lonely how?

**[W]:** I don't see my friends. They work days. I haven't hung out with anybody in like… a month?

**[S]:** A month.

**[W]:** Yeah. *(pause)* My mom said I'm turning into a vampire. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* A vampire. How long you been feeling like that?

**[W]:** Since summer.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If setting worked out, where would you want to be in a year?

**[W]:** Dunno.

**[S]:** If you had to guess?

**[W]:** …Working days. From home. Making like three K.

**[S]:** What's Walmart paying now?

**[W]:** Like twenty-two hundred.

**[S]:** And what would three K, from home, in the daytime, mean for you?

**[W]:** Seeing people. *(laughs)* Not being a vampire.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped you from getting into setting already?

**[W]:** Scared of the phone.

**[S]:** Scared of the phone?

**[W]:** Yeah. I don't really talk. *(laughs)* You can tell.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* You're talking to me right now.

**[W]:** *(laughs)* Barely.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take to get past being scared of the phone?

**[W]:** Practice, I guess. With someone. A lot.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[W]:** Six.

**[S]:** How come six?

**[W]:** 'Cause I don't know if I can do it.

**[S]:** So is this something you actually want to change, or are you good doing overnights for a while?

**[W]:** *(pause)* I want to change.

**[S]:** What makes you say that?

**[W]:** I don't want to be stocking cereal at 30.

**[S]:** So where's the number really?

**[W]:** Eight. I'm just scared.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** That makes sense. Couple money ones. From the twenty-two hundred, how much can you put aside?

**[W]:** Like six hundred. I live with my mom.

**[S]:** Usual?

**[W]:** Yeah.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[W]:** Like five K.

**[S]:** And if Callum finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to get trained something you'd be in a position to do?

**[W]:** Yeah.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you're on overnights 10 to 7, stocking cereal by yourself with headphones in, and you haven't hung out with anybody in a month. Your mom says you're turning into a vampire. You want to work days, from home, at three K, so you actually see people. What you said you need is practice, a lot of it, to get past being scared of the phone. Next step would be a Zoom with Callum, one of Luke's head coaches. He'll map out what training would look like, and yes, that's another phone call. *(laughs)* But you're doing it right now. When are you awake?

**[W]:** *(laughs)* 3 to 9.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[W]:** *(gives it)*

**[S]:** And Will, you did fine on this call. Just so you know.

## What to notice

- **One-word prospect: easy fact questions first.** Where, what, which department, what time. Each one is easy, and they build up.
- **The two-second silence after "it's fine"** got the first real answer ("Nobody talks"). It works on almost every one-word prospect.
- **Repeat one word as a question.** "Nobody talks?" "Lonely how?" "A month." "Scared of the phone?" That's Atefur's move. It's short and it works.
- **"If you had to guess?"** turned "dunno" into a real goal.
- **A temp check of 6 → "How come?" → binary challenge** ("change, or good doing overnights?"). The 6 became a real 8 with a reason. Scott: binary when challenging, not before.
