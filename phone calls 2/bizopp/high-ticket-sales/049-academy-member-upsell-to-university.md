## What to notice

- **Onboard first, for real.** The weekly calls, the roleplay channel, a link to book a partner. He paid, so give him value before anything else. It's also what makes the upgrade feel honest.
- **The pain wasn't the Academy.** It was Christmas Eve, and her mum's line. The upgrade solves *his* problem, not "you need more content".
- **His roadblock is accountability, in his words:** "It's videos. Nobody's on me." That's the bridge to the higher program.
- **Never trash the lower tier.** "The Academy's brilliant for the learning… and there's also the coaching side. I'm not saying you need it." Then let the money questions decide.
- **UK slang here** (mate, grim, mug), because the prospect's British. Scott: match the region.
