# 047 · High-ticket sales · Booked a slot (triage) · Car salesman, 10 years: "Why would I pay to learn what I already do?"

**Prospect:** Steve, 36, sells cars at a Toyota dealership, Phoenix · **Lead:** booked a 1pm slot from a Facebook ad · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Steve, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Luke's team. I've got you down for 1 o'clock about remote high-ticket sales. What got you booking in?

**[ST]:** Yeah, look, I'll save you the pitch. I've sold cars for ten years. I know objection handling, I know rapport, I know closing. So what exactly would you teach me?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Honestly, Steve? Probably not how to sell. Ten years on a lot in Phoenix is harder than most of what we do. I'm not going to teach you rapport, you'd laugh at me. So let me flip it. If you already know how to sell, what made you book?

**[ST]:** *(pause)* …Okay. Fair. *(laughs)* The remote part, mostly.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** What's wrong with the lot?

**[ST]:** Saturdays. Every Saturday for ten years. And standing outside in 115 in July waiting for someone to walk in, then they want to "think about it."

**[S]:** And are you full-time at the dealership?

**[ST]:** Fifty, fifty-five hours. Commission.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** What does a bad month look like for you?

**[ST]:** Last month I did nineteen hundred. Best month this year was sixty-two hundred.

**[S]:** That's a big swing.

**[ST]:** You can't plan anything. Interest rates are killing car sales.

**[S]:** And how has that swing been affecting you?

**[ST]:** My fiancée's a nurse. She works three twelves. Different days from me. We see each other properly like two days a month. *(laughs)* We text more than we talk.

**[S]:** Two days a month.

**[ST]:** And she wants to start a family next year. And I'm thinking, with what time? We'd be passing the baby between shifts.

**[S]:** How long has it been like that?

**[ST]:** Since we got engaged. Like a year. Before that I didn't care. *(laughs)*

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If remote closing worked out, what would you want, in a year?

**[ST]:** Six K steady. No swings. From home.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[ST]:** Weekends with her. Being there when the baby comes. Not passing it in a parking lot.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You can sell. What's stopped you from getting into remote already?

**[ST]:** I applied to like six closing jobs. They want "high-ticket experience." Or a Loom video. I don't know what a Loom is. *(laughs)* And half of them are commission-only for offers I've never heard of.

**[S]:** So it's not the selling.

**[ST]:** No. It's knowing how that world works. What's a good offer, how you get in. I'm a fish out of water with that.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[ST]:** Somebody who knows that world to show me the way in. Skip the basics.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[ST]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[ST]:** The baby. And I'm tired of July.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Callum can build something realistic. Averaged out, what are you making a month?

**[ST]:** Like four K.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside each month?

**[ST]:** Good months, a grand. Bad months, I eat it. Maybe four hundred average.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[ST]:** About nine K.

**[S]:** And if Callum finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to get into remote something you'd be in a position to do right now?

**[ST]:** If it's not a course telling me how to build rapport, yeah. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I'll make sure he knows.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, ten years on the lot, nineteen hundred one month and sixty-two the next, Saturdays forever, 115 in July. You and your fiancée see each other two days a month, and she wants a baby next year. You can sell. You applied to six closing jobs and hit a wall on "high-ticket experience" and Looms. You want six K steady from home, and what you said you need is somebody to show you the way into that world and skip the basics. Next step would be a Zoom with Callum, one of Luke's head coaches. He came from car sales too, Audi. He'll skip rapport and go straight to how you'd land a good remote offer. When are you off?

**[ST]:** Wednesdays.

**[S]:** Wednesday at 11 or 2?

**[ST]:** 11.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[ST]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. And I'll text you what a Loom is. *(laughs)*

**[ST]:** *(laughs)* Appreciate you, man.

## What to notice

- **"What exactly would you teach me?"** Agree: "Probably not how to sell… you'd laugh at me." Then flip it: "If you already know how to sell, what made you book?" He can't argue with his own reason.
- **"What does a bad month look like?"** Specific numbers say more than "it's up and down".
- **"We text more than we talk."** That's the pain, with a family deadline underneath.
- **"So it's not the selling."** It names the real gap (how that world works), which is different from what he expected you to sell.
- **Match the closer to the prospect** (ex-Audi). An ego prospect needs to respect who's on the next call.
