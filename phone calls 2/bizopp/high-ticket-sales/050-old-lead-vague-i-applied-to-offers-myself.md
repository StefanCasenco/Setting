# 050 · High-ticket sales · Old lead, 5 months · Vague: "I'm good, I've been applying to offers myself"

**Prospect:** Corey, 30, gym membership sales, Denver · **Lead:** applied in June, never booked · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Corey, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Luke's team. Kind of a random one. You applied back in June about getting into remote sales. Where are you at with it now?

**[C]:** Oh, hey. Yeah, I'm good. I've been applying to offers myself.

**[S]:** Nice. How's that going?

**[C]:** It's going. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* "It's going." What does "going" look like?

**[C]:** I've applied to a bunch.

**[S]:** How many is a bunch?

**[C]:** Like… thirty? Thirty-five?

**[S]:** And how many interviews?

**[C]:** *(pause)* Two.

**[S]:** And how did the two go?

**[C]:** One ghosted me after. The other one said I needed more experience.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** And you're still at the gym?

**[C]:** Yeah. Selling memberships. Planet Fitness.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Thirty-five applications, two interviews, no offer. How's that been for you?

**[C]:** It's fine. It's a numbers game, right?

**[S]:** It is. And how long have you been at it?

**[C]:** Since June.

**[S]:** Five months.

**[C]:** *(pause)* …Yeah. When you say it like that.

**[S]:** And what's going on at the gym that's got you wanting out?

**[C]:** Nothing major. I just want more.

**[S]:** More what?

**[C]:** Money, I guess.

**[S]:** What's the gym paying you?

**[C]:** Like twenty-eight hundred, with commission.

**[S]:** And what would you need from remote sales for it to be worth it?

**[C]:** Like five, six K.

**[S]:** What would five or six K change for you?

**[C]:** *(long pause)* …I'd stop lying to my dad.

**[S]:** Lying to your dad?

**[C]:** I told him in July I got a closing job. Remote. Because he kept asking. And he told everybody. My aunt, my uncles. At Thanksgiving last year he was like, "Corey's doing sales from his laptop now." And I'm not. I'm at Planet Fitness.

**[S]:** He's been telling everyone.

**[C]:** Everyone. And Thanksgiving's in three weeks. *(quiet)* I don't know what I'm going to say.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's a lot to be carrying.

**[C]:** I've never told anybody that.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** So if you landed a role, where would you want to be in a year?

**[C]:** Actually closing, actually remote. Five K. So it's not a lie anymore.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** Thirty-five applications. What do you think's been stopping them from turning into offers?

**[C]:** I don't know. I don't have high-ticket experience. My Loom's probably bad. I just send the same thing to everyone.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[C]:** Someone to look at what I'm sending. And help me get actual experience, or look like I have it. And interview practice.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[C]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[C]:** Thanksgiving. *(laughs nervously)*

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, just so Callum can build something realistic. From the twenty-eight hundred, how much are you able to put aside?

**[C]:** Like four hundred.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[C]:** Like six K.

**[S]:** And if Callum finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to actually get placed something you'd be in a position to do right now?

**[C]:** Yeah. I've been doing it alone for five months. It's not working.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you've sent thirty-five applications since June, the same Loom to everyone, and got two interviews and no offer. You're selling memberships at Planet Fitness, and in July you told your dad you'd got a remote closing job. He's told the whole family, and Thanksgiving's in three weeks. You want to actually be closing remote, five K, so it's not a lie anymore. What you said you need is someone to look at what you're sending and help with experience and interviews. Next step would be a Zoom with Callum, one of Luke's head coaches. He'll go through your Loom and applications, and what getting placed actually takes. When are you off?

**[C]:** Tuesday.

**[S]:** Tuesday at 10 or 1?

**[S]:** Best email?

**[C]:** *(gives it)*

## What to notice

- **"I'm good, I've been applying myself."** Don't argue. Turn each vague word into a number: "How many is a bunch?" "How many interviews?" "How did they go?" The facts beat the "I'm good".
- **"Since June." "Five months."** Saying the timeframe back made him feel it.
- **"More what?" → money → target → "what would five or six K change?"** That's Scott's money loop. It got the real thing: lying to his dad.
- **When someone tells you a secret, don't use it as a weapon.** Name it gently in the recap, and thank him at the end. That builds more trust than any technique.
- **The roadblock is guidance:** he's sending the same Loom to everyone.
