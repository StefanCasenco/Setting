# 053 · High-ticket sales · Application · He interviews you: "How much do *you* make? Have you even done it?"

**Prospect:** Hector, 29, bank loan officer, San Antonio · **Lead:** application last night · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Hector, how's it going, man? It's Stefan from Luke's team. You put an application in last night about remote sales. What made you apply?

**[H]:** Hey. Before I answer, can I ask you something?

**[S]:** Sure, go for it.

**[H]:** Are you a setter? Like, is this what the program teaches, what you're doing right now?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Yeah, this is it. I'm a setter on Luke's team.

**[H]:** How much do you make?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* Straight in. I'm not going to give you my bank statement, but enough that I'm not looking for another job. And honestly, I'm still early in it. The guys who've been doing this a couple years make a lot more than me, and the closers make more again.

**[H]:** *(pause)* Okay. That's honest. Most people would've lied.

**[S]:** No point lying, you'd see through it. You're a loan officer, you probably check people's income for a living. *(laughs)*

**[H]:** *(laughs)* All day.

**[S]:** So what made you apply?

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[H]:** I'm a loan officer at a bank. Five years. And I'm good at sales, honestly. But it's salary plus a tiny commission, and the bank caps it.

**[S]:** Caps it how?

**[H]:** Like, I can close twice as many loans as the next guy and make maybe fifteen percent more. There's no upside.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** How's that been for you, being the guy who closes twice as much?

**[H]:** Annoying. Last quarter I was number one in the region. I got a plaque. *(laughs)* A plaque. And the bank made like two hundred K off my loans.

**[S]:** A plaque.

**[H]:** It's on my desk. My wife calls it my "participation trophy." *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* And how long has it been bugging you?

**[H]:** Since the plaque, honestly. That was the moment I was like, why am I doing this for them?

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If remote closing worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[H]:** Ten K a month. I know closers make that, or more.

**[S]:** What are you at now?

**[H]:** About fifty-four hundred.

**[S]:** And what would ten K mean for you?

**[H]:** That if I'm number one, I'm paid like number one. And honestly, my wife's pregnant. I want her to have the option of staying home.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You can sell. What do you think's stopped you from switching already?

**[H]:** I don't know which offers are legit. There's so many scammy ones. And I don't know how to sell over the phone to people who aren't walking into a bank already wanting a loan.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[H]:** Someone who knows that world showing me which offers are real, and how cold-ish selling works. The skill transfers, I just need to adjust it.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[H]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[H]:** The baby. March.

**[S]:** And what happens if nothing changes by March?

**[H]:** I'm getting another plaque, and she's going back to work after six weeks. *(pause)* No.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Callum can build a realistic plan. With how expensive everything is, how much are you two putting aside each month?

**[H]:** About fifteen hundred. We're saving for the baby.

**[S]:** And in total?

**[H]:** About twenty-eight K.

**[S]:** And if Callum finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to make the switch something you'd be in a position to do?

**[H]:** Yes. I'd want to see what it is first. I check things for a living. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* I know you do.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you were number one in the region, you closed twice as much as the next guy, you made the bank two hundred K, and you got a plaque your wife calls a participation trophy. The bank caps you at maybe fifteen percent more. You want ten K a month, so if you're number one you're paid like it, and so your wife has the option to stay home after March. What you said you need is someone who knows which offers are real and how to adjust your selling for the phone. Next step would be a Zoom with Callum, one of Luke's head coaches, and Hector, he'll show you the numbers. You can do your due diligence on him. *(laughs)* When are you free?

**[H]:** *(laughs)* After 5:30.

**[S]:** Tomorrow at 6 or Thursday at 6?

**[H]:** Thursday.

**[S]:** Best email? Not the bank one.

**[H]:** *(laughs)* Definitely not the bank. *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it. And bring the plaque. *(laughs)*

**[H]:** *(laughs)* It's coming with me.

## What to notice

- **When they ask "how much do you make?", answer honestly and don't brag.** "Enough that I'm not looking for another job… I'm still early." He said, "That's honest. Most people would've lied."
- **Don't get defensive about being "just a setter".** Confidence is being comfortable with where you are.
- **Turn his test into rapport.** "You're a loan officer, you probably check people's income for a living." It shows respect and gets a laugh.
- **"A plaque."** Repeat the one word that holds the pain. The participation trophy was the North Star.
- **"He'll show you the numbers. You can do your due diligence on him."** It gives an analytical sceptic a role on the next call.
