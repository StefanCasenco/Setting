## What to notice

- **Welcome the sceptical parent. Don't dodge them.** "Great question, and honestly, I'm glad you're here." Then a plain answer: nobody recruits, no product to buy. Then "I'd be suspicious too."
- **"Can I ask Kayla a couple questions first? Then you can tell me if I've got it wrong."** It takes the frame back from Mom, kindly, and gives her a role.
- **Ask the parent directly at the right moment.** "Teresa, what would it mean for you?" got the most powerful line on the call: "I clean offices. I want better for her."
- **Be transparent about money with a young prospect.** "It's not cheap, and it's not a job that pays you to start." Teresa: "Nobody says that." That honesty is why she shows up.
- **"If you don't like the answers, you walk."** It gives the sceptic control.
