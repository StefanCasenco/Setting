# 055 · High-ticket sales · Watched the video (VSL) · "I'm just curious": interest DQ

**[S]:** Already remote. How's that going?

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how important is getting into closing for you?

**[S]:** *(laughs)* A 3. And what happens if nothing changes, and you're at the SaaS company in a couple years?

**[B]:** Honestly, that sounds fine. Good, actually.

**[S]:** Then I'll be straight with you, Brent. I don't think booking you in makes sense. You've got a remote job you like, a good boss, a raise coming, and you'd be swapping a salary for commission. That's a great spot to be in. If I put you on a call with a closer, it'd be forty-five minutes of you saying "I was just curious."

**[B]:** *(laughs)* Yeah, that's fair. I appreciate that, honestly.

**[S]:** If that ever changes, like the company does layoffs or you get bored, Luke's got free stuff on YouTube and my number's here. Enjoy the dog walks, man.

## What to notice

- **There's no pain to dig.** "Is there anything you'd change?" "Not really." Ask twice, differently, then believe him.
- **A high income doesn't mean qualified, if there's no reason to change.** He's in Scott's "higher income" box, but this is an interest DQ.
- **The temp check is 3, and the future-pace is "sounds good, actually."** That's Scott's settle test. He's happy to settle, so let him go. No boxing match.
- **Tell him why you're not booking him, in his own facts.** Remote, good boss, raise coming, salary vs commission.
- **This protects the closer's calendar and your show and close rates.** A three-minute call beats a forty-five-minute no-show.
