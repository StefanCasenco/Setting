**Prospect:** Nadia, 24, customer service at a cable company call centre, Tampa · **Lead:** filled out the form 3 minutes ago, on her break · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Nadia, how are you? It's Stefan from Luke's team. You literally just put your info in about remote sales. What made you fill it out?

**[N]:** Oh my god, that was fast. *(laughs)* I'm on break. Honestly? I had the worst call of my life like ten minutes ago.

**[S]:** Oh no. What happened?

**[N]:** Guy screaming at me about his bill for twenty-five minutes. Called me stupid. And I can't hang up, it's policy. I have to keep saying "I understand your frustration, sir." *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Called you stupid for twenty-five minutes, and you just had to sit there.

**[N]:** And smile. They can hear if you're not smiling. That's what they say.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** How long you been at the call centre?

**[N]:** Two years.

**[S]:** And have you done sales before?

**[N]:** No. Just this. Customer service.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** Is that normal, calls like that?

**[N]:** Like five a day. It's cable. Everybody's mad about their bill.

**[S]:** How does that leave you by the end of the day?

**[N]:** Drained. I get home and I don't want to talk to anyone. My boyfriend asks how my day was and I'm like, "don't." *(laughs)* And I'm on a headset eight hours, and they time my bathroom breaks.

**[S]:** They time your bathroom breaks?

**[N]:** There's a system. Over six minutes and you get flagged.

**[S]:** That's crazy.

**[N]:** Right?

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If you were in remote sales instead, where would you want to be in a year?

**[N]:** Honestly? Three, four K a month would change my life. I'm on like nineteen hundred.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[N]:** Talking to people who actually want to talk to me. *(laughs)* And working from home. And going to the bathroom whenever I want.

**[S]:** Why's that important now?

**[N]:** Because I'm 24 and I feel like I'm wasting my twenties being yelled at. My friends are posting from Cancún, and I'm in a cubicle saying "I understand your frustration."

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** Have you looked into remote sales before today?

**[N]:** A little on TikTok. But I didn't know you could actually get trained. I thought you had to know someone.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take for you to actually get in?

**[N]:** Somebody to train me properly. And honestly, help me get confident. I've never sold anything.

**[S]:** Nadia, can I point something out?

**[N]:** Sure.

**[S]:** You stayed calm for twenty-five minutes while a guy called you stupid. That's not nothing. A lot of people can't do that.

**[N]:** *(pause)* …I never thought of it like that.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[N]:** Nine.

**[S]:** What makes it a nine?

**[N]:** The bathroom timer. *(laughs)* And Cancún.

**[N]:** Like two hundred. I'm saving for an apartment with my boyfriend.

## What to notice

- **Speed to lead catches the emotion live.** "Worst call of my life, ten minutes ago." A three-minute callback is worth ten later ones.
- **"Called you stupid for twenty-five minutes, and you just had to sit there."** Repeat the hard part slowly. She feels heard.
- **Turn the pain into a skill, and ask first.** "Can I point something out?" Then: "You stayed calm for twenty-five minutes." For call-centre prospects, this is the turning point.
- **"They time your bathroom breaks?"** React honestly to a crazy detail ("that's crazy"). It builds rapport faster than technique.
- **Short break, short money section, link by text.** Fit the booking around how she is right now.
