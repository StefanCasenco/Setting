# 066 · SMMA · He calls back a missed call, rude · "Who the f*** is this?"

**Prospect:** Dre, 30, barber, owns a two-chair shop, Baltimore · **Lead:** filled out a form about growing his shop's Instagram and an agency side business. Setter missed him, he calls back angry. · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hello, this is Stefan.

**[D]:** Yo, who the f*** is this? Y'all called me three times.

**[S]:** *(calm)* Hey, my bad, man. It's Stefan from Sam's team. You filled out a form yesterday about growing your barbershop's Instagram. I tried you once this morning, and I don't know who the other two were. If now's bad, I'll leave you alone.

**[D]:** *(pause)* …Once?

**[S]:** Just once, this morning, about 10.

**[D]:** *(sighs)* Man, my phone's been blowing up all day. Some car warranty, some "you won a cruise." I thought you was one of them.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* No cruise, I promise. You filled out the form on Sam's page about the shop.

**[D]:** Yeah, yeah. Okay. My bad for cussing. I got a client in the chair, but I got a minute.

**[S]:** No worries at all. If it's easier, I can call you after the client?

**[D]:** Nah, he good. He's getting a lineup, he ain't going nowhere. *(client laughs)*

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* So what made you fill out the form?

**[D]:** I got my own shop. Two chairs. And I'm good, you feel me? I'm nice with the clippers. But I'm sitting there on Tuesdays with no clients, and the shop down the street is full, and he's worse than me. *(laughs)* For real. He just got a better Instagram.

**[S]:** He's worse than you but his Instagram's better.

**[D]:** Bro, I'm telling you. His fades are trash. *(client: "facts")* See, even my client know.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** *(laughs)* What's a slow Tuesday cost you?

**[D]:** I'm paying rent on the shop whether I cut or not. Twenty-two hundred a month. So a dead Tuesday, I'm losing money just being open.

**[S]:** And how's that been sitting with you?

**[D]:** It stresses me out, man. I opened this shop two years ago. My pops lent me the money. And if it don't work, that's his money too.

**[S]:** His money too.

**[D]:** Yeah. *(quieter)* He's retired. He put his savings in this. I can't let that go.

**[S]:** Yeah. That's a lot riding on it.

**[S]:** And the form mentioned doing social for other businesses too?

**[D]:** Yeah. Like, if I figure out Instagram for my shop, I know like fifteen other barbers who need the same thing. I could do it for them. Make money while I'm cutting.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If it worked, where would you want to be in a year?

**[D]:** My shop full every day. And like five barbers paying me to do theirs. Like five hundred each.

**[S]:** And what would that mean for you?

**[D]:** I pay my pops back. That's number one. That's it, really.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** What do you think's stopped your Instagram from working so far?

**[D]:** I post a pic of a cut and that's it. I don't know about reels, hashtags, all that. And I don't have time. I'm cutting.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[D]:** Somebody to show me what actually works for barbers. Simple. And then how to sell it to other shops.

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[D]:** Ten. My pops.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Rachel can build a realistic plan. What are you taking home after shop rent?

**[D]:** Like four K. Good months five.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside?

**[D]:** Like five hundred. And I'm paying my pops two hundred a month.

**[S]:** And saved?

**[D]:** Like six K.

**[S]:** And if Rachel finds the right fit, is investing in yourself to get this working something you'd be in a position to do?

**[D]:** If it fills my Tuesdays, yeah. It pays for itself.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you're better than the shop down the street, but his Instagram's better and he's full while you're dead on Tuesdays. You pay twenty-two hundred rent whether you cut or not, and your pops put his retirement savings into the shop. You want the shop full and five barbers paying you, so you can pay him back. What you said you need is someone to show you what actually works for barbers, simple, and how to sell it. Next step would be a Zoom with Rachel, one of Sam's head coaches. She'll look at your page and map it out. When's the shop closed?

**[D]:** Mondays.

**[S]:** Monday at 11 or 2?

**[D]:** 11.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[D]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Accept it after your client. And Dre, I'll only call once to remind you, I promise. *(laughs)*

**[D]:** *(laughs)* Appreciate you, bro. My bad for earlier.

**[S]:** All good. Clean up that lineup.

## What to notice

- **"Who the f*** is this?"** Stay calm, say who you are and the context, admit what you did ("I tried you once"), and offer to leave him alone.
- **Correct the facts without arguing.** "Just once, this morning." He realised the other calls weren't you, and calmed down.
- **"My bad for cussing."** Let it go: "No worries at all." Never make them feel bad for being angry.
- **His father's savings was the North Star.** "His money too." Repeat it and stop.
- **The client in the chair became part of the call** ("even my client know"). Go with the room.
