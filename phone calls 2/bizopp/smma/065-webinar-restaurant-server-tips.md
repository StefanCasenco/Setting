# 065 · SMMA · Webinar attendee · Restaurant server who already runs the restaurant's Instagram

**Prospect:** Maria, 27, server at an Italian restaurant, Boston · **Lead:** attended the webinar on her night off · **Money:** program · **Ends:** booked

---

*— OPEN —*

**[S]:** Hey Maria, how are you? It's Stefan from Sam's team. You were on Sam's webinar Monday. What stood out to you?

**[M]:** Hi! The part about local businesses. Because I already kind of do that. I run my restaurant's Instagram.

**[S]:** Wait, you run it?

**[M]:** Yeah. *(laughs)* The owner's like seventy, he doesn't know what Instagram is. I started posting the specials a year ago. Now we've got like eleven thousand followers, and the weekend reservations are full every week.

*— SOFT QUAL —*

**[S]:** Eleven thousand followers and full weekends. Does he pay you for it?

**[M]:** *(laughs)* He gave me a free tiramisu once.

**[S]:** *(laughs)* A tiramisu. So you're serving full-time?

**[M]:** Five nights a week.

*— CURRENT —*

**[S]:** How's serving?

**[M]:** It's good money some nights. Like three hundred in tips on a Saturday. But Tuesday, maybe forty. And my feet. I'm 27 and my feet hurt like I'm 70. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** Forty on a Tuesday. How's it been with the up and down?

**[M]:** Stressful. I never know what I'm going to make. Last January was so slow I had to borrow money from my mom for rent.

**[S]:** How was that, asking your mom?

**[M]:** Embarrassing. She's a nurse, she works so hard. And I'm 27 asking for rent money. *(quiet)* I don't want to do that again.

**[S]:** How long have you been feeling that way about serving?

**[M]:** The last two years. Since my friends started getting real jobs.

*— GOAL —*

**[S]:** If the agency worked, where would you want it in a year?

**[M]:** Like four or five restaurants. Five hundred to a thousand each. Then I could stop serving, or just do weekends.

**[S]:** What would that mean for you?

**[M]:** Knowing what I'm making every month. And honestly? Getting credit. I filled that restaurant, and nobody knows it was me.

*— ROADBLOCK —*

**[S]:** You've already got proof, eleven thousand followers and full weekends. What do you think's stopped you from getting paid for it?

**[M]:** I don't know how to ask. Like, how do you walk into a restaurant and say "pay me to do your Instagram"? It feels so awkward. And I don't know what to charge.

**[S]:** What do you think it'd take?

**[M]:** Someone to show me how to pitch it. And what to charge. And a contract, so I'm not doing it for tiramisu. *(laughs)*

*— URGENCY —*

**[S]:** On a scale of 1 to 10?

**[M]:** Eight.

**[S]:** What makes it an eight?

**[M]:** January's coming. And I'm not asking my mom again.

*— MONEY —*

**[S]:** Couple money questions, so Rachel can build a realistic plan. Averaged out, what do you make a month serving?

**[M]:** Like thirty-four hundred. Summer more, winter less.

**[S]:** And how much are you able to put aside?

**[M]:** Like three hundred. Boston rent. *(laughs)*

**[S]:** And in total?

**[M]:** About forty-five hundred. That's my January money.

**[S]:** And if Rachel finds the right fit, is investing in yourself something you'd be in a position to do, or is it genuinely too tight before January?

**[M]:** It's tight. But if it means I don't borrow from my mom again, I'd want to hear it.

*— BOOK —*

**[S]:** So from what you said, you took a restaurant's Instagram to eleven thousand followers and full weekends, and got paid in tiramisu. You're serving five nights, three hundred on a Saturday and forty on a Tuesday, and last January you had to borrow rent from your mom and it was embarrassing. You want four or five restaurants at five hundred to a thousand each so you know what you're making and get credit for it. What you said you need is someone to show you how to pitch, what to charge, and a contract. Next step would be a Zoom with Rachel, one of Sam's head coaches. She'll use your restaurant as the case study and be honest about the money before January. When's your night off?

**[M]:** Mondays and Tuesdays.

**[S]:** Monday at 1 or Tuesday at 1?

**[M]:** Tuesday.

**[S]:** Best email?

**[M]:** *(gives it)*

*— LOCK —*

**[S]:** Sent. Bring screenshots of the restaurant's growth. And Maria, ask for more than tiramisu. *(laughs)*

**[M]:** *(laughs)* I will!

---

## What to notice

- **"Wait, you run it?"** A surprised, curious reaction to the best fact on the call. It's her case study.
- **"He gave me a free tiramisu once."** Laugh, and keep the line. It's the gap between her value and her pay.
- **"Forty on a Tuesday."** The range from three hundred to forty is the impact. Then the real cost: borrowing from her mum.
- **"Getting credit."** The goal under the goal. "What would that mean for you?" found it.
- **Seasonal money, said honestly:** "Is it too tight before January?" She answered with her real why: not asking her mum again.
