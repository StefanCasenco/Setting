## What to notice

- **"I'm good at Instagram."** "A lot of people are good at Instagram. What's going on with you specifically?" That's Scott's "not everyone" move, done kindly.
- **Pull it to money: wage → rent → "how far does that go?"** In expensive cities, the rent number does the work.
- **"The wrong side of the desk."** Her image is the North Star. Repeat it and use it in the recap.
- **"Give me a number."** "Good money" means nothing until it's five thousand, and then "what would that change?"
- **Her limiting belief ("my own Instagram is different from a business") is actually smart.** Say so. The closer can test it.
