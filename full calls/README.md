# FULL CALLS

The whole setting call, from "Heyy… [name]?" to "take care", in five niches.
`current situation/` zooms in on one part of the call. This folder shows how the whole thing **flows**: how one section hands over to the next, and how to add context so nothing sounds like a form.

## How the calls are built

- **Same framework, every time:** intro → intent → current → timeframe → what got in the way → why now → financial qualification → pitch → book → commit. The section is marked in each call so you can see the handover.
- **Not word tracks.** Each prospect gives the setter something different, and the setter uses it. The lines are examples of a principle. Say them your way.
- **Setter, not closer.** One pain, one feeling, one cost, then move on. No accountability, no objection handling past a line or two, no promises. Atefur was playing a closer in a lot of your roleplays, so some of what he did is deeper than a setter needs to go.
- **Simple English on purpose.** Short sentences, words that are easy to say fast. If a line feels hard in your mouth, change it.
- **Invented people.** None of them are from your real calls.

## How to use them

1. Read one call out loud, setter side only. Twice.
2. Cover the setter lines. At each one, say what you'd ask before you look.
3. Read "What made it flow". Find the context tool in each line (their words, the reason, normalise, options, real life).
4. Do a roleplay with Kevin or Atefur in that niche, and try to use three "Lines to steal" from that file.

## Per niche: the one line that matters most

| Niche | The line | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Trading** | "Have you actually put money on a trade, or has it been more watching and paper trading so far?" | Most have lost money. Options let them say it without shame. |
| **Ecom** | "Three sales is three more than most people get." | Most have a dead store. Normalise it and they'll tell you why. |
| **AI agency** | "So you basically [reframe what they built]." | They undervalue what they can do. Saying it back opens them up. |
| **Credit repair** | "No judgement at all, most people I speak to have a story behind the number." | It's the most shameful niche. This line turns shame into a story. |
| **Tech sales** | "You've already tried the free way, [40] applications…" | The answer to "why would I pay to get a job?". |
