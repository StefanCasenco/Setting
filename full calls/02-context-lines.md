# 02 · Context lines: how to ask without it feeling like a form

Atefur, call #4:
> "It's not just about being conversational. It's how can I get the answer I want without this guy thinking I'm fishing for something shady. Why is he trying to ask me how much I'm making?"

That's the whole point of the "fluff". It's not padding. **Every lead-in does one of five jobs:**

| Tool | What it does | Example lead-in |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Their words** | Shows you listened, makes the question feel like it came from them | "You said the shifts are killing you…" |
| **2. The reason** | Tells them *why* you're asking, so they don't guess something shady | "Just so I'm not booking you into something that doesn't fit…" |
| **3. Normal-ise** | Makes their answer feel normal, not embarrassing | "Most people I speak to…" / "No judgement…" |
| **4. Options** | Gives them easy answers to pick from | "…is it more X, or more Y?" |
| **5. Real life** | A small true thing about the world or you | "With how expensive everything is now…" |

**One tool per question is enough.** Two at most. If the lead-in is longer than the question, cut it.

**Not context:** "like… I guess… like… so…" before every question. That's hedging. It makes you sound unsure. Say the lead-in, pause, then ask the question clean.

## Section by section

Each block: the **bare** version (what to avoid) → **with context** (2–3 ways, pick one).

### INTENT
Bare: *"What had you interested in trading?"*
- "So tell me, man, out of all the ways to make money online, what had you interested in trading?" *(tool 4: makes them choose it)*
- "Everyone gets into this for a different reason, so I always like to ask, what had you interested in [X] in the first place?" *(tool 3)*
- *(webinar)* "What stood out to you from the webinar?" *(tool 1)*

### WHEN THE INTENT IS "MONEY"
Bare: *"Why do you want more money?"*
- "Sure, of course, who doesn't. But money's just a tool, right? Nobody wants to stare at numbers on a screen. What's the real reason behind it for you?"
- "Is it more about getting out of something, or getting to something?" *(tool 4, short. Use after the line above if they're stuck.)*

### CURRENT: WORK
Bare: *"What do you do for work?"*
- "And [X] is usually a side thing for most people at the start, so what's paying the bills right now?"
- "What keeps you busy at the moment, work-wise?" *(Flavius line, softer)*
- *(if they hinted)* "You mentioned the shifts, what is it you do?" *(tool 1)*

### CURRENT: THE JOB FEELING
Bare: *"How do you feel about the job?"* *(Atefur #4: "does that question push the sale forward?")*
- "Twelve-hour nights, damn, that's no joke. Is that something you don't mind, or more of a means to an end right now?" *(tools 1 + 4)*
- "Nine years in real estate is a long time. Do you still enjoy it, or is it more just what you know at this point?"
- "And how's that been on you, honestly?" *(only after they already said something negative)*

### CURRENT: DIGGING (the softener)
Bare: *"Why aren't you fulfilled?"* → gets a defensive answer.
- "What makes you feel like you're not as [their word] as you want to be right now?" *(Atefur #2, "as you want to be" is the whole trick)*
- "When you say [their word], what does that actually look like day to day?"
- "You said [their word]. What do you mean by that?" *(Atefur: "what do you mean" beats "give me an example")*

### CURRENT: HOURS
Bare: *"How many hours do you work?"*
- "And with [their job] I know it's never really 9 to 5, so what does a normal week look like hours-wise?"
- "Is that full-time, or more like a few days a week?" *(tool 4)*

### CURRENT: IMPACT
Bare: *"How does that affect you?"*
- "And with you not being able to spend as much time with them as you'd like, how does that sit with you?" *(you did this in Kevin #2. It was good.)*
- "What kind of effect does that have outside work, like at home?"

### RECAP → TIMEFRAME (the transition)
- "Okay, so basically your main goal is to [their real goal, their words], correct?" *(framework)*
- "Got it. Give me the bigger picture then…"

### TIMEFRAME
Bare: *"How long have you been looking into trading?"*
- "And [X] is one of those things people sit on for ages before they actually do anything. How long's it been on your radar?" *(tool 3)*
- "Give me the bigger picture, how long have you been looking into this?"

### WHAT HAVE YOU TRIED
Bare: *"What have you tried?"*
- "And in that time, have you actually [put money on a trade / launched a store / sent outreach], or has it been more watching and research so far?" *(tool 4)*
- "Besides YouTube, have you tried anything yourself?"

### WHAT GOT IN THE WAY
Bare: *"What stopped you?"*
- "Six months is a while to be looking at something, and no judgement, most people get stuck in the same spot. What would you say kept you from actually taking the first real step?" *(tools 1 + 3)*
- "If you had to point at one thing that held you back, what would it be?"

### WHY NOW
Bare: *"Why now?"*
- "You've had this on your mind about [time], and life gets busy, I get it. So what's different now that you actually [booked / filled it in / picked up]?" *(tool 1)*
- "What made you reach out this month, specifically?"

### FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION: THE INTRO
*Set it up so the money questions don't come out of nowhere.*
- "Okay, let me ask you a couple of questions about the money side, just so I know where you're at. Cool?"
- "And I'm sure you know, in [X] it takes money to make money, right?"

### GOAL NUMBER
Bare: *"How much do you want to make?"*
- "Everyone's number's different. Some people just want a few hundred extra for bills, some want to replace their job. What would make it worth your time, say a year from now?" *(tools 3 + 4)*

### THE GAP
Bare: *"How much do you make?"*
- "So your goal's [number], I just want to understand the gap between where you're at and that. How far would you say you are right now, with your current monthly income?" *(Atefur #4, word for word)*
- If they ask "what do you mean?": "Like, what are you bringing in a month right now, roughly?"

### MONTHLY SAVINGS
Bare: *"How much do you save?"*
- "And you know how expenses are nowadays, the way the economy is going… how much would you say you're actually able to save from that each month?"
- Then: "And is that like on a good month, a tight month, or your usual?"

### TOTAL SAVINGS
Bare: *"How much have you saved?"*
- "And outside the monthly stuff, in total, how much have you been able to put away so far?"
- "Is that roughly it, or is there a bit more sitting in savings or investments?" *(tool 4)*

### WHERE THE MONEY SITS
Bare: *"Is that cash under the mattress?"*
- "Some people have it sitting in the bank ready to go, some have it in stocks or a pension they don't want to touch. Where's yours mostly?"

### CREDIT
Bare: *"Where are you at with credit cards?"*
- "Some people like to spread something like this out rather than put it all down at once. Is credit something you've got available, or would you rather keep it to savings?"

### "WHY ARE YOU ASKING?" (the money pushback)
- "Fair question. Honestly, it's because I don't want to book you into something that has you eating noodles for two weeks. *(Kevin's)* I'd rather know now."
- "Yeah man, I'm pretty sure you know in [X] it takes money to make money, right? Like if you go to a personal trainer and say you want to gain muscle, first thing he asks is what you're working with. Same thing." *(framework + Atefur's PT line)*
- "Can we help you if we don't know your situation? That's all it is."

### WILLING TO INVEST
Bare: *"Would you invest?"*
- "And if we found a way that actually fits you, would you be open to investing in yourself, time, energy and resources, to get there?"

## PITCH → BOOK → COMMIT

### Pitch (sell the meeting, not the product)
- "Alright [name], from what you've told me, I think this could actually be a fit. The next step would be a call with [closer], one of our [coaches / advisors]. He'll go more in depth, look at your situation, and help you build a plan on how to get to [their goal, their words]. Sound good?"

**Use their words for the goal.** "Get home before the kids are asleep" lands harder than "your goal".

### If they hesitate ("what's that call about?")
- "Honestly, he'll look at everything you told me, the [job / savings / what you tried], and map out what it would actually take for you. If it's not a fit, he'll tell you straight."

### Booking (two options, never open)
- "Let me check what slots he's got. What works better, tomorrow or [day]?"
- "Morning, afternoon or evening?"
- "I've got [time 1] or [time 2], which one?"

### Details
- "What's your best email for the call details?"
- "And is this number good for a reminder text?"
- "I've just sent the invite. Can you check your email real quick and accept it, so it's in your calendar?"

### Commit
- "Okay, you're locked in. Just to double check, are you 100% sure [time] works, and nothing's going to come up that could clash?"
- "If anything comes up, life happens, just message me and we'll move it. Cool?"
- "For the call, find a quiet spot, be at a computer if you can, and [closer] will break it all down."
- *Optional (if they have a partner who decides):* "Is there anyone else you'd normally want to speak to before deciding on something like this? Might be worth having them on the call."
- "Amazing brother, take care."

## Setter vs closer: what's not your job

Atefur was roleplaying **as a closer** in a lot of those calls. Some of what he did is too much for a setting call.

| Setter does | Leave for the closer |
|---|---|
| Find one real pain, with one feeling and one cost | Dig five layers deep into it |
| Ask the money questions to qualify | Hold them accountable ("so you're okay with that?") |
| Say "that's what the next call is for" | Handle objections for 10 minutes |
| Sell the *meeting* | Sell the program, price, guarantee |
| 10–20 minutes total | 45–90 minutes |

**If you catch yourself reframing or handling objections for more than one or two lines, stop.** Either book them or let them go.
