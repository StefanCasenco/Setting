**[SETTER]:** 6 is perfect. I'll give you a ring on WhatsApp

### Why the DMs worked
- **Message 1 named the exact post.** Not "thanks for the follow".
- **Message 2 was the inbound question with options:** "building it yourself, or following the space?". Easy to answer.
- **No pitch, no call request until he'd said something he wants** ("doing it for clients on the side") **and something he doesn't like** ("they bill 5k, I get my salary").
- **"I type like a grandma"** is a light reason to move to a call. It's about you, so it doesn't feel like a sales move.

## What made it flow

- **The call started from his DM, in his words.** "You were saying… tell me a bit more about that." No intent question needed. He'd already given it.
- **"So you basically created a product for them"** is a reframe of his own story. He'd never seen it that way, so he opened up.
- **"Is it something you enjoy, or more a means to an end right now?"** The job-feeling question, with two options.
- **"Have you reached out to businesses, or has it been more planning?"** Options again. He told the dentist story by himself.
- **"The proposal, that's where a lot of people freeze"** normalised it. No shame, so he kept talking.
- **The gap was worked out loud with him.** "27 a year, so about 1,900 a month?" is softer than "how much do you make?", because he'd already said it in the DMs.
- **"I'm guessing rent, maybe living with mates?"** is a guess, not a question. People correct a guess more easily than they answer a direct question.
- **"How much is it?" mid-call** got an honest "depends, that's what the next call is for", plus the reason for the money questions. No price.

## Setter, not closer

- **The dad line was the pain.** "Same company 25 years, never got anywhere." The setter said "yeah" and let it breathe. No digging into his relationship with his dad.
- **No objection handling on price.** The setter didn't say "think of it as an investment, the ROI on one client…". That's closing.
- **The credit card was asked, not pushed.** "Would you rather keep it to savings?" leaves him an easy out.
