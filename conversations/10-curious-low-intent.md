## Worse version

Bad line: "Come on, you clicked the ad for a reason. What's holding you back? Let's get you on a call with our advisor this week."
He answers: "Nah, you're alright. Gotta go."
Why it fails: it chases a lead that already said "not ready" and turns a friendly chat into pressure. The fix is to say "no stress", offer a short note, and let him go. Nobody is made to feel bad for not being ready, which is how this lead might come back one day.
