- The variant frame did its job in one line. She never had to defend herself for not remembering, and the setter never had to describe the form.
- One probe, then out. When the answer was "nothing, it was a joke", the call ended in the next few turns. It stays friendly so a friend who wants to look at trading later knows the call was painless.
- Tone stayed warm with a stranger who had every right to be confused. A lead who had a good ten seconds with the company is a lead who may come back on her own terms.
