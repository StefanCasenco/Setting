# 02 · "Freedom, I guess. Not being stuck."

**First answer type:** Vague freedom · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** booked
**Lead:** Amira, 31, call-centre team lead, Dubai, UAE · **Surface story:** wants "freedom", not to feel stuck · **Hidden truth:** she is on rotating shifts, cares for her mother who is unwell, and has no time left for anything else · **Savings truth:** about AED 20,000 she can use, plus a separate bit she keeps for her mother's care and won't touch
**Resistance style:** vague, hard to explain, laughs it off, answers with "I don't know" first · **Depth target:** L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~11 min
**Setter focus:** when "freedom" has no content, guess out loud and offer two options, then follow the one word she picks. Includes a recovery.

## Worse version

Bad line: "So you feel stuck. Why aren't you free already?"
She answers: "I don't know, I guess I'm just not doing enough."
Why it fails: it turns her word into an accusation, and she answers with self-blame. The fix is the echo plus a guess: "Stuck. What do you mean by that?" then "Is it more the work itself, or the schedule?"
