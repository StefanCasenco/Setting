## What to notice
- "I've got about five minutes" is the whole answer to being rushed. No apology, no skipping nodes. CURRENT is still the longest part, just shorter.
- With little time, pick one scene question ("walk me through a normal day") and one feeling question ("what's the hardest part of that?"). That is enough for L3-L4.
- The setter checks time once, after the heavy moment, and keeps the promise. She did not ask to leave, so the call runs on.
