# 24 · "I'm in Year 11, is that a problem?"

**First answer type:** Underage (DQ) · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** DQ
**Lead:** Ethan, 17, school student, Sydney, Australia · **Surface story:** "saw it on YouTube, looks cool" · **Hidden truth:** wants his own money for a car and to stop asking his dad · **Savings truth:** a few hundred dollars from a weekend job, never asked
**Resistance style:** upbeat, short answers, a bit nervous about being found out · **Depth target:** none; ends at the age check · **Approx. duration:** ~4 min
**Setter focus:** find out the age naturally, end the application kindly, don't pitch, don't lecture, give him one safe next step.

He answers: "My dad has a bit put away, I could ask."
