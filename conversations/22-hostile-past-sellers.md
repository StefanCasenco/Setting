**First answer type:** Hostile, venting about past sellers · **Offer:** ecom · **Outcome:** no-book

**Setter focus:** stay calm, make one fair acknowledgement, ask honest questions, never defend or promise. When he says no more calls, let him go with respect and no pitch.

## Worse version

Bad line: "I totally understand, Walt, but we're not like those other companies. We get real results for our clients."
He answers: "Sure you do. Bye."

Why it fails: it defends, claims a difference the setter can't prove, and promises results. A burned man hears the same pitch he heard before. The fix is the one used above: admit what you can't know, acknowledge once, ask what happened, and let the "no" stand.
