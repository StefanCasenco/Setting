# 23 · "I'm not okay, to be honest"

**First answer type:** Loaded drop early (emotionally heavy) · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** no-book
**Lead:** Ravi, 40, bus driver, Leicester, UK · **Surface story:** "a bit of extra money for the bills" · **Hidden truth:** his wife died four months ago and he is not okay; the call was booked before, from an ad, when things were different · **Savings truth:** never asked, and it does not matter on this call
**Resistance style:** quiet, polite, apologises for himself, offers to "carry on" because sitting in silence is harder · **Depth target:** none; the setter stops the framework · **Approx. duration:** ~5 min
**Setter focus:** when someone says something heavy, stop the script. One human sentence, silence, no pitch, no probing, no booking. Includes a recovery.

## The call

> [FRAME]

**S:** Heeey… Ravi?

**P:** Yeah, speaking.

**S:** This is Ștefan from $COMPANY, it looks like you've booked from an ad, about possible help with trading, is that right?

**P:** Yeah. I think I did that a while back.

**S:** Cool, just to give you some insight into our application process, it's two steps. The first step is this call. My goal here is to get an understanding of where you're at, some of your goals and see if we can actually help you. If by the end of the call I feel like that's the case, I'll walk you through the next stage, cool?

**P:** Okay. Go on.

> [INTENT]

**S:** Okay, so tell me man, like… out of all the ways to make money, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**P:** Bit of extra. For the bills, you know.

> [CURRENT L1] Vague money. The usual move.

**S:** Sure man, most people want more money. What's the real reason behind it for you?

**P:** Just me paying everything now.

> [CURRENT L2] "Now" is a word to follow.

**S:** Just you now. What do you mean?

**P:** My wife died. Four months ago. I'm not okay, to be honest.

> [WHY THIS LINE] This is the moment the framework stops. No echo, no "what's the hardest part". One human sentence, then quiet.

**S:** I'm so sorry, Ravi.

*(The setter says nothing. About ten seconds.)*

**P:** Sorry. Didn't mean to drop that on you.

**S:** You don't need to be sorry.

**P:** Can we carry on? Easier than sitting here with it.

> [RECOVERY] The setter starts the next scripted question out of habit, hears it, and drops it mid-sentence. He says so in plain words and does not go back to the script.

**S:** Okay, walk me through a normal wee... no. Sorry. That's the script talking. Forget it.

**P:** It's alright. I can answer.

**S:** I know. But I'd rather not do this call today.

**P:** Right. Yeah.

> [WHY THIS LINE] No pitch, no recap of his pain, no "when you're ready you could book". Nothing to sell him today.

**S:** Is it alright if I check in with you later, when you're ready?

**P:** Yeah. Maybe in a few weeks. I don't know.

**S:** That's fine. No rush, and no pressure either way.

**P:** Thanks.

**S:** One more thing, and I'm not trying to tell you what to do. It's okay to talk to someone close to you about this.

**P:** My brother keeps ringing. I keep not picking up.

**S:** Maybe he's a good one to pick up for.

**P:** Maybe. Yeah. Maybe.

**S:** Take care of yourself, Ravi.

**P:** You too, mate. Thanks for being decent about it.

## Handoff note

Ravi · trading · ad lead. Bereavement, about four months ago. Not qualified, not asked, not pitched. No booking. Follow up once in a few weeks, gently, only if he is open to it. Do not mention the call content in the follow-up beyond "how are you doing".

## Depth scorecard
Layers reached L2, and deliberately stopped there · exit test met: not applicable, the framework was stopped · echoes used: 1 ("just you now") · questions that assumed something: 0 · minutes in CURRENT: ~1

## What to notice
- The setter does not echo the loss. "Just you now" opened the door, and after that he asks nothing more about it. The one sentence is "I'm so sorry, Ravi", and then he waits.
- Ravi offers to carry on. The setter kindly says no. A script that carries on because the prospect is polite would be using his grief to finish a call.
- The setter does not diagnose or counsel. He says it is okay to talk to someone close, and leaves it there. When Ravi mentions his brother, he does not push further.

## Worse version

Bad line: "I'm sorry for your loss. That's exactly why this could be a good move, so what would more money change for you?"
He answers: "I don't know. I can't think about it."
Why it fails: it turns his loss into a reason to buy. The fix is the one used above: one human sentence, silence, no pitch, no booking, and an offer to check in later.
