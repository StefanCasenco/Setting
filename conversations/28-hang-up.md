## What to notice
- The setter used the five-minute line, not a long frame. A driver cannot give attention, and the call has to match the moment.
- The call-back offer was made once, plainly, and he said no. The setter did not make it twice, and there was no case made for the call. When he hung up, the setter said one safe line and stopped.
- Nothing in the call was salvaged by pressure. The pain (four nights away from home) came out on its own, a few words late, and it is written down for the future instead of used now.
- The log is a fact sheet, not a mood. It tells the next person what happened and that nobody is chasing, and it keeps the one detail he gave.
