**First answer type:** Skeptic · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** booked

## Worse version

Bad line: "It's not a scam, we've helped hundreds of people, and you've got nothing to lose by trying."
He answers: "That's what they all say."

Why it fails: it argues, claims results the setter can't back, and promises no risk. That is exactly the pitch that burned him before. The fix is the script line plus "What made you ask?", which turns the doubt into his story.
