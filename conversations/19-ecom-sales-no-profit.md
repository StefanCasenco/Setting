# 19 · "The store sells, I just need to scale" (ecom)

**First answer type:** Sales but no profit · **Offer:** ecom · **Outcome:** booked
**Lead:** Pedro, 30, junior accountant, Madrid, Spain (€) · **Surface story:** has a store that sells about €6,000 a month, thinks it is doing well and wants to scale · **Hidden truth:** the ads, fees and refunds eat most of the margin and he has never worked it out; he works on the store until midnight and his partner Lucía moved out two months ago, saying she felt she lived with a laptop · **Savings truth:** about €5,500, in his own account
**Resistance style:** confident, jargon as armor, answers a different question, asks "why are you asking?" once · **Depth target:** L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~12 min
**Setter focus:** he is wrong about his margins. The setter doesn't argue or correct him, asks what is left at the end of the month, and lets him hear it himself.

## What to notice
- The setter didn't tell an accountant his numbers might be off. One plain question ("what's left at the end of the month?") and an echo of "haven't done it properly" was enough, and the closer gets the flag.
- "Temporary" was the real subject. Echoing it led to Lucía without asking him about his feelings.
- The credit card: not encouraged, not lectured, flagged in the handoff.
