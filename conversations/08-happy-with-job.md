# 08 · "My job is fine, I just want more"

**First answer type:** Happy with job · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** booked
**Lead:** Priya, 38, hospital pharmacist, Toronto, Canada · **Surface story:** likes her job, well paid, "I just want more" · **Hidden truth:** she is turning 40 soon and is afraid she will get there without having built anything of her own · **Savings truth:** about $12,000 in a separate savings account, retirement untouched
**Resistance style:** calm, dry humour, polite deflection; never complains about work · **Depth target:** L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~12 min
**Setter focus:** never dig at the job. Ask the motivation question, then follow her phrase, "none of it is mine".
