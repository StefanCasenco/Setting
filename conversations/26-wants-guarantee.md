# 26 · "Guaranteed results or my money back"

**First answer type:** Wants a guarantee · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** DQ
**Lead:** Oscar, 37, car salesman, Auckland, New Zealand (NZ$) · **Surface story:** wants steady side income from trading, but only if the result is covered · **Hidden truth:** a bad commission month last quarter, and he has been burned by a "guaranteed" investment before · **Savings truth:** around NZ$8,000, never reached
**Resistance style:** salesman's confidence, tests the setter like a customer, says "I need guaranteed results or my money back" and will not move · **Depth target:** L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~9 min
**Setter focus:** run real CURRENT first, then answer the guarantee question honestly. No hedging, no fudging, and no pushing when he says no.

## The call

> [FRAME]

**S:** Heeey… Oscar?

**P:** Yep, Oscar here.

**S:** This is Ștefan from $COMPANY, it looks like you've booked from an ad, about possible help with trading, is that right?

**P:** Yeah, that was me.

**S:** Cool, just to give you some insight into our application process, it's two steps. The first step is this call. My goal here is to get an understanding of where you're at, some of your goals and see if we can actually help you. If by the end of the call I feel like that's the case, I'll walk you through the next stage, cool?

**P:** Sure. I do this all day, so go ahead.

> [INTENT]

**S:** Okay, so tell me man, like… out of all the ways to make money, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**P:** Extra income. Something that isn't tied to selling cars.

> [CURRENT L1] "Isn't tied to selling cars." He gave a direction, not a reason yet.

**S:** Tied to selling cars. What do you mean?

**P:** My pay is commission. Good month, great. Bad month, not great.

> [CURRENT L2]

**S:** What was the last bad month like?

**P:** March. Four cars all quarter, basically. Interest rates, nobody buying.

> [CURRENT L3]

**S:** Walk me through what that does to a normal week.

**P:** I'm on the lot, six days. Smiling, doing the pitch. Checking my account at lunch.

**S:** Checking it for what?

**P:** Whether the mortgage came out okay. *(short laugh)* It did. Just.

> [WHY THIS LINE] The laugh and "just" are the door. Echo and wait.

**S:** Just.

**P:** Yeah. We've got two kids. My wife works part-time. I don't like her seeing me stressed about it.

> [CURRENT L4] Pain in his words: "checking my account at lunch", "I don't like her seeing me stressed". One feeling (tense, hiding it), one cost (the mortgage rides on commission).

**S:** Does she see it anyway?

**P:** Probably. She never says.

**P:** Why are you asking about my wife? I thought this was about trading.

**S:** I'm not asking just for the sake of asking. The better I understand you, the better I can help, like a personal trainer would.

**P:** Fine. Fair enough.

> [RECAP]

**S:** Okay so basically your main goal is to have income that doesn't depend on commission, so the mortgage isn't a lunchtime worry, correct?

**P:** Yeah. That's it exactly.

> [TIMEFRAME] [TEST B]

**S:** Got it. Give me the bigger picture, like how long have you been looking into trading?

**P:** About eight months. Reading, mostly.

**S:** Okay and within the last eight months… what have you tried in trading?

**P:** Paper trading. A couple of courses I didn't finish.

**S:** What do you feel has been missing to get you there?

**P:** Honestly? A safety net. I don't want to lose money I can't afford to lose.

> [WHY THIS LINE] This is the first sign of where the call is going. Do not react yet. Let him finish.

> [WGW]

**S:** What happened with the courses?

**P:** They promised a lot. I read the fine print. Nothing was actually backed up.

> [WHY NOW]

**S:** What made you start looking now?

**P:** March. Seeing that quarter. I decided I'm not doing another one like it.

> [FIN] The guarantee question comes before any money question. The setter answers it plainly and does not go on to qualify.

**P:** So before we go on. Is it guaranteed? I need guaranteed results or my money back.

**S:** I can't promise you any results, and I won't say I can. Nobody can honestly promise that in trading.

**P:** Then I can't do this. I sell for a living. Anything without a guarantee is a pitch.

> [WHY THIS LINE] He is right that a guarantee would be a pitch. The setter does not soften the answer, does not point to testimonials, and does not suggest the closer will "sort it out".

**S:** That's fair. I'd rather tell you now than have you find out later.

**P:** So what do I get?

**S:** A call with $CLOSER, where you ask him the detail. But I can't tell you it ends in a guarantee.

**P:** No. I'm not proceeding without one. That's my line.

> [RECOVERY] The setter nearly said "let's at least get you on the call." He stopped, because that would book someone who has already said no.

**S:** Understood, Oscar. I won't book you for something you've told me you don't want.

**P:** Appreciate the honesty, at least.

**S:** If your view changes, you know where to find me. Thanks for your time.

**P:** Cheers. Take care.

## Depth scorecard
Layers reached L4 · exit test met: yes ("commission month, checking my account at lunch, I don't like my wife seeing me stressed") · echoes used: 3 ("tied to selling cars", "just", "a safety net") · questions that assumed something: 1 ("Checking it for what?") · minutes in CURRENT: ~4

## What to notice
- The call did the full CURRENT work before the guarantee came up. The DQ is on the guarantee, not on rapport, and the setter still has the pain in his words for a later note.
- The answer was one flat sentence, "I can't promise you any results." No "but", no testimonial, no "most people see". Hedging here would be a promise in disguise.
- The setter did not book a person who said no twice. Booking him "to see what the closer says" would waste the closer's slot and Oscar's trust.

## Worse version

Bad line: "Most of our clients see results. Let's get you on the call and the advisor can talk about protection."
He answers: "So is it guaranteed or not?"

Why it fails: it hints at a guarantee without saying it, which is a promise the setter cannot back. The fix is the plain line used above: "I can't promise you any results, and I won't say I can."
