# 13 · "How much is it?"

**First answer type:** Price-first · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** DQ
**Lead:** Leo, 24, barista, Melbourne, Australia (A$) · **Surface story:** wants to know the price before anything else · **Hidden truth:** shares a rental with three housemates, rent keeps eating his pay, and he feels everyone his age is further ahead · **Savings truth:** about A$200, nothing set aside for trading
**Resistance style:** impatient, jokes, repeats the price question, goes quiet when asked about himself · **Depth target:** L3-L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~8 min
**Setter focus:** answer the cost question once with the taster, stay calm when it comes back, and return to the person. Never deny there is a cost and never say a number.

## The call

> [FRAME]

**S:** Heeey… Leo?

**P:** Yeah, that's me.

**S:** This is Ștefan from $COMPANY, it looks like you've booked from an ad, about possible help with trading, is that right?

> [WHY THIS LINE] Direct question, so the taster is allowed. It is Option 1 from the script, word for word. No number, no "free", no dodging that there is a cost.

**S:** It's a four-figure investment, depending on the program, but we can't even know if we can help you yet. That's what the call with $CLOSER is for.

**P:** Four figures. Right. Okay.

**P:** Yeah, fine. Go.

> [INTENT]

**S:** Okay, so tell me man, like… out of all the ways to make money, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

> [CURRENT L1] Social-media answer. Not a goal yet, so ask for the real reason behind it.

**S:** Easy compared to what?

**P:** Compared to what I do. I make coffee. Eight hours on my feet.

> [CURRENT L2]

**S:** What do you mean, compared to what you do?

**P:** I mean, it pays what it pays. Forty grand with tips, maybe. Thirty-eight.

**S:** And what's the part of the job you don't like?

**P:** Mate, honestly, what's the price range on the programs? Like the cheapest one.

> [RECOVERY] The price question came back, now asking for a range. The setter does not take the bait, repeats the honest core (it costs something, it depends), and returns to the thread Leo left.

**S:** I can't give you a number I can't stand behind, and it does cost something. That's why I'm asking about you first. You said eight hours on your feet?

**P:** Yeah. Six days sometimes. Early opens, then covering for people.

> [CURRENT L3]

**S:** Walk me through a normal week.

**P:** Up at four-thirty. Shift at six. Home at three, I'm cooked. Maybe gym. Then it's the house.

**S:** The house?

**P:** I live with three other guys. Share house in Footscray. Always someone's mess.

> [WHY THIS LINE] He said "the house" with a sigh. That is where the feeling is, not the job.

**S:** What's the hardest part of that?

**P:** Rent. I pay three-fifty a week for a room. That's like a third of my pay gone.

**S:** A third.

**P:** More, honestly. By Thursday I'm counting dollars. My mates are buying places and I'm eating toast.

> [CURRENT L4] Pain in his words: "counting dollars by Thursday", "eating toast", "mates buying places". One feeling (behind), one cost (a third of his pay in rent).

**P:** Why are you asking about all this? You said this was about where I'm at.

**S:** I'm not asking just for the sake of asking. The better I understand you, the better I can help, like a personal trainer would.

> [WHY THIS LINE] A genuine "why are you asking?" and the verbatim reason line. Exit test is met, so no more digging.

**P:** Ha. Okay. Fair enough.

> [RECAP]

**S:** Okay so basically your main goal is to move out of the share house and stop counting dollars by Thursday, correct?

**P:** Yeah. Pretty much. Have my own place one day.

> [TIMEFRAME] [TEST C]

**S:** Got it. Give me the bigger picture, like how long have you been looking into trading?

**P:** Few months. Mostly watching videos.

**S:** Okay and within the last few months… what have you tried in trading?

**P:** Downloaded an app. Put in fifty bucks, made about six. Then lost it.

**S:** What do you think would make the difference?

**P:** Someone showing me how to not lose it, I guess.

> [WGW]

**S:** What happened with that?

**P:** Nothing really. Bought on a tip, price dropped. I got scared and sold.

> [WHY NOW]

**S:** What made you start looking now?

**P:** Lease is up in three months. Landlord's putting the rent up again.

> [FIN]

**S:** What would you want to be making every month from trading, to make it worth your time?

**P:** Like a thousand a week? Is that dumb?

**S:** Not dumb. I just want to understand where you're at.

**S:** How much are you making right now? (just so I can understand how far away you are from that)

**P:** About eight hundred a week, after tax.

**S:** How much of that are you able to save?

**P:** Not much. Like thirty bucks, if I'm good.

**S:** I'm sure you know it takes money to make money. How much would you say you have saved up for your trading account?

**P:** Like a couple hundred.

> [WHY THIS LINE] DQ on savings, not on attitude. The setter says it plainly, keeps it kind, and sends something useful. No price, no hint of "what you'd need".

**S:** Got it man. Right now it wouldn't make sense to book you. I'll send you some resources and we pick this up in a month or two.

**P:** Yeah, fair enough. Honestly I figured.

**S:** Appreciate you being straight with me. Take care, Leo.

**P:** Cheers. You too.
