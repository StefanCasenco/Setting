# 05 · "Since my dad died, the money's been a mess"

**First answer type:** Loaded drop early · **Offer:** trading · **Outcome:** booked
**Lead:** Tomás, 38, delivery driver, Lisbon, Portugal · **Surface story:** wants extra income · **Hidden truth:** after his father died, the family's bills and his mother's rent landed on him, and he is working longer days to cover them · **Savings truth:** about €5,000, his own, not borrowed
**Resistance style:** drops the loss in passing, goes quiet, then goes back to practical talk · **Depth target:** L3-L4 · **Approx. duration:** ~11 min
**Setter focus:** one calm human sentence, then silence. Don't use the loss as ammo, and don't dig into it.

> [CURRENT L4] Exit test met. His words: money a mess, bills on him, long days, no time together. The cost is time and a queue. Stop digging.

> [RECAP]

## Worse version

Bad line: "I'm sorry about your dad. And if you don't fix this now, what happens to your mother's rent?"
He answers: "…I don't know. I have to go."
